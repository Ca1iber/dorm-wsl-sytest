# Support for third-party plug-ins

The SmarTest Work Center is based on the open-source Eclipse IDE, which lets you customize your work environment with a large number of third-party plug-ins (such as the Subclipse Apache Subversion client).

 CAUTION Do not upgrade any supported SmarTest plug-in or Eclipse plug-in. Failure to comply could result in an unstable or disabled SmarTest installation. When installing third-party plug-ins, you must install plug-in versions that are compatible with SmarTest based on the current Eclipse (3.7.2 Indigo) and CDT plug-in (8.0.2) versions.

Extending the functionality of the SmarTest Work Center in this way is supported by Advantest in this manner:

- Advantest continues to support the documented functionality of the SmarTest Eclipse Work Center when third-party Eclipse plug-ins are installed.
- Any defects and stability issues detected in SmarTest will be handled in the same manner, whether or not third-party plug-ins are installed.

Advantest may not have access to all such plug-ins; therefore, fixing an issues that involves a specific plug-in may require additional cooperation by you.

- The responsibility for fixing any issues within the third-party plug-ins themselves or otherwise providing support for them continues to rest with the respective third parties.

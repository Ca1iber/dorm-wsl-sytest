# Opening additional measurements

You can open the Measurement window for the associated signal from the Hardware view.

## Before you begin

You have opened a signal in the Hardware view.

## Procedure

-  In the Hardware view, click Measure.

The Measurement window for the associated signal is displayed.

![Opening the Measurement window from the Hardware view](<../../../../raw/353737.png>)

-  Repeat in another Hardware view to open another Measurement window for another signal.

## Results

You have opened Measurement windows for the signals you are interested in and can continue with debugging steps.

## Functional changes

- Introduced in SmarTest 7.4.3

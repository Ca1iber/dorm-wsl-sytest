# Removing a Device from your Workspace

 Note Do not remove the current device. Removing the current device may result in an undefined state of the SmarTest software.

To remove a device from your workspace:

- Highlight the device you want to remove.
- Click the right mouse button and select Delete. ![Removing a device](<../../../../raw/91652.png>)
- Choose, if you want to remove the device from your workspace or if you want to delete the content of the device directory. Note If you select Also delete contents under ... the device directory and its content will be deleted permanently. ![Removing a device (2)](<../../../../raw/91653.png>)
- Click OK.

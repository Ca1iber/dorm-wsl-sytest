The SmarTest Work Center is the main window which appears when SmarTest is started. All SmarTest tools are controlled from the SmarTest work center.

The SmarTest Work Center window contains several predefined perspectives. Perspectives contain views and editors and control what appears in certain menus and toolbars. The work center is also sometimes referred to as the work bench.

## Functional changes

- Introduced before SmarTest 6.3.2

# Importing a device into your workspace

With this task you import an existing device. If you want to create a new device, see [Creating a new device](<01.01.10.03.01_Creating_a_new_device.md>).

Importing a device means that you create a link to your device directory. It does not copy the contents of your device directory to your workspace.

To import a device:

- Highlight the navigator and click the right mouse button. Select Import... . ![Importing a device into your workspace (1)](<../../../../raw/91636.png>)
- In the Import window click Existing Device/TestMethod into Workspace. ![Importing a device into your workspace (2)](<../../../../raw/91637.png>)
- Select the device directory. ![Importing a device into your workspace (3)](<../../../../raw/91638.png>)
- Click OK.Note When you select the device directory, you have to enter the device directory by double-clicking, otherwise you will get the error message /<path>/<directory> does not have .technology file.

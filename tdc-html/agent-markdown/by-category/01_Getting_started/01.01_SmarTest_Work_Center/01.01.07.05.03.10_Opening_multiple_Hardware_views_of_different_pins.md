# Opening multiple Hardware views of different pins

You can compare the settings of different pins by opening multiple Hardware views.

## Before you begin

You have updated the hardware information in the Pin Browser view.

In the Hardware view, the Link with Editor and Selection button has been pressed.

## Procedure

-  To open a Hardware view for one pin, in the Pin Browser view, right-click the desired pin and select Show in Hardware. The setting of the selected pin is displayed in the Hardware view.
-  In the Hardware view, click the Pin this View button ![Zoom in by large step button](<../../../../raw/106962.png>) on the top right corner. The display of this Hardware view is pinned to this pin.
-  Then you can repeat step 1 to open the second Hardware view for another pin.

## Results

Multiple Hardware views are opened. Then you can compare the settings of different pins.

## Functional changes

- Introduced in SmarTest 6.5.0

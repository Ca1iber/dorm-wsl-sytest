# Changing Preferences

You can change the general preference and the 93000 customized preference of your workspace. The following example changes the font of the System and Datalog console and the 93000 test number setting.

## Procedures

### Change the General preference

- Click Window > Preferences.
- In the Preferences window, navigate to General > Appearance > Colors and Fonts > Advantest V93000 > System and Datalog Console > Default Font, click the Change button on the right side. ![Changing Preferences](<../../../../raw/108780.png>)
- Select the Font you want to see on the Console and click OK. ![Changing the font](<../../../../raw/108781.png>)

### Change the 93000 customized preference

- In the Preferences window, navigate to Advantest V93000 > Test Numbering.
- Change the First test number to 10 and click OK. ![Change the 93000 test number preference](<../../../../raw/109161.png>)

## Functional changes

- Introduced before SmarTest 6.3.2

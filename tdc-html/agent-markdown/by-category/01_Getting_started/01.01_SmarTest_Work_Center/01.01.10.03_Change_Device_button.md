# Change Device button

#### Subtopics

- [Creating a new device](<01.01.10.03.01_Creating_a_new_device.md>)
- [Changing the device with the Change Device button](<01.01.10.03.02_Changing_the_device_with_the_Change_Device_button.md>)
- [Editing technology data](<01.01.10.03.03_Editing_technology_data.md>)

The Change Device button is on the V93000 menu icon bar of the SmarTest Eclipse Work Center. You can also access this button by clicking the menu 93000 > Device > Change Device.

  ![Change Device button](<../../../../raw/111192.png>)

When you click the Change Device button, you can perform the following actions:

- changing the active device
- [Editing technology data](<01.01.10.03.03_Editing_technology_data.md>)

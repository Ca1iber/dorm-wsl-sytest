# V93000 Tool Bar

#### Subtopics

- [Break button](<01.01.10.01_Break_button.md>)
- [Connect and disconnect buttons](<01.01.10.02_Connect_and_disconnect_buttons.md>)
- [Change Device button](<01.01.10.03_Change_Device_button.md>)

The V93000 toolbar is a part of the short cut bar of the SmarTest Eclipse window . The V93000 toolbar appears in all predefined perspectives.

  ![V93000 Tool Bar](<../../../../raw/92180.png>)

Starting SmarTest with the Setup perspective (nothing loaded yet) you can access the following SmarTest tools.

Note Depending on the active perspective and view, the available functions may vary. Bold typed functions are available for all perspectives. Additional functions, not listed in the table, may be available for other perspectives.

# Editing a fixed pin group

## Procedure

-  To add pins to the pin group, select them in the Available Pin List field and click Add, or click Add All.

To remove pins from the pin group, select them in the Group Members field and click Remove, or click Remove All.

-  To change the order of pins in the pin group, select pins in the Group Members list and use the Up and Down buttons.

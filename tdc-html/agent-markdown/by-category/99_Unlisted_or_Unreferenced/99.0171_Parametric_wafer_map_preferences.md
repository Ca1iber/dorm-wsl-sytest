# Parametric wafer map preferences

X Axis: How x-coordinates are distributed in the graphs:

- 0-------> +x: Dies with higher x-coordinates are located at the right.
- +x <-------0: Dies with higher x-coordinates are located at the left.

Y Axis: How y-coordinates are distributed in the graphs:

- 0-------> +y: Dies with higher y-coordinates are located at the top.
- +y <-------0: Dies with higher y-coordinates are located at the bottom.

Exchange Axes: If this option is checked, the x-axis is the vertical axis and the y-axis is the horizontal axis. If it is not checked, the x-axis is horizontal and the y-axis vertical.

Keep Square: If this option is checked, the wafer map is always square.

Show legend: If this option is checked, a legend is displayed beside the chart.

# TDC feedback and support

Send an e-mail to request a specific version of the standalone TDC or a documentation set, ask questions about a topic, report an issue, or provide feedback for improvement.

- Click Send Feedback at the bottom of the page.

An e-mail is generated in your default e-mail client, with the subject and text body already filled in.

Describe your request or question in detail in the body of the message.

- For Linux users, send an e-mail to [PDL-V93000-Docs@advantest.com](<mailto:PDL-V93000-Docs@advantest.com>).

In your message, refer to a particular page by its unique topic ID (or by a combination of its topic title and topic ID, rather than by its topic title alone) to accurately locate your issue. The topic ID is at the top and bottom right corners of the page.

Send the e-mail and the TDC support team will respond as soon as possible.

# Replying to a ticket

To reply to a ticket, enter the reply in the ticket detail page and click Send.

## About this task

If you reply to a ticket whose status is Closed, it will be reopened.

## Before you begin

You must be [logged in to the help center](<02.16.02.04_Opening_the_help_center.md>).

## Procedure

-  In the help center, click the My Area tab.
-  Click the ticket to which you want to reply.

The details of the ticket are displayed.

![The detail page of the ticket](<../../../../raw/355679.png>)

-  Click Reply.

The [ticket edit field](<02.16.02.03_Ticket_submission_page.md#_355403__edit>) opens.

-  Enter your reply.
-  Click the Send button.

## Results

Your reply has been sent to the TE-Cloud support team.

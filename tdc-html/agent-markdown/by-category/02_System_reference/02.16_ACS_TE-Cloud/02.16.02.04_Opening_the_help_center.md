# Opening the help center

To open the help center, log into the myAdvantest portal and click Help Center on the ACS TE-Cloud home page.

## About this task

The help center provides the support documentation and feedback system for the TE-Cloud platform.

## Before you begin

If you have not received your account information for the myAdvantest portal, request it from the TE-Cloud support team (PDL-ATC-TECloud@advantest.com).

## Procedure

-  Open the [myAdvantest portal](<https://my.advantest.com/>).

Google Chrome and Firefox are the recommended browsers.

-  Log in with your user name and password.
-  In the My Services area, click the ACCESS button in the ACS TE-Cloud pane.

If the My Services area does not include the ACS TE-Cloud pane, you can request TE-Cloud access by clicking the REQUEST button on the ACS TE-Cloud pane in the Other Services area, and log in the myAdvantest portal later until the ACS TE-Cloud pane appears in the My Services area.

The ACS TE-Cloud home page displays.

-  Click Help Center at the top.

## Results

The [Help Center](<02.16.02_Help_Center.md>) is displayed.

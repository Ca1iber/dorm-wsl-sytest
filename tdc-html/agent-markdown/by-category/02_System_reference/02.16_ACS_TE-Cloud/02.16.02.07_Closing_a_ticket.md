# Closing a ticket

To close a ticket, click Close Ticket in the ticket detail page.

## About this task

You can close the ticket when the issue proposed in the ticket has been resolved.

## Before you begin

You must be [logged in to the help center](<02.16.02.04_Opening_the_help_center.md>).

## Procedure

-  In the help center, click the My Area tab.
-  Click the ticket that you want to close.

The details of the ticket are displayed.

-  Click the Close ticket button.

A dialog opens.

![Close a ticket](<../../../../raw/355680.png>)

-  Click Yes.

## Results

This ticket is closed.

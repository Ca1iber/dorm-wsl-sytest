# Utility Card

#### Subtopics

- [Utility Card sitemap](<02.05.08.04.01_Utility_Card_sitemap.md>)
- [Utility Card specifications](<02.05.08.04.02_Utility_Card_specifications.md>)
- [Utility Card functionality and features](<02.05.08.04.03_Utility_Card_functionality_and_features.md>)
- [Utility Card specific setup tasks](<02.05.08.04.04_Utility_Card_specific_setup_tasks.md>)

The Utility Card provides 256 utility lines and several utility power supplies on eight 4-row pogo blocks. The cards can reside in any slot of a V93000 EXA Scale system. There are two variants available, EK051 and EK051A. The EK051 Utility card is used in SmarTest 8 only. The EK051A Utility card is used in both SmarTest 7 (SmarTest version 7.10 and higher) and SmarTest 8.

In the basic configuration, a V93000 EXA Scale system is equipped with few [internal utility resources](<02.05.08.03_Internal_utility_resources_-_V93000_EXA_Scale_systems.md>). These internal resources are 32 statically controlled utility lines, one +5 V utility power supply and a replacement of the legacy [SE-UDPS](<../02.03_DUT_board_design_reference/02.03.05.07_Internal_Utility_Power_Supply__SE-UDPS___V93000_systems.md>) supply.

 The optional Utility Card, replaces the internal utility lines and the internal +5 V utility power supply and provides

- 256 dynamically controlled utility lines,
- one +5 V utility power supply that provides more current,
- a replacement of the legacy [External utility power supply](<02.05.08.01_External_utility_power_supply.md>),
- one utility power supply providing +15 V, -15 V and
- two floating 24 V utility power supplies.
   ![Utility Card photo](<../../../../raw/353302.png>)

Multiple Utility Cards can be installed. This enables high scalability of the provided utility resources.

The utility lines and utility power supplies of a Utility Card are routed to eight 4-row utility pogo blocks.

## Functional changes

- Introduced in SmarTest 7.10.0

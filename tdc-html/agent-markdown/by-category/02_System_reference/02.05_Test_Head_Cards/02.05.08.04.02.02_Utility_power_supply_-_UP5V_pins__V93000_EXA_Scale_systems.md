# Utility power supply - UP5V pins (V93000 EXA Scale systems)

The "Utility power supply - UP5V pins" of V93000 EXA Scale systems is routed to the UP5V pins of the utility pogo blocks. Depending on the availability of the Utility Card, the supply is connected to utility pogo block 1, to utility pogo blocks 1 ... 8 or to other pogo blocks.

Note: The 4-row utility pogo blocks of the second Utility Card replace all existing 3-row DPS-slot pogo blocks.

The DPS block sequence of the second Utility Card differs from the UTIL block sequence of the first card.

# PMU operating mode of DC Scale cards

#### Subtopics

- [Hardware view diagram of DC Scale channels in PMU operating mode](<02.05.05.05.01_Hardware_view_diagram_of_DC_Scale_channels_in_PMU_operating_mode.md>)
- [Measuring current or voltage in PMU operating mode](<02.05.05.05.02_Measuring_current_or_voltage_in_PMU_operating_mode.md>)
- [Relay switching in PMU operating mode](<02.05.05.05.03_Relay_switching_in_PMU_operating_mode.md>)

For PMU operating mode specify the pin type as SIG. In PMU operating mode you can use DC Scale cards like a standard PMU to temporarily supply power in a single testsuite or for a group of testsuites. Each of the 32 channels can source and sink current at a programmed voltage, and each channel can measure the voltages and currents supplied.

## Connect/Disconnect

Note In PMU operating mode, signal pins of a DC Scale card will not be connected during connect and not disconnected during disconnect.Note In PMU operating mode a DC Scale card does not take part in standard connect and disconnect sequences, and cannot take part in the set activation, although you can add DC Scale cards to user-defined connect and disconnect sequences.Note On startup SmarTest always performs a standard disconnect.

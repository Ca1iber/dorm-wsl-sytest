# AVI64 channel group and differential compare assignment

The AVI64 card features some shared resources for groups of eight channels. The card features also ganging of channels and differential compare between combinations of two channels within these groups.

The assignment of the channels to the groups and the channel combination for the differential compare is defined by the hardware of the card and must be considered when designing the DUT board.

## Channel groups

The following table shows the eight channel groups of the AVI64. Shared resources like the floating high-current unit, the floating differential voltmeter and the high-resolution voltage AWG can be connected to any channel or combination of channels within the groups. Ganging is supported for up to eight consecutive channels within the groups.

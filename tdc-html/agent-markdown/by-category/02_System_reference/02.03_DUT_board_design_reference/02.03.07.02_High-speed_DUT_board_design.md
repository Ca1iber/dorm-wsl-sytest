DUT boards are printed circuit board (PCB) structures intended to connect the DUT (device under test) to the pin electronics of the test head.

Depending on the speed and application, a DUT board can be considered as as a simple wire or as an RLC lumped element network. For other applications you may need to treat it like a transmission line, or in some special cases even as a 3D structure that has to be analyzed by a field solver.

For additional information on high-speed design and signal integrity please see the following references.

- Eric Bogatin, Signal and Power Integrity-Simplified (2nd Edition), Prentice Hall, 2009
- Jose Moreira and Hubert Werkmann, An Engineer's Guide to Automated Testing of High-Speed Interfaces, Artech House 2010
- Stephen C. Thierauf, Understanding Signal Integrity, Artech House 2010
- Howard Johnson, High Speed Signal Propagation: Advanced Black Magic, Prentice Hall 2003
- "Right the First Time, A Practical Handbook on High Speed PCB and System Design, Volume 2" Speeding Edge, 2007

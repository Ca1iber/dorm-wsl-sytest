# Safety information

All general safety precautions must be observed during all phases of operation, service, and repair of the V93000 test system. Failure to comply with these precautions or with specific warnings in this document, or in the other V93000 documentation violates safety standards of design, manufacture, and intended use of the system. Advantest assumes no liability for the customer's failure to comply with these requirements.

The installation, service, and maintenance documentation contains further safety information, warnings, and cautions. These guidelines must be followed at all times by service personnel and by users (customers) who install, service, repair, or maintain the V93000.

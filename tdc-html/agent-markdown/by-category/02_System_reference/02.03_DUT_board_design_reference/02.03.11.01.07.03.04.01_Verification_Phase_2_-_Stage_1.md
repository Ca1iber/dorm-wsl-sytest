# Verification Phase 2 - Stage 1

## About this task

This procedure must be performed by trained personnel experienced with the maintenance and operation of the Accretech UF3000EX series probers.

In this task you verify "high" stiffener clamping. You will load the "high" stiffener into the prober during this procedure.

## Procedure

-  Disconnect the tester interlock cable from the V93000 test-head.
-  Connect the Tester Interlock Emulator to the tester interlock cable.
-  Turn on the prober.
-  On the prober User Interface, press INITIALIZE. Wait for the prober to complete initialization.
-  On the prober User Interface, press CARD CHANGE, then press CARD CHANGE START.
-  On the prober User Interface, press ATTACH to begin the probe-card loading process. Follow the instructions shown on the prober UI to access the APC tray.
-  Load the "high" stiffener onto the APC tray and follow the instructions shown on the prober User Interface to load the "high" stiffener onto the bridge beam.

  -  During loading, when the "high" stiffener touches the bottom of the bridge beam, toggle the emulator SD switch to ON.
  -  When the prober User Interface is showing 'Card-holder is setting', toggle the emulator DD switch to ON.

-  After the APC tray has completely retracted into the loading area, press EXIT on the prober User Interface.
-  Move Z Pos up until the target pads on the "high" stiffener come into focus in the camera view window.
-  Center the crosshairs in the camera view window on the center of any one of the target pads.
-  Hook the tension gauge onto "high" stiffener corner posts and pull in the directions shown, then release.
-  With each pull of the tension gauge and release, observe the camera view window. The crosshairs should not deviate by more than 3 µm from their original position, before the pull of the tension gauge.     ![Stiffener pull directions](<../../../../raw/344005.png>)
-  On the prober User Interface, press CARD CHANGE, then press CARD CHANGE START.
-  On the prober User Interface, press REMOVE, to begin the probe-card unloading process. Follow the instructions shown on the prober User Interface to access the APC tray.
-  The prober APC tray will retrieve the "high" stiffener from the bridge beam.

  -  During unloading, when the "high" stiffener physically disconnects from the bottom of the bridge beam, toggle the emulator SD switch to OFF.
  -  When the prober User Interface displays 'Tray-unit moved at the Upper-Position', toggle the emulator DD switch to OFF.

-  After the APC tray has completely retracted into the loading area with the "high" stiffener, remove the “high鈥?stiffener from the tray.

## Results

You have completed Stage 1 of the second verification phase involving the "high" stiffener tool.

# Installing Direct-Probe bridge beam on TEL Precio wafer prober

## About this task

This topic tells you how to position and affix the WSRF Bridge Beam on the TEL Precio wafer prober.

## Before you begin

Remove all six cable channel covers, see figure below.

 ![Prober stage with cable channel covers fitted](<../../../../raw/152083.png>) CAUTION Two persons are required to install the Direct Probe™Bridge Beam hardware. This is to ensure that no damage is caused to the sensor cables or to the pneumatic hoses leading to the Bridge Beam.

Make sure you have covered the wafer prober chuck with protective material. Refer to [Protecting the chuck on the wafer prober](<02.03.11.01.08.01_Protecting_the_chuck_on_the_wafer_prober.md>) for instructions.

## Procedure

-  Place the Bridge Beam on the wafer prober.

The wafer prober has two guiding pins that serve to align the Bridge Beam on the prober. These are shown in the figure below.

  ![Locations of guiding pins for Bridge Beam on TEL wafer prober](<../../../../raw/151889.jpg>)

Each person grasps the handle at his end of the Bridge Beam with one hand and the cables and hoses leading to the Bridge Beam with the other, refer to the figure below. Locate the Bridge Beam on the guiding pins. Make sure the arrows on the Bridge Beam point towards the manipulator.

Take care not to pinch the cables or the hoses between the bridge beam and the prober stage, or otherwise damage them.

  ![Placing Bridge Beam on TEL prober](<../../../../raw/151890.jpg>)

  -  Lay out the cables and hoses so as to keep them away from the Bridge Beam, see below. This avoids damaging these parts.  ![Placing Bridge Beam cables and hoses](<../../../../raw/151891.jpg>)

-  Remove both handles from the Bridge Beam.    ![Bridge Beam handle](<../../../../raw/151893.jpg>)

![Torx](<../../../../raw/3154.png>)Use a T30 Torx screwdriver to unscrew the fixing bolts.

   ![Removing bridge beam handle](<../../../../raw/152084.png>)
-  Affix the Bridge Beam to the wafer prober with the bolts provided.

  -  Insert the eight fixing bolts supplied with the Bridge Beam into the mounting holes, four on each side. Refer to the figure below.
  -  ![Allen8](<../../../../raw/112994.png>)Use an 8 mm angled Allen wrench to affix the Bridge Beam.
  -  Tighten to a torque of 19 Nm.    ![Affixing bridge beam to prober](<../../../../raw/152085.png>)   Note Leave the protective foam sheeting on the prober chuck.

## Results

You have mounted the WSRF Bridge Beam hardware on the TEL Precio Wafer Prober.

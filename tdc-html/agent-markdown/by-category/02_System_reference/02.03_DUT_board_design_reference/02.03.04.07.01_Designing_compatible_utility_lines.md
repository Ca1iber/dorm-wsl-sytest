# Designing compatible utility lines

Within the V93000 system, the utility lines are routed to four or eight 2-row or 4-row utility blocks and, depending on the type of test head, the internal utility power supplies are shared by two, four or eight utility blocks. We recommend assigning the utility lines and the utility power on the DUT board so that the DUT board can be used on all test head types.

## Configuration of the internal utility power supplies

 Note This section applies only to V93000 Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems. The [internal utility power supplies](<02.03.05.05_Internal_utility_power_supply__V93000_systems.md>) are part of the clock boards. An internal utility power supply delivers a maximum of. 3.2 A at 4.85V (+1%, -5%, measured at the pogo block). Depending on the test head type and the type of the DUT interface, up to five internal utility power supplies are routed to up to eight utility blocks. Therefore, an internal utility power supply is connected to one, two, four or eight utility blocks in parallel.Note You can connect together the blocks sharing a common internal utility power supply. The minimum line width for a low-impedance connection is 10 mm. CAUTION Do not connect together the power supply pins of individual power supplies or those supplying different card cages.

## Design rules

 Note This section applies only to V93000 Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems.

- Evenly distribute all active components and utility lines among the available utility pogo blocks up to a total current of 800 mA per block!

This way all blocks provide the same current and are used independently of each other, and no further design considerations need to be taken into account.

- On dedicated pogo blocks, using an individual internal utility power supply, you can draw a maximum current of 1 A!Note The large DUT board cannot be used on all test heads with the large DUT interface if this rule is applied.

If more than 1 A is drawn, the output voltage will go below 4.85 V -5%, due to the increased voltage drop across the pogo cable. The pogo pins within the utility pogo block have a 1 A current rating in total. If more is drawn, the pins overheat and may become damaged.

If a group of components requires more than 1 A, only the pogo blocks supplied by a common internal utility power supply can be recombined (ganged) on the DUT board. However, compatibility issues with other test head types must be considered in this case.

- Do not gang different internal utility power supplies on the DUT board!

If supplies are ganged, strong currents may flow between them which may cause them to shut off.

Note The internal utility power supplies are electronically fused. If more than 3.2 A are drawn, the supply heats up triggering the electronic fuse. The supply turns off. After cooling down, the supply comes back up.
CAUTION Never connect the external 5V utility power lines (XPP5V) to the internal 5V power lines (UP5V). Strong currents will flow and may cause damage to the test system.Note To minimize voltage drops on the internal 5V power lines (UP5V) it is good practice to always

- connect the two UP5V pogo pins on each pogo block together on the DUT board and to
- use wide traces with heavy metalization to distribute power on the DUT board.

## Utility line characteristics and assignments

The utility lines feature an open collector design and can sink up to 60 mA each on V93000 Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems and up to 100 mA on V93000 EXA Scale systems. For more information, refer to [Utility line characteristics](<02.03.04.02_Utility_line_characteristics.md>).

Each utility block provides 16 or 32 utility lines, depending on the block type used. For information on utility block types see [Utility line assignments](<02.03.04.01_Utility_line_assignments.md>).

# WPI DUT board stiffener manufacturing guidelines

Drawings D-E7018-24104 (512 pins) and D-E7018-24101 (1024 pins) show the mechanical dimensions, tolerances, and machining instructions for the 9.5" WPI DUT board stiffeners. Drawings D-E7018-24150 and D-E7018-24160 provide that information for the 12" WPI DUT board stiffeners.

 Note Refer to drawings D-E7018-24104, D-E7018-24101, D-E7018-24150, and D-E7018-24160 in [Stiffener prober interface](<02.03.12.01.17_Stiffener_prober_interface.md>).Do not mill the stiffener to provide additional clearance for surface trace lines or components on the DUT board. Such milling decreases the mechanical stability of the assembly. Route surface trace lines in areas where the stiffener does not contact the board and place components in the available mounting areas. Whenever possible, place signal lines on inner layers of the DUT board. If milling is absolutely necessary, maintain mechanical stability by milling as little as possible. Avoid milling in the mounting screw areas, as this can cause damage to the board when it is mounted to the stiffener.

Advantest is not responsible for direct or indirect damages caused by the use of non-Advantest stiffeners.

Use clear anodizing on all aluminum stiffener surfaces and use hard chromium plating on all steel surfaces.Stainless steel surfaces must be passivated to minimize oxidation on the stainless steel. Advantest recommends a coating thickness of 10 to 15 碌m. All dimensions shown for the stiffeners are final dimensions after coating. Anodizing is not a reliable insulation and is used mainly for cosmetic reasons. Advantest uses clear anodizing, though other types are acceptable.

The stiffener must have the company name, logo and part number engraved on top. Two engraved arrows on the top and on the bottom surfaces of the stiffener must be included that point toward the manipulator when the stiffener is properly installed on the DUT interface.

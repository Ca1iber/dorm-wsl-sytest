DUT board design guidelines and recommendations for DC applications differ from those for high-speed designs.

This section provides some general design recommendations for DC applications and guidelines on shielding and guarding. Recommendations for connecting force and sense lines for very low currents, high accuracy voltage forcing, and high accuracy voltage measurement are also covered.

For high-speed designs see [High-speed DUT board design](<02.03.07.02_High-speed_DUT_board_design.md>).

## Power limitation

 In all cases you must consider the power consumption during device test and the card cage power limitations of the V93000 test system:

- The sustainable total load per channel card for PPMU and active load may not exceed 1600 mA for the following digital cards:

  - [PS1600](<../02.05_Test_Head_Cards/02.05.04.01_Pin_Scale_1600_digital_card__Smart_Scale.md>)
  - PS400 (SmarTest 7 only)

- You must distribute DUT sites across the DUT board so as not to overload individual DC/DC power units (for PPMU, TMU, Active Load).
- For power supply requirements for high frequency RF DUT board designs see [DPS layout rules for high frequency designs](<02.03.07.01.04_DPS_layout_rules_for_high_frequency_designs.md>) .
- For lower frequency design information refer to the section for the specific DPS you are using (see links above).
- For the pin layout of the various DPSs see [Pogo pin assignment - DC Scale cards](<02.03.06.02_Pogo_pin_assignment_-_DC_Scale_cards.md>) and for SmarTest 7 also [Pogo pin assignment - DPS-slot device power supplies](<02.03.06.04_Pogo_pin_assignment_-_DPS-slot_device_power_supplies.md>).

Using utility lines allows you to control or read the states of external devices such as relays, EEPROMS and indicators on your DUT board. Utility lines can be controlled statically (workstation鈥慶ontrolled) or dynamically (sequencer鈥慶ontrolled).

  Utility lines are provided by

- the internal wiring board of V93000 Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems, or by
- the [internal utility resources](<../02.05_Test_Head_Cards/02.05.08.03_Internal_utility_resources_-_V93000_EXA_Scale_systems.md>) or the [optional Utility Card](<../02.05_Test_Head_Cards/02.05.08.04_Utility_Card.md>) of V93000 EXA Scale systems.

Depending on the system configuration, the utility lines can be accessed via the utility pogo blocks UTIL 1 鈥?nbsp;UTIL 8 of the [DUT interface](<02.03.03.01_DUT_interface_layout.md>). On V93000 EXA Scale systems that are equipped with a second [Utility Card](<../02.05_Test_Head_Cards/02.05.08.04_Utility_Card.md>), utility lines are also available on the pogo blocks DPS 1 鈥?nbsp;DPS 8.

 Utility lines can be programmed by

- dedicated utility instrument properties or test methods of SmarTest 8 or by
- dedicated firmware commands, routing sets or test methods of SmarTest 7.

Utility lines are not affected by standard connect/disconnect sequences.

## Sequencer鈥慶ontrolled utility lines

On V93000 EXA Scale systems equipped with the [optional Utility Card](<../02.05_Test_Head_Cards/02.05.08.04_Utility_Card.md>), the pattern sequencer controls utility lines via an internal data path.

On Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems equipped with an active wiring board, utility lines are generated by a controller on this board and can be controlled dynamically by the pattern sequencer.

For more information, refer to [Sequencer-controlled utility lines](<../../03_Use_cases_and_application_papers/03.09_How_to_set_up_sequencer__ontrolled_utility_lines/03.09.01_Sequencer-controlled_utility_lines.md>).

## GND and noise of utility lines

The GND connection and the noise level of utility lines need your attention:

- For information on the use of filters to reduce noise, see [Reducing noise on utility lines](<02.03.04.03.02_Reducing_noise_on_utility_lines.md>).
- The ground pins of the utility pogo blocks (UGND) can be connected to digital ground planes (DGND) of the DUT board, however you may need to insert galvanic separation circuitry on the DUT board between the two ground domains. This is to prevent a negative impact on level accuracy and the introduction of noise into your measurements, due to ground loops. An example of such circuitry is provided in [Galvanic separation between utility line serial interface and digital pins](<02.03.04.05.05_Galvanic_separation_between_utility_line_serial_interface_and_digital_pi.md>).

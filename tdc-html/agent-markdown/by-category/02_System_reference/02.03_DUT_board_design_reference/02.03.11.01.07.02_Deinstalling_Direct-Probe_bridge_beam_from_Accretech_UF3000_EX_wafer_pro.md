# Deinstalling Direct-Probe bridge beam from Accretech UF3000 EX wafer prober

## About this task

This topic tells you how to remove a bridge beam installed on the Accretech UF3000 EX Wafer Prober.

## Before you begin

 Note Deinstallation must only be performed by trained personnel experienced with the operation and maintenance of Accretech UF3000EX series probers.

## Procedure

-   ![Pozidriv Pz3](<../../../../raw/73673.png>) Remove bridge beam sensor wiring/air tubing covers from the prober head-stage.  ![Removing cable covers from the wafer prober stage](<../../../../raw/151270.jpg>)
-   Disconnect the bridge beam sensor cables at the connection point closest to the bridge beam inside the head-plate cable groove. The cables are marked R-CLP, R-UNCLP, L-CLP, and L-UNCLP.    ![Bridge beam sensor cables to disconnect](<../../../../raw/343908.jpg>)
-  Remove the sensor cables and air hoses from the cable clamp.    ![Clamp for cables and hoses](<../../../../raw/343909.jpg>)
-  On the side of the prober, remove the cover over the compressed air valves.    ![Compressed air valves and cover](<../../../../raw/343910.jpg>)
-  Shut off compressed air to the bridge beam by turning the air valve 180° clockwise as shown in the figure.     ![Compressed air valve](<../../../../raw/343911.jpg>)
-  Disonnect the air hoses from the Bridge Beam to the pneumatic connectors as shown in the figure below:     ![Air hoses disconnected](<../../../../raw/343912.jpg>)
-  Remove the bridge beam mounting screws.

![Allen8](<../../../../raw/112994.png>)Use an 8 mm Allen wrench.

   ![Removing Bridge Beam mounting screws](<../../../../raw/151267.jpg>)
-  Lift off the bridge beam from the prober head-plate. It is recommended that the removal of the bridge beam is performed by two persons.    ![Removing the bridge beam](<../../../../raw/151266.jpg>)

## Results

You have de-installed a bridge beam from the Accretech UF3000 EX wafer prober.

   ![Empty base plate on wafer prober](<../../../../raw/151261.jpg>)

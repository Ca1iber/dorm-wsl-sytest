# HV protection - Compliance range

Two kinds of test head cards work together in HV applications. High-voltage cards provide enough voltage and energy to damage low-voltage cards.

## High-voltage test head cards

The following high-voltage cards are available:

- DC Scale device power supplies and VI cards

  - AVI64
  - [DPS128/DPS64 HV version](<../02.05_Test_Head_Cards/02.05.05.01_DC_Scale_DPS128_DPS64_cards.md>) (SmarTest 7)
  - DPS128 HV version (SmarTest 8)
  - FVI16 (SmarTest 7)

- Pin Scale and Smart Scale digital cards

  - [PS 1600 HV (High voltage mode)](<../02.05_Test_Head_Cards/02.05.04.01_Pin_Scale_1600_digital_card__Smart_Scale.md>) (SmarTest 7)
  - PS 1600 HV (High voltage mode) (SmarTest 8)

The high-voltage cards provide enough voltage and energy to damage low-voltage cards but the combination of different high-voltage cards must also be checked carefully. The channels of a high-voltage card need additional protection circuitry on the DUT board if the compliance range can be exceeded by another card or by an external supply. The following list shows some examples for possible conflicts:

- Incompatibility of the operating range and the compliance range of different cards.
- Combination of high-voltage device power supplies and VI cards with high-voltage Pin Scale and Smart Scale digital cards.
- Combination of high-voltage cards with floating channels (for example, PVI8) that are stacked.
- Combination with floating channels (for example, PVI8) that are connected with inverted polarity.

## Low-voltage test head cards

Generally, all low-voltage cards need additional protection circuitry on the DUT board if a high-voltage card is used in an application:

- Low-voltage digital cards
- Analog cards
- Device power supplies (DC Scale cards)
- DPS-slot device power supplies of SmarTest 7
- Port Scale RF cards of SmarTest 7 and Wave Scale RF cards of SmarTest 8
- Utility cards

## Maximum external input voltage (compliance range) of test head cards

The maximum external input voltage (compliance range) of the test head cards defines the maximum voltage that can be applied without damage. Additional protection circuitry is required on the DUT board if these test head cards are used together with high-voltage cards and the programmed voltage exceeds the compliance range as defined below.

Note All test head cards provide a basic protection against electrostatic discharge (ESD) but this protection cannot absorb the energy coming from a high-voltage card.

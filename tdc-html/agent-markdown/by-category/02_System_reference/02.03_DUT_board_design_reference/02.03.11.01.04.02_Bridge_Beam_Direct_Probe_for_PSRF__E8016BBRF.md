# Bridge Beam Direct Probe for PSRF (E8016BBRF)

The bridge beam for PSRF (E8016BBRF) supports the connection to the PSRF Direct-Probe鈩?tower of the tester.

  The E8016BBRF bridge beam provides

- certified tolerances, matching with Advantest made probe card stiffeners, for trouble free operation,
- cam lock interlock with probe cards eliminates realignment need after cleaning interval.

## Availability

The E8016BBRF bridge beam is available from Advantest.

## Integration

The following graphic shows how the Direct-Probe鈩?bridge beam is integrated for overhead docking on a prober. The prober hosts the Direct-Probe鈩?solution that consists of the PSRF Direct-Probe鈩? tower and the Direct-Probe鈩?bridge beam. PSRF Direct-Probe鈩?tower and Direct-Probe鈩?bridge beam are designed to frame the probe card that is docked from below. The test head is docked from the top and connects to pogo pin areas on both sides of the bridge beam and, via the RF tower, to the PSRF brackets on the probe card.

  ![Direct-Probe鈩?RF components](<../../../../raw/123155.png>)

## Improved probe card interlock

Advantest Direct-Probe鈩? bridge beams feature a new probe card interlock with superior XY repeatability after cleaning step.

 |   ![Bridge Beam E8016BBWS probe card interlock (349670)](<../../../../raw/349670.png>) |

This interlock improves the probe card alignment after probe head cleaning.

During card clamp operation, the Direct-Probe鈩?bridge beam pushes and holds the probe card alignment pins to the reference point and restricts the XY card movement.

 |

As shown in the following graphics, the improved probe card alignment refers to guide pins 1 (top right) and 4 (bottom left) in the outer positions but guide pin 2 (bottom right) is also required.

 ![Bridge Beam E8016BBWS probe card alignment pins](<../../../../raw/349669.png>) For more information regarding the referenced and required guide pins, refer to note 3 on page 4 of file E7018-64170_...pdf in the [E7018DP fabrication package](<02.03.12.01.02_Direct-Probe_stiffener.md#_138465__dle_E7018-64170_E7018DP>).

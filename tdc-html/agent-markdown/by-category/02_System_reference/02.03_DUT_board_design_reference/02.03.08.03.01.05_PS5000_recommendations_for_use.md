# PS5000 recommendations for use

For safe handling of the Pin Scale 5000 card and getting out the best performance follow the recommendations for designing the DUT board and setting up the test.

## Sense connection test

The PS5000 card features [remote sensing](<02.03.08.03.01.03_PS5000_remote_sensing.md>) and [ground sensing](<02.03.08.03.01.04_PS5000_ground_sensing.md>). For the basic principle of remote sensing and ground sensing, refer to [Remote sensing](<02.03.07.01.07_Remote_sensing.md>) in the [DUT board design reference](<02.03_DUT_board_design_reference.md>).

Sense lines that have no contact with the DUT are useless and can falsify your test results. An unstable connection that changes from DUT to DUT can increase the variance of the test results. For reliable test results, we recommend therefore, testing the force to sense (FS) connection:

- The test is required once after the DUT board or probe card has been loaded if the FS connection is on the DUT board or on the probe card.
- The test is required after each connection of the DUT if the FS connection is on the DUT or a Kelvin socket is used.
  Principle of the sense connection test

The sense connection test is done by forcing a small test current via the sense line to the FS connection at the other end and measuring the voltage drop caused by the line and the contact resistance of the probe or socket.

Measuring the voltage on the sense line without applying a defined test current will not deliver reliable results due to parasitic leakage currents and the input bias current of the sense line.

Since the input impedance of the sense lines is very high, an accuracy up to some kilo-ohms is sufficient for the sense connection test.

 Implementation of a sense connection test

PS5000 digital cards do not feature a sense connection test unit like the DC Scale cards, Therefore, you have to set up a test manually and also consider the test of the ground sense connection when designing a DUT board:

- [Remote sensing](<02.03.08.03.01.03_PS5000_remote_sensing.md>):

Remote sensing means that two adjacent channels are connected together with one channel used as force channel and the other one used as sense channel. A variant of remote sensing are two ganged channels that are sensed by a third channel.

For remote sensing you need just another signal definition in SmarTest that allow you to setup the force channel and the sense channel separately. Set the force channel to 0.0 V, force a small test current by the sense channel and measure the voltage drop on the sense line. You may combine this test also with of continuity test of all DUT pins.

- [Ground sensing](<02.03.08.03.01.04_PS5000_ground_sensing.md>):

A reliable test of the ground sense connection requires also that a defined test current is forced via the ground sense connection to the common ground.

Therefore you need to connect another PS5000 channel or another resource to the ground sense line. The connected resource must be able to force a small current and measure the voltage drop to ground. You need a separate resource for each ground sense connection to the DUT.

## V93000 Smart Scale and Pin Scale systems

  Analog terminology

Analog cards for the V93000 combine three basic categories of source and measure units:

- Arbitrary Waveform Generators (AWG) are used as analog signal sources.
- Digitizers are used for analog measurements.
- Samplers (SP) can capture very high frequency periodic signals that cannot be captured by general waveform digitizers.

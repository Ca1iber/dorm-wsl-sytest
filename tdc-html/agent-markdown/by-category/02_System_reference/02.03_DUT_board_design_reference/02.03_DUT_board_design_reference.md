Advantest is committed to providing high quality interfacing solutions for the V93000 test systems. These interfacing solutions include DUT boards, wafer prober interface boards, and wafer probe cards which can be obtained directly from Advantest or from other manufacturers that specialize in dedicated interfacing components.

This design guide provides the information needed to design and manufacture these devices such that they maintain the high-performance testing capabilities of the V93000.

## V93000 systems covered by this guide

Many sections of the guide apply to all generations and variants of the V93000 system and are independent of the installed SmarTest version. This common guide contains therefore topics that may not apply to your system or SmarTest version. The involved topics are structured accordingly in an overview topic or otherwise marked. An example for this structure is used in topic [Pogo pin assignment](<02.03.06_Pogo_pin_assignment.md>).

 Generations and variants of the V93000 system covered by this guide:

- V93000 EXA Scale system (SmarTest 8)
- V93000 Smart Scale system (SmarTest 8)
- V93000 Smart Scale system (SmarTest 7)
- V93000 Pin Scale system
- V93000 HSM system

## Design and manufacturing knowledge

This design guide assumes that the reader has knowledge of the design and manufacture of controlled impedance printed circuit boards. Although Advantest recommends that you obtain stiffeners and inlay plates used in the interface assemblies directly from Advantest, information needed to manufacture these components is also provided. These mechanical components must be manufactured within the tolerances specified in this design guide and in the referenced technical drawings. The electrical and mechanical characteristics of the described parts are critical to the test system's proper operation. Be sure to carefully follow all instructions in this design guide and in all referenced technical drawings.

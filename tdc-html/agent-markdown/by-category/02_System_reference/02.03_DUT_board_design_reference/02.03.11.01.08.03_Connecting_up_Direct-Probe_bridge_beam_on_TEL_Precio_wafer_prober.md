# Connecting up Direct-Probe bridge beam on TEL Precio wafer prober

This topic tells you how to connect up the sensor cables and compressed air hoses between the Bridge Beam and TEL Precio wafer prober.

## About this task

The WSRF Bridge Beam is fitted with four compressed air hoses for the control of the interface and four cables that provide sensor signals back to the wafer prober about the status of the Bridge Beam.

At the front end of the Bridge Beam (the end mounted closest to the SACC door on the prober) there are the following services:

- Sensor cable marked FRONT CLAMP
- Sensor cable marked FRONT UNCLAMP
- Compressed air hose marked FRONT CLAMP
- Compressed air hose marked FRONT UNCLAMP

At the rear end of the Bridge Beam (the end mounted away from the SACC door on the prober) there are the following services:

- Sensor cable marked REAR CLAMP
- Sensor cable marked REAR UNCLAMP
- Compressed air hose marked REAR CLAMP
- Compressed air hose marked REAR UNCLAMP

A set of extension cables is provided with the Bridge Beam. These cables connect between the cables from the Bridge Beam and those from the wafer prober.

## Before you begin

Remove the large top cover at the back of the prober, fee the figure below. Exercise care, the cover is heavy.

 ![Pneumatic hose cover](<../../../../raw/151919.jpg>)

Underneath the cover you will find sensor cables to the prober as well as compressed air hoses.

 ![Pneumatic hose cover removed](<../../../../raw/151920.jpg>)

It is these cables and hoses that you will connect the cables and hoses from the Bridge Beam to in the procedure below.

## Procedure

-  Connect the end of each sensor cable from the Bridge Beam to the matching end of the extension cable, refer to the mapping table above. The figure below shows the connections at the front end of the Bridge Beam.    ![Connections between sensor cables and extension cables](<../../../../raw/151902.jpg>)
-  Place the extension cables and the compressed air hoses into the openings in the back of the prober stage (on the opposite side to the SACC door).    ![Opening in prober stage for cables and hoses](<../../../../raw/151903.jpg>)
-  Route the sensor cables and the compressed air hoses as shown in the figure below.

Press the cables and hoses neatly into the routing channels in the prober stage.

   ![Routing of cables and hoses from Bridge Beam](<../../../../raw/151904.jpg>)
-  Replace all covers over the cable channels.   ![Cable channel covers in position](<../../../../raw/151915.jpg>)
-  Connect up all sensor cables with those from the prober. Following cables have to be connected:

  - CN4054
  - CN4055
  - CN4056
  - CN4057

The following figure gives the appearance of one of the cables.

   ![Bridge Beam sensor cable](<../../../../raw/151896.jpg>)

-  Cut the compressed air hoses to the required length. The following figure shows the completed layout of the cables and hoses.    ![Pneumatic hose cover removed](<../../../../raw/151920.jpg>)
-  Connect the compressed air hose A2 on the left side as shown in the figure below.    ![Connection of compressed air hose A2](<../../../../raw/151921.jpg>)
-  Connect the compressed air hose A1 on the right side as shown in the figure below.    ![Connection of compressed air hose A1](<../../../../raw/151922.jpg>)
-  Close all covers.
-  Reconnect the compressed air supply to the wafer prober, refer to [Protecting the chuck on the wafer prober](<02.03.11.01.08.01_Protecting_the_chuck_on_the_wafer_prober.md>).
-  Remove the protective foam sheeting from the chuck, refer to [Protecting the chuck on the wafer prober](<02.03.11.01.08.01_Protecting_the_chuck_on_the_wafer_prober.md>).

## Results

You have completed the installation of compressed air hoses and sensor cables to the Bridge Beam.

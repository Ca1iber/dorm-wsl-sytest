# HV protection - Impact on stimulations and measurements

#### Subtopics

- [HV protection - Impact on leakage current example](<02.03.07.03.04.01_HV_protection_-_Impact_on_leakage_current_example.md>)
- [HV protection - Impact on rise time example](<02.03.07.03.04.02_HV_protection_-_Impact_on_rise_time_example.md>)

Additional circuitry on a DUT board always will have an impact on the stimulation and the measurements performed in a test. The details differ for DC and AC.

## DC impact

For DC, the impact may include:

- Leakage current measurements may show a minimally increased result.
- The settling time may increase a little.

The reverse current of the clamp diodes and the parasitic capacitance will affect leakage currents and settling times. Please see [HV protection - Impact on leakage current example](<02.03.07.03.04.01_HV_protection_-_Impact_on_leakage_current_example.md>) for more information.

## AC impact

For AC, the impact may include:

- The maximum usable frequency may be reduced a little.
- The waveform quality may reflect the additional disturbances.

The clamp diodes and the additional conductive tracks, pads, and vias will decrease the maximum usable frequency and degrade the waveform quality. Please see [HV protection - Impact on rise time example](<02.03.07.03.04.02_HV_protection_-_Impact_on_rise_time_example.md>) for more information.

## Functional changes

- Introduced in SmarTest 7.3.1

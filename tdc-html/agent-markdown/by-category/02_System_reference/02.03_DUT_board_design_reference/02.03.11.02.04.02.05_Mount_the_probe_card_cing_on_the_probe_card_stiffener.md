# Mount the probe card cing on the probe card stiffener

## About this task

The prepared probe card ring including the RF cables needs to be mounted onto the probe card stiffener.

## Before you begin

Ensure you have prepared the probe card ring and have T10 countersink screws available.

## Procedure

-  Arrange the stiffener so the arrows on its edge point away from you.
-  Place the probe card ring on the stiffener, aligning the arrows on the ring with those on the stiffener.  ![Mount the Probe Card Ring on the Probe Card Stiffener](<../../../../raw/103252.jpg>)
-  Secure the ring to the stiffener with six T10 countersink screws.
-  Connect the SMP rectangle female connectors (N2398A-003) to the probe card as illustrated in the next picture.  ![SMP female connected to probe card](<../../../../raw/103253.jpg>)

## What to do next

Mount the probe card into the prober manufacturer kit.

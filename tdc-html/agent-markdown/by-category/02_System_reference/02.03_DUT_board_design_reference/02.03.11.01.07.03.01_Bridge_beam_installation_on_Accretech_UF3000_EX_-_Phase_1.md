# Bridge beam installation on Accretech UF3000 EX - Phase 1

An outline of the installation process is given in [Bridge beam installation on Accretech UF3000 EX prober - Overview](<02.03.11.01.07.03_Bridge_beam_installation_on_Accretech_UF3000_EX_prober_-_Overview.md>).

## About this task

This task deals with Phase 1 (of 2) of the installation process.

 Note This procedure must be performed by trained personnel experienced with the operation and maintenance of Accretech UF3000EX series probers.

## Before you begin

 CAUTION Two persons are required to install the Direct Probe鈩?Bridge Beam hardware. This is to ensure that no damage is caused to the sensor cables or to the pneumatic hoses leading to the Bridge Beam.

## Procedure

-  Place the Bridge Beam on the wafer prober.

Note the orientation of the head plate on the prober:

  - For side dock, arrows on the bridge beam must point towards the left side of the prober when viewed from its front.
  - For rear dock, arrows on the bridge beam must point towards the rear of the prober when viewed from its front.

The wafer prober has two guiding pins in the head plate that serve to align the Bridge Beam on the prober. These are shown in the figure below.

  ![Locations of guide pins on the wafer prober](<../../../../raw/151265.jpg>)

  -  One person grasps the Bridge Beam with both hands and positions it on the wafer prober as shown in the following figure. The guiding pins on the prober must align with the guiding holes in the Bridge Beam.    ![Placing the bridge beam](<../../../../raw/151266.jpg>)
  -  At the same time the other person ensures that the sensor cable and the pneumatic hoses connected to both ends of the Bridge Beam do not get get pinched between it and the wafer prober, refer to the figure above. Lay out the cables and air hoses as shown in the next figure.
    ![Laying out Bridge Beam cables and air hoses](<../../../../raw/151269.jpg>)
-  Remove the shipping handles from either side of the bridge beam.
-  Affix the Bridge Beam to the wafer prober with four M8 socket head bolts at each end.

![Allen8](<../../../../raw/112994.png>)Use an 8 mm Allen wrench. Tighten to a torque of 19 Nm.

   ![Affixing Bridge Beam to prober](<../../../../raw/151267.jpg>)
-  Connect up the sensor cables to the bridge beam as follows:

  - Connect the white connector at the end of the adapter cable to the appropriate sensor cable on the WSRF bridge beam.
  - Connect the black connector on the adapter cable to the matching prober connector. ![Sensor cable to Bridge Beam](<../../../../raw/343900.jpg>)
  - Install the two adapter cables between the connections shown in the table.

 | Rear of prober (black connector)  | WSRF bridge beam (white connector) |
 | CN644  | Rear clamp / rear unclamp |
 | CN645  | Front clamp / front unclamp |

-  Locate the pneumatic hose connectors on the left side of the wafer prober (when looking from the front of the prober).    ![Pneumatic hose connectors on wafer prober](<../../../../raw/151271.jpg>)

Connect the air hoses from the Bridge Beam to the pneumatic connectors according to the labeling on the bridge beam hoses:

  ![Pneumatic hoses connected](<../../../../raw/151272.jpg>)
-  Apply compressed air to the Bridge Beam by turning the air valve 180掳 counterclockwise, as shown in the figure above.

# Connecting force and sense Lines

The sense lines are used to compensate the voltage drop on the power lines.

For very low currents it may be possible to connect sense to force close to the connector coming from the DPS-board. This saves pins at the pogo tower and also avoids pads and copper traces which might generate leakage.

The floating GND of the VI card should be fed to the pogo tower and connected there with the common GND of the whole system. If the voltage drop inside of the pogo tower is not large, you might use one common GND sense and connect it with all the GND senses of the VI channels. Again this saves pogo pins.

## Routing sense lines

For the routing of sense lines follow these rules:

- Route sense lines as traces.
- Connect sense lines to force +/- as close to the DUT as possible.

  - Connect sense lines at DUT supply pins, or
  - Connect them at the closest bypass capacitor

- Treat sense lines as critical signal paths.

  - Separate them from other traces to avoid feeding noise back to the DPS voltage source.

# Measuring and adjusting the planarity of a probe card assembly

This topic describes a method of measuring and adjusting the Datum A planarity of the Direct Probe probe card per specification drawing.

## About this task

In most cases, the method implements the use of readily available mechanical measurement tools.

Note The drawings in this guide serve as conceptual descriptions only and may differ from the latest original design drawings. For the original drawings see [Direct-Probe probe card layout information](<02.03.12.01.01_Direct-Probe_probe_card_layout_information.md>) and [Direct-Probe stiffener](<02.03.12.01.02_Direct-Probe_stiffener.md>).

All subsequent planarity adjustments to the probe card assembly, such as adjusting the probe head planarity, should be made in reference to datum A (4x contact tabs) of the probe card assembly.

Measurement and adjustment of the probe card assembly is recommended when any potential mechanical variation is possible, such as replacing the PCB, shipping, etc.

In this topic the following tools are used.

Granite table:

- Minimum working area: 457.2 mm x 304.8 mm
- Inspection grade +/- 1 mil (0.00254 mm) unilateral tolerance

Rectangular gauge block:

- Minimum quantity: 4
- Grade ASME 0
- NIST traceable

Thickness gauge:

- Minimum thickness: 1 mil (0.0254 mm)

## Before you begin

Review the specification drawing, specifically the datum A planarity requirement of the contact tabs on the stiffener frame, see [Direct-Probe probe card layout information](<02.03.12.01.01_Direct-Probe_probe_card_layout_information.md>) and [Direct-Probe stiffener](<02.03.12.01.02_Direct-Probe_stiffener.md>).

Verify that the probe card is assembled to the stiffener frame. The probe card assembly may experience static deflection when the probe card is attached to the stiffener frame, thus the requirement to measure and adjust the datum A planarity of the probe-card assembly after the PCB has been attached.

## Procedure

-  Measure the planarity of a probe card assembly:

  -  On the granite table, adjust the gauge blocks.

If available use a gauge block fixture, see note above.

  ![Direct-Probe鈩?measurement tools](<../../../../raw/123501.png>)  Note Advantest recommends using a fixture plate to hold the gauge blocks in place, see drawings for N2360-21605 in [Direct-Probe probe card layout information](<02.03.12.01.01_Direct-Probe_probe_card_layout_information.md>) and [Direct-Probe stiffener](<02.03.12.01.02_Direct-Probe_stiffener.md>).
  -  Place the probe card/stiffener assembly onto the gauge blocks so that the contact tabs on the stiffener frame rests with its reference surface on the gauge blocks.   ![Direct-Probe鈩?placing the probe card](<../../../../raw/123503.png>)   ![Direct-Probe鈩?cam follower with reference surface](<../../../../raw/123483.png>)
  -  Measure the gap, if any between the contact tab and the gauge block at all positions using the thickness gauge.   ![Direct-Probe鈩?measuring planarity](<../../../../raw/123505.png>)
  -  If adjustment is required, proceed as described below.

-  Adjust the probe card datum A planarity:

  -  Note the gap measurement result using the thickness gauge.
  -  Remove the appropriate contact tab from the stiffener frame.
  -  Add round shims between the stiffener frame and the contact tab as shown.

Thickness of the round shim will depend on the gap measurement.

  -  Remount the contact tab onto the stiffener frame.
  -  Re-measure the datum A planarity to verify.

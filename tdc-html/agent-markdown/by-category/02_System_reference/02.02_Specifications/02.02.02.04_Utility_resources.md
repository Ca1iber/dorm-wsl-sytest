# Utility resources

#### Subtopics

- [Utility lines (V93000 systems)](<02.02.02.04.01_Utility_lines__V93000_systems.md>)
- [Utility power supplies (V93000 systems)](<02.02.02.04.02_Utility_power_supplies__V93000_systems.md>)

Utility lines of V93000 Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems allow you to control external devices such as relays, solenoids or indicators, depending on the application. The lines are implemented as FET switch outputs.

The utility power supplies of V93000 Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems support DUT boards with additional active components. Typical examples for these active components are relays, switches, operational amplifiers, and buffers.

## Overview

 These utility resources are available on V93000 Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems:

- [Utility lines (V93000 systems)](<02.02.02.04.01_Utility_lines__V93000_systems.md>):

Up to 256 utility lines of V93000 Pin Scale and Smart Scale are provided by active or passive wiring boards and routed to up to eight 2-row or 4-row utility pogo blocks.

- [Internal utility power supply](<02.02.02.04.02_Utility_power_supplies__V93000_systems.md#_346945__s_Internal_utility_supply_V93000_systems>):

The +5 V supply is routed to the UP5V pins of the utility pogo blocks.

- [External utility power supply](<02.02.02.04.02_Utility_power_supplies__V93000_systems.md#_346945__s_External_utility_power_supply_V93000_systems>):

The optional external utility power supply is routed to the XP... pins of the utility pogo blocks. It provides +5 V, -5 V and +3.3 V.

- [Internal Utility Power Supply (SE-UDPS)](<02.02.02.04.02_Utility_power_supplies__V93000_systems.md#_346945__s_SE-UDPS_V93000_systems>):

The optional internal supply is routed to a DPS-slot pogo block. The supply provides +5 V, +12 V, +15 V and -15 V.

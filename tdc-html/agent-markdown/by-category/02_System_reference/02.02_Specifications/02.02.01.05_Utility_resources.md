# Utility resources

#### Subtopics

- [Internal utility resources](<02.02.01.05.01_Internal_utility_resources.md>)
- [Utility Card (EK051 and EK051A)](<02.02.01.05.02_Utility_Card__EK051_and_EK051A.md>)

Utility resources are the utility power supplies and the utility lines that you can use to supply and control additional hardware on the DUT board.

Utility lines of V93000 EXA Scale systems allow you to control external devices such as relays, solenoids or indicators, depending on the application. The lines are implemented as FET switch outputs.

The utility power supplies of V93000 EXA Scale systems support DUT boards with additional active components. Typical examples for these active components are relays, switches, operational amplifiers, and buffers.

## Overview

 These utility resources are available on V93000 EXA Scale systems:

- Utility lines:

  - [32 statically controlled utility lines](<02.02.01.05.01_Internal_utility_resources.md#_347659__s_Utility_lines>) of the first card gage are provided in the basic configuration. Optionally, 32 more lines can be installed in the second card cage of the SX and LX test heads.
  - The optional [Utility Card](<02.02.01.05.02_Utility_Card__EK051_and_EK051A.md#_347661__s_Utility_lines>) replaces the internal utility lines of the basic configuration with 256 dynamically controlled utility lines per card.

- Utility power supplies

  - +5 V supply on UP5V pins:

    - In the basic configuration, one [+5 V supply on UP5V pins](<02.02.01.05.01_Internal_utility_resources.md#_347659__dle_Utility_power_supply_UP5V>) of the first card gage is provided as internal utility resource. An additional optional +5 V supply can be installed in the second card cage of the SX and LX test heads.
    - The optional [Utility Card](<02.02.01.05.02_Utility_Card__EK051_and_EK051A.md#_347661__dle_Utility_power_supply_UP5V>) replaces the internal supplies per card with a +5 V supply that has a higher current capability.

  - [Utility power supply - XP pins](<02.02.01.05.02_Utility_Card__EK051_and_EK051A.md#_347661__dle_Utility_power_supply_XP_pins>):

The supply on the XP pins of utility pogo blocks is available per optional [Utility Card](<../02.05_Test_Head_Cards/02.05.08.04_Utility_Card.md>) and replaces the legacy [External utility power supply](<../02.05_Test_Head_Cards/02.05.08.01_External_utility_power_supply.md>).

  - [Utility power supply - UPP15V / UPM15V / F24V pins](<02.02.01.05.02_Utility_Card__EK051_and_EK051A.md#_347661__dle_Utility_power_supply_UPP15V_UPM15V_F24V_pins>):

The supply provides +15 V, -15 V and two floating 24 V outputs. The supply is routed to selected utility pogo blocks and is available per optional [Utility Card](<../02.05_Test_Head_Cards/02.05.08.04_Utility_Card.md>).

  - [Internal Utility Power Option](<02.02.01.05.01_Internal_utility_resources.md#_347659__dle_Internal_Utility_Power_Option>):

This supply replaces the legacy [Internal Utility Power Supply (SE-UDPS)](<02.02.02.04.02_Utility_power_supplies__V93000_systems.md#_346945__s_SE-UDPS_V93000_systems>). This internal utility resource is routed to a DPS-slot pogo block. Since an optional second Utility Card occupies the required DPS-slot pogo block, the Internal Utility Power Option is available only in the basic configuration or with maximum one optional Utility Card installed.

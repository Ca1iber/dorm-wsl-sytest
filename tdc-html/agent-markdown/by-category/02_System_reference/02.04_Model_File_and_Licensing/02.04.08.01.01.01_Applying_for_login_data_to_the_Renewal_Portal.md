# Applying for login data to the Renewal Portal

You must have an Advantest customer account to access the Advantest License Renewal Portal. In addition, your Advantest customer account must be given permissions for the Renewal Portal. If your login does not work, please contact your Advantest representative, providing the Advantest customer account (user name only - no password) and the error message you received from the Renewal Portal.

## About this task

To be set up within the Renewal Portal, you must contact your Advantest account representative. The account representative is your contact person for all questions concerning the setup of your user accounts.

## Before you begin

Advantest recommends the following approach for user accounts:

- If your company consists of several legal entities, have one general user account and have all entitlements assigned or co-assigned to this account. This user account enables you to have an overview of the overall license renewal progress across all legal entities.
- Have one or more separate user accounts for the actual license renewal. This is helpful, for example, if you want dedicated site users to manage and renew their own entitlements.
- Avoid having more user accounts than required. Every additional account is a potential security vulnerability.CAUTION Inform your Advantest account representative when users no longer need access, for example, because they leave your company. In this case Advantest will disable the user account.

## Procedure

-  To apply for a user account for the Renewal Portal, send the following data to your Advantest account representative:

  -  Advantest customer account name (user name)
  -  First and last names
  -  Legal entities with which you are associated. If you are authorized to act for several legal entities worldwide, please provide the names of all legal entities.

-  When contacting the Advantest account representative, you must provide a valid company email address that belongs to a registered Advantest customer. Private email addresses are not accepted.

## Results

The Advantest account representative will handle or forward your request as appropriate.

After logging in to the Advantest Licensing Renewal Portal, you can access the User profile page by clicking your user name at the upper-right of the License Renewal Portal. The following information about your Advantest License Renewal account is displayed:

- User name
- E-Mail address associated with your Advantest customer account name
- Roles assigned to you and their associated organizations (legal entities)

## What to do next

After you receive notification that you have given access to the License Renewal Portal via email from license.management@advantest.com, login and start with assigning entitlement IDs to your user account.

# Ordering licenses and available license options

## When does the licensing period begin?

- The licensing period begins when you activate your licenses: not when you purchase your licenses.
- Note that you can activate your licenses at any time you choose. You can activate multiple licenses at the same or different times, as you choose.
- Note that the license expiration date of all permanent, annual renewable licenses is always 31 January of the following year. You can activate subsequent licenses on 1 January, or any date after 1 January, of that following year.

## What kind of licenses are available for channel cards?

- Currently, only permanent, annual renewable licenses are available for channel cards. While licensing rights are purchased on a permanent basis, you must renew your licenses annually. All permanent licenses are delivered with an expiration date of 31, January of the following year.

  - During the month of January of the year after your first license activation, you will need to renew your licenses. You will need to follow the license renewal process on 1 January, or any day after 1 January, of each subsequent year.
  - Since all licenses expire at the same time, regardless of when they were purchased and activated, you can renew all licenses acquired in different purchase orders during the same web session.
  - Your entitlement certificate from each purchase is necessary to access the annual license renewal process. If you lose or misplace an entitlement certificate, contact your Advantest sales representative for a replacement copy.

- Licensing is currently available on a per pin basis. If you want to upgrade all pins on a board, you will need to purchase 32 licenses for a particular feature.
- The scalable hardware features currently available for Pin Scale cards are speed, memory, timing bundles (waveforms, drive and receive edges), ppTIA, and differential receiver. Contact your Advantest sales representative for updates about available features.
- The scalable hardware features currently available for HSM cards are speed, memory, timing bundles (waveforms, drive and receive edges), and differential receiver. Contact your Advantest sales representative for updates about available features.

## Is there a way for me to expedite my license purchase?

- No. But once you have purchased licenses, the license activation process, which is performed online, is almost immediate (note that once you have received your license entitlement certificate, you can activate your licenses at any time you choose).

## How can I find out about all the factors I may need to consider when selecting licenses?

## How can I find out what licensed features are currently available to me?

- Contact your Advantest sales representative.

## What is the difference between floating and node-locked licenses?

- A floating license requires you to have one or more license servers. The license server enables the sharing of licenses among all test systems which have a network connection to it, one use at a time per license.
- Other SmarTest software applications make use of both floating and node-locked licenses. A node-locked license can only be used on the single test system to which it has been locked.

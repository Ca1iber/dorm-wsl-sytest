# Switching to high resolution mode to view several cycles

If you want to view several cycles in detail, but they do not all fit into the display at the same time, you can switch the mode of the entire Timing Diagram to High Resolution.

- Scroll to the beginning of the area that you want to examine.
- On the menu click Select > high res.

You can now scroll through the Timing Diagram as usual, but when you stop scrolling, the Timing Diagram automatically samples the cycles shown in the display - to give you a high resolution illustration.

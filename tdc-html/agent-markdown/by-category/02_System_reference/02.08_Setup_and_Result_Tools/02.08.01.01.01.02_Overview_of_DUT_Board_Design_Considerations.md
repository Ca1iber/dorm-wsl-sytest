# Overview of DUT Board Design Considerations

The previous sections explained the overall issues regarding the available resources, DUT requirements and their interdependencies.

The result of what you have accomplished in the previous steps provides you with a physical allocation plan of the pogo-pins in relation to the established tester resources.

Your next step is to deal with the DUT specific pin requirements in terms of signal type, power needs and their physical location in relationship to the pogo-pins. Equally, you need to consider in relation to the pogo-pin resource allocations:

- The physical location of your DUT
- The size chosen for the DUT board

Any custom test-circuit components you may wish to locate on the DUT board

## Functional changes

- Introduced before SmarTest 6.3.2

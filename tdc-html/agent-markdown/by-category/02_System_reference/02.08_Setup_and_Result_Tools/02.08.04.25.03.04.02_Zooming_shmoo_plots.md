# Zooming shmoo plots

There are three manners for you to zoom in and zoom out either a whole shmoo plot or [a selected area of the plot](<02.08.04.25.03.04.03_Viewing_and_magnifying_a_selected_area_of_a_shmoo_plot.md>):

- In the toolbar of SmarTest: Click the ![Zoom in icon](<../../../../raw/136469.png>) to zoom in. Click the ![Zoom out button](<../../../../raw/136470.png>) to zoom out.
- In the menu bar of SmarTest: Click Shmoo > ZoomIn or ZoomOut.
- Right-click a shmoo plot and select:

  - Zoom In
  - Zoom Out
  - Full Size: Displays the whole shmoo plot from (x-start, y-start) to (x-end, y-end).

 Note When you zoom in and zoom out, the scroll bars around the shmoo plot can help you to view.

HX encapsulation

Programming HX card pins

Because two or four HSM3600 channels are multiplexed to one HX card channel the HSM3600 Pins must be mapped to the HSM HX pins.

The X-modes for the HX channels can be up to x32 (x8 mode for the HSM3600 channels times mux-4 mode). Therefore it is necessary to define this information in the X-Mode definition of the device access file.

Parameter: bin_parity

Some handlers use additionally to the bin information a parity signal. The parity signal may be defined for either all sites (global_bin_parity_line) or a separate parity signal may be defined for each site (per_site_bin_parity_line). The parity rule the parity lines follow may be defined as either odd or even. This configurable value defines the bin parity used and may be either "odd", "even" or "none". If "none" is defined no parity lines are set and any defined parity lines are ignored.

Driver default: none

Changeable at run-time: yes

Applicable for: handlers

Example: bin_parity: "even"

9.5" and 12" wafer prober interfaces (WPI)

Subtopics

  1. WPI overview and docking
  2. WPI pogo tower elements
  3. 9.5 inch and 12 inch WPI generic DUT boards
  4. 12" Port Scale RF wafer prober interface

The wafer prober interfaces (WPI) consist of a DUT board, a DUT board stiffener, and a pogo tower. Several high-performance WPIs are available for connecting the V93000 to a wafer prober.

Supported V93000 systems : Pin Scale, Smart Scale
Not supported in EXA Scale systems.

The WPI DUT board connects the pogo pins of the test head to the pogo pins of the pogo tower and in doing so, maps the rectangular pogo pin layout of the test head to the circular contact layout of the pogo tower and probe card. Generic WPI DUT boards provided by Advantest have an EEPROM which identifies the board.

Customized WPI DUT boards are sometimes required for specific applications. For example, a customized board may use a different test system channel-to-pogo-pin mapping, connect several pins together, provide relays, or provide filter circuits. A customized WPI DUT board may require a nonstandard stiffener. Advantest offers consulting services and stiffener design information to third party stiffener manufacturers.

PVI8 wiring of guard

"Guarding" is a technique to minimize parasitic leakage currents of the signal path to the DUT. A guard voltage of the same potential as on the signal path is applied to parallel traces. This will avoid leakage currents flowing to DUT board ground or other traces.

For guarding of a PVI8 channel, the high force line (HF#) and the high sense line (HS#) are shielded by parallel traces connected to the GUARD# pogo pin of the channel.

Some design rules for guarding PVI8 channels:
  • We recommend guarding all lines that are designated for low current measurements.
  • Both the force lines (HF#) and the sense lines (HS#) should be guarded.
  • Relays should also be implemented for the guard lines if the guarded force and sense lines are switched on the DUT board.

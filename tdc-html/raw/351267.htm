Voltage clamp

The voltage clamp is available in current force mode only.

Voltage clamp
Voltage clamp range -1.5 V to +6.5 V at |VGS| ≤ 100 mV
Clamp resolution 1 mV
Clamp accuracy, verified with a measurement current of 900 µA ± 100 mV at VGS = 0 mV

To avoid clipping effects, the clamp must be specified with a sufficient distance to the expected measurement result. In symbolic notation, |clamp| > max(|expected measurement result range|) + |clamp accuracy| + |result accuracy|.

Parameter: test_start_polarity, end_of_test_polarity, and bin_data_polarity

These parameters define whether the communication signals (the DUT signal (test start), the binning information, and the end-of-test signal) between test system and handler are active low ("negative"), or active high ("positive).

Driver default: depends on plug-in

Changeable at run-time: yes

Applicable for: handlers and probers

Examples: test_start_polarity: "negative"

end_of_test_polarity: "negative"

bin_data_polarity: "negative"

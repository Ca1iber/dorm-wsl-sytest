Configuring the Error Map recording

The internal detailed Error Map is rather small. It can hold information about 8192 cycles at most.

Therefore, it will often not be possible to record detailed results for the complete test. In order to get the required data in such a case, you will have to configure

  • the termination mode of the sequencers, and
  • the recording parameters of the Error Maps

in an appropriate way.

The overview Error Map compresses multiples of 16 cycles into one result. The compression factor can be selected by the user. In this way you can always get an overview of the complete test.

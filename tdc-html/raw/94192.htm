DPS32 and VI32 pogo pin assignment

The DC Scale DPS32 and VI32 power supplies are part of the DC Scale family. Both cards provide 32 channels and can reside in any I/O channel card slot. Each of the 32 channels can source and sink current at a programmed voltage, and each channel can measure the voltages and currents supplied.

Supported V93000 systems : Smart Scale (SmarTest 7), Pin Scale, HSM

The table below shows the pogo pin information for the DC Scale DPS32 and VI32 cards. The following names are used in the table:

  • Px: Power x
  • PSx: Power Sense x
  • GND: Ground (all grounds are connected at the relays board)
  • GSx: Ground Sense (separate line for each channel)

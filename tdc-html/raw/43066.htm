Topic 43066

Orientation of the Wafer

FLAT describes the orientation of the flat edge of the wafer. Valid values (0 to 359°, 1° resolution) for FLAT are stored and can be used by a prober file. However, the display of the wafer geometry is limited to four values.

316-45°

flat at bottom

46-135°

flat to the left

136-225°

flat at top

226-315°

flat to the right

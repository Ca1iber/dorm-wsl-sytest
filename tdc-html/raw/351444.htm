PS5000 pogo cable assembly EK601-61602: 16 SD pogo blocks

The EK601-61602 pogo cable assembly connects the 256 channels of a PS5000 card via 16 standard (standard-density / SD) pogo blocks to the DUT board.

Basic data and pogo block

Overall length: 585 mm
Pogo block style: 4-row standard (standard-density / SD) pogo block
Pogo blocks: 16
Channels per pogo block: 16
Total channels: 256

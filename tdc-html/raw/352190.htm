PS5000 ground sensing

Pin Scale 5000 channels support remote sensing of the ground (DGND) connections via separate pins. Use the ground sense connections to the DUT to compensate the voltage drop on the return path both for force and measure operations. The ground sense pins are shared by 16 channels on the SD and HD pogo cable assembly and are shared by 32 channels on the VHD pogo cable assembly.

Ground sense to channel assignment

The pogo cable assemblies for Pin Scale 5000 feature one DGND sense pin per SD pogo block and two DGND sense pins for each HD and VHD pogo block. The ground sense pins are therefore shared by 16 channels on the SD and HD pogo cable assemblies, and are shared by 32 channels on the VHD pogo cable assembly.

Ground sense pin to channel assignment:
  • SD pogo block:
    • "DGNDS‑A" is assigned to channels A1 … A16.
  • HD pogo block:
    • "DGNDS‑A" is assigned to channels A1 … A16.
    • "DGNDS‑B" is assigned to channels B1 … B16.
  • VHD pogo block:
    • "DGNDS‑AB" is assigned to channels A1 … A16 and B1 … B16.
    • "DGNDS‑CD" is assigned to channels C1 … C16 and D1 … D16.

Characteristics of the ground sense pins

The following characteristics of the ground sense pins are to be considered when designing a DUT board:
  • Ground sense voltage range:
    • |VGS| ≤ 500 mV, for |VGS| > 100 mV voltage ranges specified have to be reduced accordingly

    Refer also to PS5000 ground sense input of the PS5000 specifications.

  • By default, the ground reference of the Pin Scale 5000 channels is connected to an internal ground of the card. In order to achieve the best possible accuracy, remote sensing via the ground sense pins must be enabled by SmarTest. See also section Defining ground sense in SmarTest below.
  • The ground sense pogo pins of two neighboring logical boards are connected to each other via a 1 kΩ resistor on the 32-channel modules of the Pin Scale 5000 card. This internal connection provides the ground sense connection for the two additional logical boards on a VHD pogo block.
  • The 1 kΩ resistor must be considered for the SD pogo cable assembly and for the HD pogo cable assembly: For higher voltage differences between the ground sense pins of the two logical boards, a small current will flow through the resistor and the ground sense lines.

Recommendations for wiring of the ground sense pins

We recommend that you observe the following rules:
  • DUT board designs with ground sensing are required for the Pin Scale 5000. This must be taken into account in order to achieve the best possible accuracy.
  • We recommend using a ground sense design for all new PS1600 DUT boards if these boards are to be used with Pin Scale 5000 later. PS1600 will ignore the ground sense connections.
  • The type of DUT board (with or without ground sense) used needs to be reflected in the ground sense definition of your test program. For more information, see section Defining ground sense in SmarTest below.
  • The type of connection depends on whether the "DGNDS-..." pin is available per site or shared by several sites:
    • "DGNDS-..." pin is available per site:
      • For best result, use a separate probe or the sense connection of a Kelvin socket to connect the pin to the associated DUT ground pad or pin.
    • "DGNDS-..." shared by several sites:
      • Connect to the associated ground plane close to the DUTs.
  • Do not insert additional series resistors into the ground sense connections of the DUT board.
  • Compatibility of the SD and VHD pogo block:
    If a DUT board is to be used on both systems with the HD pogo cable assembly and systems with the VHD pogo cable assembly, you not only have to provide landing pads for the additional rows of the VHD pogo block, but also use the assignment of the ground sense pins for the VHD pogo block:
    • Although the HD pogo block provides a ground sense pin per logical board, you must use only the ground sense pin "DGNDS‑A" for both pins A1 … A16 and B1 … B16 on the DUT board.
    • Do not connect "DGNDS‑B". Connect "DGNDS‑B" only on DUT boards that are designed to access all pins of a VHD pogo block.
    • When connecting via a HD or VHD block, pins B1 … B16 are the always connected to the same ground sense pin "DGNDS‑A" via the internal 1 kΩ resistor of the channel module.

Defining ground sense in SmarTest

For information on how to enable remote sensing of the common ground, refer to section Ground sensing on PS5000 in topic PS5000 remote sensing of the test head card description.

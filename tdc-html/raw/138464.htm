Direct-Probe probe card layout information

Below you find the Direct-Probe™ card layout related drawings when using a semi-automatic card changer (SACC). They provide the specs and dimensions required for your design tasks.

For non-RF applications, we recommend using the Dedicated non RF probe card listed below. For full-capability and RF applications (Port Scale RF (PSRF) only) use the Universal and RF probe card (PSRF only).

In any case you need to chose one KOZ (keep-out zone) and one FF (form factor) drawing set.

General Purpose DPS (GP-DPS) pogo pin assignments

One general purpose DPS (GP-DPS) can reside inside each card cage. A general purpose DPS contains four general purpose DPS channels which share a common return path. The common return path is often referred to as "ground".

Supported V93000 systems : Smart Scale (SmarTest 7), Pin Scale

Each channel has a Force+, Sense+, Force-, Sense-, and guard line. Note that "Force-" is the line which is also frequently labelled "ground". The Force- lines for all four channels are connected together inside the test head. The Force- lines from different DPS cards are not tied together inside the test head. Most signals on the DPS have two names, as shown in the following list. (Please familiarize yourself with both names.)

  • G: ground pin (also known as Force-)
  • GRD: guard pin
  • GS: ground sense pin (also known as Sense-)
  • P: power pin (also known as Force+)
  • PS: power sense pin (also known as Sense+)
    Note The GP-DPS channels are all bipolar: the output voltage may be positive or negative. If the DPS is operated with a negative polarity, this means that "Force+" will be held at a negative voltage with respect to "Force-".

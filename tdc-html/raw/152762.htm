Pogo pin assignment - DPS-slot device power supplies

Subtopics

  1. General Purpose DPS (GP-DPS) pogo pin assignments
  2. High Current DPS (HC-DPS) pogo pin assignments
  3. High Voltage DPS (HV-DPS) pogo pin assignments
  4. Multisite DPS (MS-DPS) pogo pin assignments

The DPS-slot device power supplies contain independently programmable device power supply (DPS) channels. The DPS-slot device power supplies can reside in the DPS-slot (that is the ninth slot beside the DC/DC cards) of any card cage of the V93000 Smart Scale and Pin Scale systems.

Supported V93000 system generations and variants

This section holds the pogo pin layouts for all V93000 system generations and variants. For the supported set of test head cards on your system, refer to Pogo pin assignment.

V93000 Smart Scale and Pin Scale systems

The pogo block of a DPS-slot device power supply is installed at one of the DPS pogo block positions on the DUT interface. There can be up to eight such positions.

These DPS-slot device power supplies reside in DPS slots:

  • General Purpose DPS
  • High Voltage DPS
  • High Current DPS
  • Multisite DPS
  • Internal Utility Power Supply (SE-UDPS)

    The SE-UDPS can reside in the DPS-slot of any card cage. The three-row pogo block of supply is installed at one of the DPS pogo block positions on the DUT interface.

    For more information, refer to Internal Utility Power Supply (SE-UDPS) pogo pin assignments.

Functional changes

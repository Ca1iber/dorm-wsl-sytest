Internal utility power supplies

In the basic configuration of a V93000 EXA Scale system, one +5 V supply on UP5V pins is provided as internal utility resource. One more optional +5 V supply can be installed in the second card cage of the SX and LX test heads. The additional Internal Utility Power Option replaces the legacy Internal Utility Power Supply (SE-UDPS).

Internal Utility Power Option

The Internal Utility Power Option can be routed to a 3-row DPS-slot pogo block if maximum one optional Utility card (EK051 or EK051A) is installed. The supply provides compatibility with the legacy SE-UDPS and requires the EK051UP pogo cable assembly.

Topic 343907

Deinstalling Direct-Probe bridge beam from Accretech UF3000 EX wafer prober

About this task

This topic tells you how to remove a bridge beam installed on the Accretech UF3000 EX Wafer Prober.

Before you begin

Note Deinstallation must only be performed by trained personnel experienced with the operation and maintenance of Accretech UF3000EX series probers.

Procedure

  1. Remove bridge beam sensor wiring/air tubing covers from the prober head-stage.
  2. Disconnect the bridge beam sensor cables at the connection point closest to the bridge beam inside the head-plate cable groove. The cables are marked R-CLP, R-UNCLP, L-CLP, and L-UNCLP.
  3. Remove the sensor cables and air hoses from the cable clamp.
  4. On the side of the prober, remove the cover over the compressed air valves.
  5. Shut off compressed air to the bridge beam by turning the air valve 180° clockwise as shown in the figure.
  6. Disonnect the air hoses from the Bridge Beam to the pneumatic connectors as shown in the figure below:
  7. Remove the bridge beam mounting screws.

    Use an 8 mm Allen wrench.

  8. Lift off the bridge beam from the prober head-plate. It is recommended that the removal of the bridge beam is performed by two persons.

Results

You have de-installed a bridge beam from the Accretech UF3000 EX wafer prober.

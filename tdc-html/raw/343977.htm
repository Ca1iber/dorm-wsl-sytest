Installation verification - Phase 2

Subtopics

  1. Verification Phase 2 - Stage 1
  2. Verification Phase 2 - Stage 2
  3. Verification Phase 2 - Stage 3

By following the instructions in this section you verify the proper operation of the wafer prober following the completion of phase 2 of the installation.

About this task

The figure shows the stages making up Phase 2 of the installation verification process for the bridge beam.

Before you begin

  1. Installation of the V93000 Direct Probe Bridge Beam on the prober must be completed.
  2. Adjustment of the Automated Probe Card Changer (APCC) tray must be complete per V93000 Direct Probe requirements (see V93000 Direct Probe Bridge Beam Theory of Operation for details). The APCC tray carries the load board stiffener.
Tools:
  • High CAM Follower Stiffener Fixture (high stiffener)
  • Low CAM Follower Stiffener Fixture (low stiffener)
  • Tension gauge
  • Tester Interlock Emulator
  • H7-8T0051 Direct Probe VPG Gauge

What to do next

Go to Verification Phase 2 - Stage 1.

Functional changes

  1. Introduced in SmarTest 7.4.3
  2. SmarTest 7.5.1: Obsolete VPG gauge E5210B has been replaced with H7-8T0051.

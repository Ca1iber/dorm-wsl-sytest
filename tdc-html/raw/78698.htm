Defining the Scan Padding Type

The SCAN_TYPE statement defines how the drive scan vectors are padded if the template length is not a multiple of the required x-mode factor. It must follow the FORMAT statement immediately. The possible options are

  • STANDARD: The drive scan vectors are padded at the start.
  • BS_INTEST: The drive scan vectors are padded at the end.

For details see ASCII Vector Padding for Scan Vectors.

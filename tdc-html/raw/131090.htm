Minimum sequencer period (base rate) for digital cards

Digital cards

The minimum sequencer period (base rate) depends on the digital card (hardware), the available speed license, and the number of actions (edges) used in a waveform. The tables below show the minimum sequencer period for individual digital cards. Read the tables as follows:

X.XX ns

Minimum sequencer period (base rate) in nanoseconds

 

Limited by hardware (HW)

 

Limited by software (SW)

For example, on the Pin Scale 1600 card with a 533 Mbps speed license and 3 actions (edges) per waveform, your minimum sequencer period is 5.628 ns.

For details on the setting of the sequencer period and the minimum period restrictions of DPS128/DPS64 cards, see Minimum period / maximum frequency of the sequencer, Defining the period (for fixed timing), and Defining the global period (equation based timing).

Data rate licensing

While drive actions (edges) are licensed on the PS9G card, receive actions (edges) are "free" on PS1600 cards starting with SmarTest 7.1.3.

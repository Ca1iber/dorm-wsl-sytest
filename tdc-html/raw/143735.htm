PVI8 stacked wiring

In order to achieve a higher output voltage, serial connection of multiple PVI8 channels is allowed. This is called "stacking". Some specific electrical design rules must be considered for stacked PVI8 channels.

WARNING Stacking is allowed for two channels or groups of ganged channels and up to a maximum output voltage of ±160 V with respect to the DUT board ground.

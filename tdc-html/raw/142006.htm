Enhancements of the MTP_MEMORY_PATTERN API

There are several enhancements of the MTP_MEMORY_PATTERN_API.

These enhancements are:

  • Adds new member functions: getDeviceSize(), getFailingCycleAddressList(), getPassFailResult(), judgeAndLog(), and print().
  • The member function setBitMapFileName() is enhanced to support multiport.
  • Adds new syntaxes to the getFailBitCount() member function.
  • Adds new syntaxes to the getFailingAddressList() member function.

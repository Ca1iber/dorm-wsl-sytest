Termination mode

The Pin Scale cards can be configured to have a variety of terminations. This is done by entering keywords into the level setup editor window (or other keywords directly into the level setup file).

The termination options differ for the various Pin Scale cards.

Note
  • The selection of available terminations depends on the card and its operation mode.
  • The available modes are displayed in the Level Setup window when you click in the termination mode column.
  • You can see and change the termination mode used for your Pin Scale card from the Hardware view.

MUX cards design data

Below you find the MUX card related drawings. They provide the design rules, specifications, and dimensions required for your design tasks.

Design data for the standard solution:

  • DUT board

    DUT board for the standard MUXSTIF01 stiffener.

  • Stiffener (MUXSTIF01)

    Design data for the MUXSTIF01 stiffener.

  • Interposer (MUXIP01)

    Design data for the MUX cards interposer (MUXIP01). This interposer is also required for the other variants of the DUT board.

Other variants of the stiffener and DUT board:

  • Direct-Probe PMUX design kit

    Design data for the Direct-Probe specific stiffener and DUT board.

  • Wide Opening PMUX design kit for final test

    Design data for the wide opening stiffener and DUT board which provide additional space in the central DUT board area.

  • Wing Stiffener PMUX design kit

    Design data for the wing stiffener and DUT board which provide additional space on the left and right side of the DUT board.

The interposer (MUXIP01) is also required for these variants of the stiffener and DUT board.

Restrictions for DUT board docking on MUX card and WSRF test heads

Some restrictions are to be respected for the combination of MUX cards and Wave Scale RF (WSRF) cards:

  • WSRF DUT board docked on a test head with installed MUX cards

    A WSRF DUT board cannot be docked on a test head with installed MUX cards. There is a mechanical protection if a WSRF DUT board will be put on a PMUX Tester. The WSRF stiffener has two feet that will touch the guide pins of the MUX cards frame before the board is completely docked.

  • DUT board with MUX Cards Stiffener docked on a test head with WSRF cards installed

    The MUX cards DUT board can be docked on a WSRF test head.

General note for DUT boards on test heads with installed MUX cards

All DUT boards with a component height less than 12 mm in the area of MUX cards have no collision with installed MUX cards. See also the keep-out zone (KOZ) drawing E8021-96570.

General note for DUT boards with WSRF cards installed

All DUT boards with a component height less than 20 mm in the area of the WSRF hardware have no collision with the WSRF hardware in the Tester. See also the KOZ drawings N2605-96550 (PCB layout for final test) and N2605-96570 (PCB layout for Direct-Probe™).

Note Wave Scale RF cards are supported only in SmarTest 8.0.2 or higher versions.

Topic 96732

Inserting and Deleting Sequencer Instructions

Subtopics

  1. Inserting a sequencer instruction
  2. Deleting a sequencer instruction

Most sequencer instructions can be inserted into or deleted from a sequencer program in a straightforward manner. But if you insert or delete a vector generating instruction, you must take care that the already existing GENV and RPTV instructions still point to the correct location of the vector areas after modifying the program.

PMU operating mode of DC Scale cards

Subtopics

  1. Hardware view diagram of DC Scale channels in PMU operating mode
  2. Measuring current or voltage in PMU operating mode
  3. Relay switching in PMU operating mode

For PMU operating mode specify the pin type as SIG. In PMU operating mode you can use DC Scale cards like a standard PMU to temporarily supply power in a single testsuite or for a group of testsuites. Each of the 32 channels can source and sink current at a programmed voltage, and each channel can measure the voltages and currents supplied.

Connect/Disconnect

Note In PMU operating mode, signal pins of a DC Scale card will not be connected during connect and not disconnected during disconnect.
Note In PMU operating mode a DC Scale card does not take part in standard connect and disconnect sequences, and cannot take part in the set activation, although you can add DC Scale cards to user-defined connect and disconnect sequences.
Note On startup SmarTest always performs a standard disconnect.

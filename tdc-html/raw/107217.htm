Topic 107217

9.5 inch power signal recommendations

For DC Scale cards like the UHC4 / UHC4T, which can be placed in any channel card slot, on the 9.5 inch pogo tower the signal pins are also used for power distribution.

Each signal pin is rated for 3 A. But to reduce the overall resistance to the power distribution through the pogo tower, the current should be shared between several pins. As guideline use an effective power rating (not peak) of 1 A per pin.

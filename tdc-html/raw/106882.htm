SmarTest is the software package provided with the V93000 Series. Product engineers, test engineers, QA engineers and service engineers use SmarTest for different purposes. In multiple test stages like wafer test, final test, and assembly test you can use SmarTest to create test programs, collect production data, perform failure analysis or system diagnostic. In engineering or in production, the target applications are multiple: Digital, Analog, Memory, PMIC, RF or combinations of those in form of SIP or SOC.

FlexDC APIs

Use Flex DC for:

  • Support of different measurements on different pins in parallel.
  • Support to force voltages, currents to a device pin.
  • Support of fast multi-site measurements with different settings on different sites.
  • Optimizing per pin value, per pin pass fail measurements.

The following commands are implemented from FlexDC and are intended to provide maximum parallelism and thus optimal multisite efficiency.

They can execute various actions on multiple pins in parallel.

  • FLEX_RELAY
  • PPMU_SETTING, PPMU_RELAY, PPMU_MEASURE
  • DPS_TASK
  • SPMU_TASK
  • SEARCH_FUNC_TASK (parallel spec search!)
  • PPTIA_TASK

Function: ph_disable_equipment

Disables the generic prober/handler driver for the specific equipment.

After the drivers of the equipment are disabled, the subsequent function calls will not be performed on the equipment until they are enabled again.

Applicable for: handlers and probers

Example: PROB_HND_CALL(ph_disable_equipment MT1 MT2)

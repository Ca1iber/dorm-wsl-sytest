AVI64 output pogo pins

Subtopics

  1. AVI64 Kelvin connection
  2. AVI64 reverse Kelvin connection
  3. AVI64 Sense only connection
  4. AVI64 Force only connection

The output pins of an AVI64 card are the channel specific P#, PS#, and GS# pin and several common GND pins. Several different connection schemes ensure that the pins are connected for optimal performance and adapted to the needs of the application.

Connection schemes

The AVI64 is a multi-functional card that covers VI source features, ranging from µA to Amperes range and on the other hand digital I/O and time measurement functionality that requires a controlled impedance environment. Different connection schemes are available to connect to the DUT with optimum performance depending on the needs of the application. These connection schemes have to be considered for the DUT board design.

  • Kelvin connection: The Kelvin connection is the standard scheme to use an AVI64 channel in DC, high precision, and high-current applications.
  • Reverse Kelvin connection: The reverse Kelvin connection is the scheme to use an AVI64 channel in high-precision DC and high-speed applications with low capacitive load.
  • Sense only connection: This connection mode is the standard scheme for the Digital I/O operating mode that requires a controlled impedance environment and low capacitance for the optimal performance. This scheme is also preferred for high impedance measurements.
  • Force only connection: This connection mode is an optional scheme to use an AVI64 channel with only the force pogo pin P# connected on the DUT board.

Pogo pin "P#"

P# is the low impedance high force output pin of an AVI64 channel. The pin is optimized for low DC resistance and high carry currents up to 4A pulsed. The pin is used if a channel is wired in the Kelvin connection scheme for high precision and high-current DC applications. The PS# pin must be connected to the high force pin P# directly at the DUT to compensate the voltage drop on the high force line.

The force connection to the shared resources floating high-current unit and high-resolution voltage AWG is supported only via the high force pin P#.

Pogo pins "GND"

The GND pins are the common ground and return path for all channels of an AVI64 card. The GND pins must be connected to a common ground plane of the DUT board. All GND pins must be connected in parallel to ensure that the specifications for the card and the maximum ratings for the pogo pins are met.

Pogo pin "PS#"

The PS# pin is intended to be the sense input connection for the Kelvin connection scheme, sensing the voltage at the DUT pin.

The pin is also used as digital input and output pin with controlled impedance and low capacitive load in the Digital I/O operating mode. The sense only connection scheme is to be used for this operating mode.

The pin must be connected to the high force pin P# directly at the DUT if the Kelvin connection scheme is used.

Pogo pin GS#

GS# is the low voltage sense pin and ground reference of an AVI64 channel for all connection schemes and operating modes.

This pin must be connected to the common ground pin (GND) or an adequate DUT pin. The maximum voltage referenced to the common GND pins must be observed for all connection schemes and operating modes for the complete testflow. Refer to the following paragraph for the maximum voltage at this pin.

Voltage sense lines

The voltage at the DUT is measured differentially between the high sense pin (PS#) and the ground sense pin (GS#). Both pins are available per AVI64 channel.

The high sense pin (PS#) must be connected to the related high force pogo pin (P#) directly at the DUT pins if the Kelvin connection scheme or the reverse Kelvin connection scheme are used. The ground sense pin (GS#) must be connected to the common ground pin (GND) or an adequate DUT pin directly at the DUT both for all connection schemes.

The voltage difference between the force pins and sense pins is typically caused by the voltage drop, produced by current flow over parasitic resistances on the DUT board. A maximum voltage difference must be maintained between the high force pin (P#) and the high sense pin (PS#) and between the ground sense pin (GS#) and the common ground. Violating this rule will result in a wrong force voltage and wrong voltage measurements.

Force to sense (FS) clamp between P# and PS#

Each AVI64 channel features a switchable clamp circuitry between the P# pogo pin and the PS# pogo pin. This clamp circuitry limits the voltage difference between the two pins for abnormal operating conditions. An abnormal operating condition is for example an open sense connection on the DUT board if the Kelvin connection scheme is used. The closed FS clamp limits the voltage increase on the force line to about 2 V in this case.

The following data must be respected for the FS clamp circuitry:

Maximum operating voltage range or P# to PS# voltage difference: ±1 V
Maximum force to sense current within the operating voltage range: ±1 µA
Typical maximum clamp current: 4.5 mA
Voltage drop @ 2 mA: 3 V

The FS clamp circuitry is automatically connected or disconnected depending on the operating mode, the connection state, the measurement mode, and the selected output connection scheme.

Pull down circuit at P#, PS# and GS#

Each of the pins P#, PS# and GS# features a circuit that pulls the pin down to ground. The circuit keeps the voltage at disconnected pins at a defined level of about 0 V referenced to the GND pin.

Note The state of the pull down circuit is always the same for P# and PS#. This means that both the pull down circuit at P# and the pull down circuit at PS# are active if the PMU is connected via a single line using the Force only connection scheme or the Sense only connection scheme.
CAUTION The active pull down circuits of a disconnected AVI64 channel may falsify a current measurement that is performed with another resource like the DC Scale FVI16 card that is connected in parallel. The disconnected AVI64 channel must be connected and set to a high impedance (HIZ) mode to deactivate the pull down circuit for the current measurement with the other resource.

Overload protection

The output pogo pins of the AVI64 channels are designed to withstand following overload conditions:

  • Voltages within the compliance range from -44 V to +84 V are tolerated on the output pogo pins P#, PS# and GS# for an unlimited time and independent of the programmed function. For more information, refer to table "Interface characteristics" in AVI64 other specifications.
  • The pins P# and PS# are protected to withstand currents applied by external sources outside the compliance range from -44 V to +84 V as follows:

    Maximum current outside the compliance range
    Absolute maximum continuous current per pogo pin:

    ±500 mA
    Absolute maximum peak current for 500 ms per pogo pin:

    ±1 A
    Absolute maximum current per AVI64 card:

    ±1 A

Additional protection circuitry is required on the DUT board if AVI64 channels are operated together with other cards that can violate the rules defined above. Other cards that may exceed the defined limits for the AVI64 channels are for example DC Scale FVI16 cards if the FVI16 channels are stacked.

For more information refer to HV (High-Voltage) protection.

  • CFVM: Force current directly and measure voltage.

  • IFVM: Force current and measure voltage (SmarTest 7 only).
  • VFIM: Force voltage and measure current.

  • VFIM_HS: Force voltage and measure current in high-speed mode.

  • VM_HIZ_COH: High-impedance voltage measure with the AVI64 VM in coherent sampling mode.

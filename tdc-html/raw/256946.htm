Un-docking the test head

Use a key combination of the remote control to un-dock the test head.

Before you begin

You have docked the test head.

Procedure

  1. To un-dock the DUT interface, simultaneously press ENABLEand DUT UNDOCK.
  2. Press HARD UNDOCK to release the grip of the docking cylinders and un-dock the test head.

Results

You have un-docked the test head.

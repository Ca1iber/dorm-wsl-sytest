Topic 30706

calAge_limit

Subtopics

  1. Associated Syntax Entities

This expression is used in conjunction with MONITOR_CALL, and enables you to set the length To be of time (in days) for which your current calibration is regarded as valid by the software, after which a warning is generated. To be of any real use, calAge_limit should also be used in conjunction with calAge_check (see above). The line,

MONITOR_CALL (calAge_limit, 80);

sets a limit of 80 days for the calibration. The calAge_limit can be from 1 to 90 days inclusive (90 days is the testers built-in calibration limit).

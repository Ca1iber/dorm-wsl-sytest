DC Scale DPS128/64 HC/HV Cookbook

The DC Scale DPS128/64 is the next generation high density channel slot device power supply focusing on high multisite and multi power-domain applications. It is part of the Smart Scale series and addresses the needs of pattern synchronized, highly efficient and massive parallel resources optimized for DC source and measurement.

As part of the Smart Scale series DC Scale DPS128/64 works together with all Smart Scale based digital cards as well as all DC Scale cards.

Defining edge delays for a pattern‑based SmartLoop test

The edges for a pattern‑based SmartLoop™ test are distributed equidistantly within the tester cycle. This holds for both input and output pins. But you can define a start delay for the first (drive or receive) edge. You must specify the number of edges per cycle with the period_div keyword. This number must correspond to the x-mode in the wavetable.

Combining pattern‑based SmartLoop™ with transition tracking

You can also combine a pattern‑based SmartLoop™ test with transition tracking on the output pins for both x4-mode and x6-mode. In both cases, you must specify the respective TTMODE statement instead of period_div in the timing set of the output pins. With TTMODE X6, the input pin must also be set to x-mode 6. But with x4-mode, a more elaborate setup is required:

  • The input pin must be set to period_div=8,
  • The two pins must be put into different ports.
  • The period of the input port must be twice as long as the period of the output port.

For details of setting up the transition tracking, see Combining transition tracking with a PRBS or pattern‑based SmartLoop test.

Examples

Example 1

EQNSET 3 "lpbk"
. . . 
TIMINGSET 1 "eqs 3 ts 1"
PINS IN1
period_div = 6
d1 = 0.5
. . .
PINS OUT1
TTMODE X6
r1 = 1.0
. . .

This example can be combined with the first example of Defining the wavetable for a pattern‑based SmartLoop test in one timing specification.

Example 2

EQNSET 1 "lpbk1"

DEFINES in
 
TIMINGSET 1 "eqs 1 ts 1"
period = 8
PINS IN1
period_div = 8
d1 = 0.5
. . .

EQNSET 2 "lpbk2"

DEFINES out

TIMINGSET 1 "eqs 2 ts 1"
period = 4

PINS OUT1
TTMODE X4
r1 = 1.0
. . .

This example can be combined with the second example of Defining the wavetable for a pattern‑based SmartLoop test in one timing specification. Note that the two pins are associated with different ports (indicated by the DEFINES statements), and that the period length of pin IN1 is twice the length of OUT1.

Functional changes

  1. Introduced in SmarTest 7.0.1
  2. SmarTest 7.1.2: Pattern‑based SmartLoop can now also be combined with transition tracking in x6-mode.

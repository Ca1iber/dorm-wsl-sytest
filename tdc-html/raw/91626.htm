PS3600 pogo assembly and channels

The Pin Scale 3600 pogo assembly consists of two 16-channel pogo blocks and a 32-channel MiniCoax inline connector. Pogo assemblies have different colors, depending on the Pin Scale 3600 digital card they are used with.

Supported V93000 systems : Pin Scale, HSM

The figures show the blue pogo assembly for the Pin Scale 3600 digital card and one of its pogo pin blocks.

The table below shows the standard pin assignments for the 16 channels of a Pin Scale 3600 pogo assembly block.

The orientation mark shown on the diagram appears as a shallow hole or depression on the pin block mounted to the test head. We recommend that you include a similar mark in the printed labeling on the DUT board.

Note The assignment of the differential signals (Ch 1+, Ch 2+, Ch 1-, Ch 2-, etc.), has historical and architectural reasons. Since the signals are not coupled on the pogo assembly, there are no signal integrity reasons for this assignment nor does it have any negative effect on signal integrity.

The table uses the following abbreviations:

  • GND: ground
  • NC: pin not connected
  • I/O: input/output

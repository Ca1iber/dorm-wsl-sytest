The DPS128 and the DPS64 cards are members of the family of DC Scale power supplies and can reside in any I/O channel card slot.

Variants

DPS128‑HC E8023CS 128 HC channels 1.0 A -2.5 V ... +7.0 V
DPS64‑HC E8023CSH 64 HC channels 1.0 A -2.5 V ... +7.0 V

See also the DPS128 / DPS64 specifications.

The memory per channel is 28 Mbit.

Supported configurations

The following test head card configurations are supported:

  • Mix of DPS128/DPS64 and Smart Scale generation channel cards like PS1600.
  • Mix of DPS128/DPS64 and UHC4T.

MTP 3.4.2

MTP 3.4.2 is released with 7.4.2 T. Two new APIs are added into the MTP_MEMORY_PATTERN class in MTP 3.4.2.

New fundamental devices for DDR4 are added and can be found in the directory /opt/93000mts/mts/device_solutions, along with devices for DDR3, GDDR3, and GDDR5.

Fundamental reading

MTP_MEMORY_PATTERN.getDQDC/DC power unitsByPinName()

MTP_MEMORY_PATTERN.getPinNameListByDQIndex()

Topic 25796

Precharging of DC Rails

The dc rail is precharged to a voltage so as to avoid hot switching of the PPMU relay when it closes. This precharge voltage can be specified for the Leakage and GeneralPMU test method.

And also, the Continuity test can force 0 V as the precharge voltage optionally before the current forcing.

Precharging prevents sudden high currents flowing through the DUT and the associated spikes on the DC Rail.

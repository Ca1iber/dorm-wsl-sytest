Topic 142663

HSM HX card

HSM HX card is a high-speed digital card located in the test head providing differential drive and receive channels at data rates of up to 12.8 Gbit/s.

  • HSM HX card specific calibration steps and diagnostic tests are described in Service Guide Part 1
  • HX interconnection board required for calibration and diagnostic is described in HSM HX card (E7996H).

Mixed-signal DUT board design

Subtopics

  1. Mechanical design for mixed-signal testing
  2. Electrical design for mixed-signal testing
  3. Crosstalk
  4. Maintaining signal quality in mixed-signal testing
  5. Troubleshooting noise problems
  6. Shielding and grounding
  7. Grounding concept for power applications

In mixed-signal testing, digital signals such as clock pulses can interfere with analog signals. To avoid deterioration of analog signal quality, physically separate digital signals from analog signals on the DUT board.

Signal quality is also affected by signal return paths (digital ground and analog ground) so that the overall grounding scheme becomes crucial. Special shielding techniques are sometimes required.

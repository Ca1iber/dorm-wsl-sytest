Internal Utility Power Option pogo pin assignments (V93000 EXA Scale systems)

V93000 EXA Scale systems provide a replacement for the legacy Internal Utility Power Supply (SE-UDPS). The Internal Utility Power Option is available for the basic configuration and with maximum one Utility Card (EK051A) installed.

Supported V93000 systems : EXA Scale (SmarTest 7+8)

The Internal Utility Power Option can be routed to a 3-row DPS slot pogo block if maximum one optional Utility Card is installed.

The functional assignment of the Internal Utility Power Option basically the same as for the legacy SE-UDPS and General Purpose DPS (GP-DPS).

Functional assignment of the Internal Utility Power Option pogo pads
Pin a b c
1 G1 NC NC

9 NC NC NC

12 G3 NC PS3
13 NC NC P3
14 NC NC NC
15 G4 NC NC
16 G4 NC PS4
17 NC NC P4

The table uses the following names:

  • G#: Ground pad
  • P#: Power pad
  • PS#: Power sense pad
  • NC: Not connected

The assignment of the channel is as follows:

  • +5.0 V: P1, PS1, G1 (two pins)
  • +12.0 V: P2, PS2, G2 (two pins)
  • +15.0 V: P3, PS3, G3 (two pins)
  • -15.0 V: P4, PS4, G4 (two pins)

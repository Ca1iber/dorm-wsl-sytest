Topic 126316

Editing pin role details

About this task

This task describes how to enter pin role and pin role group specific encode and decode functions.

Procedure

  1. Select a pin role within the structure of the protocol.
    Note If you rename the pin role here, the protocol structure will be updated accordingly. You may disable this propagation feature in the preference settings of the protocol editor.
  2. Enter the Encode function to be used on pin role level or pin role group level respectively.
  3. Enter the Decode function to be used on pin role level or pin role group level respectively.
  4. Save your modifications by pressing the CTRL + S key combination simultaneously.

Results

You have entered the encode and decode function to be applied on pin role and pin role group level.

What to do next

You may enter the required protocol field details by Editing field details.

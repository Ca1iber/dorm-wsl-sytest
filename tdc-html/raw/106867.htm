The DC Scale UHC4T is a device power supply card that addresses your needs for ultra high currents.

The DC Scale UHC4T power supply card has 4 channels per card that provide a maximum current of 40 A each (160 A per card). The ganging of channels across UHC4T cards allows even higher currents.

During the execution of a test flow the UHC4T acts like a normal device power supply (DPS). The main difference between a normal DPS and an ultra high current DPS is the need to protect the ATE equipment in case of a shortage, for example the needles on a probe card. Therefore, UHC4T card provides additionally:

  • Several alarms to protect your test equipment
  • A high-speed sampler for differential voltage monitoring
  • Measurement of voltage drop on force/return line

Cpk & Limit Calculator

To find the Cpk value that a particular set of limits would have produced in the selected test, enter the limits in the Limits section and click Calculate Cpk. The result appears in the Statistics section.

To find the limits that would have been needed in the selected test to achieve a particular Cpk value, enter the desired Cpl and Cph values in the Statistics section and click Calculate Limit. The result appears in the Limits section.

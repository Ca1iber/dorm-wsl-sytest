Pin Scale 5000 sitemap

The Pin Scale 5000 (PS5000) is the EXA Scale™ generation digital base card providing per-pin capabilities such as individual clock domain, high accuracy DC and industry-leading digital performance.

With 256 channels per card, the Pin Scale 5000 card supports higher pin count and higher multisite testing, enabling smaller infrastructure usage and cost.

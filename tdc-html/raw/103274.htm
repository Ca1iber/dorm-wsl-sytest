Topic 103274

Install the probe card stiffener in the prober manufacturer kit

About this task

This task is about mounting the probe card into the prober manufacturer kit.

Before you begin

Ensure that the probe card and the prober manufacturer kit are available.

Procedure

  1. Insert the probe card stiffener into the prober manufacturer kit (mounting ring) so that the position arrows on the probe card stiffener align with those on the mounting ring.
  2. Place the assembled pogo tower frame on top of the resulting assembly and lock it in position with the handle.

What to do next

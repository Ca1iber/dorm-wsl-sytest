Topic 108353

Opening multiple Hardware views of different pins

You can compare the settings of different pins by opening multiple Hardware views.

Before you begin

You have updated the hardware information in the Pin Browser view.

In the Hardware view, the Link with Editor and Selection button has been pressed.

Procedure

  1. To open a Hardware view for one pin, in the Pin Browser view, right-click the desired pin and select Show in Hardware. The setting of the selected pin is displayed in the Hardware view.
  2. In the Hardware view, click the Pin this View button on the top right corner. The display of this Hardware view is pinned to this pin.
  3. Then you can repeat step 1 to open the second Hardware view for another pin.

Results

Multiple Hardware views are opened. Then you can compare the settings of different pins.

Functional changes

  1. Introduced in SmarTest 6.5.0

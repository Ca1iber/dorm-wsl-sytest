E8023PB pogo cable assembly: 2 VHD pogo blocks

The E8023PB pogo cable assembly connects the DPS64 or half of the DPS128 channels via two VHD pogo blocks to the DUT board.

Pogo cable assembly E8023PB is supported by SmarTest 7.5.4.4, 8.3.4.2, and higher.

Basic data and pogo block

Overall length: 750 mm
Pogo block style: VHD, 7-row pogo block
Pogo blocks: 2
Channels per pogo block: 32
DPS128 channels per 1 channel on pogo block: 1
Total number of channels on pogo block level: 64
Max. current per channel on pogo block level: 1 A

Pogo cable assembly

Pogo pin assignment

The VHD pogo block can also connect to DUT boards that are designed for the HD pogo block as long as landing pads are provided for the extra pogo pins of the VHD block. For more information, refer to Pogo block compatibility.

Layout and dimension
See also Pogo pad and via design.
CAUTION The 7-row VHD block uses the row sequence a-e-b-f-c-g-d for compatibility with the 4-row HD block. This sequence is different to the row sequence a-b-c-d of the 4-row HD pogo block. Do not use the wrong row sequence a-b-c-d-... for the 7-row VHD block. Use only the correct row sequence a-e-b-f-c-g-d for the 7-row VHD block. Violating this rule results in unusable DUT boards.

For the layout of the channel modules and logical boards see DPS128/DPS64 hardware overview and Concept of logical boards.

Note There is no guarding. Force and sense lines should be connected as close as possible to the load.

Pogo pin description

Pin Description DUT board connection

PA#, PB#

High force / power line of channel #

PA# pins of the VHD pogo block correspond to the P# pins of other pogo cable assemblies using the HD block.

PB# are the extra pins on rows e, f, and g of the VHD block.

PSA#, PSB#

High sense / power sense line of channel #

PSA# pins of the VHD pogo block correspond to the PS# pins of other pogo cable assemblies using the HD block.

PSB# are the extra pins on rows e, f, and g of the VHD block.		 Connect to the related high force line of the channel close to the DUT or via a separate sense connection of the probe card or DUT socket.
GND Ground line of all channels Connect to DUT board ground.

GSA#, GSB#

Ground sense line of channel #

GSA# pins of the VHD pogo block correspond to the GS# pins of other pogo cable assemblies using the HD block.

GSB# are the extra pins on new rows e, f, and g of the VHD block.		 Connect to the related GND reference pin or layer close to the DUT or via a separate sense connection of the probe card or DUT socket.
NC Do not connect pin These pins are internally used. Do not connect these pins on the DUT board but provide landing pads for compatibility with other DPS cards using the VHD pogo block.

Notes on the pogo pin descriptions:

  • # = Channel 1 … 16

  • DC/DC power units A and B of pin designations "PA#", "PSA#", "GSA#", "PB#", "PSB#", and "GSB#" refer to the two logical boards that are covered by a VHD pogo block. Since, this information is required only for the pin assignment, these pins are referenced as "P#", "PS#", and "GS#" in all other sections of the TDC.

    For more information, refer to section Examples in DPS128 and DPS64 pogo cabling and model file configuration.

CAUTION The common ground is wired to several pogo pins. All "GND" pogo pins must be connected in parallel on the DUT board to ensure that the specifications for the card and the maximum ratings for the pogo pins are met.

DPS128 power budget

The DPS128/DPS64 card features two power domains with a dedicated power budget. Each domain is able to deliver half of the overall power budget. The power domain to channel mapping must be observed for DPS128 cards when assigning the available channels to the DUT pins. For more information refer to DPS128/DPS64 power budget.

Topic 354873

Step 2: Contact Tab planarity measurement of complete Probe Card

This topic describes details on the contact tab, measure its planarity and adjust accordingly if required.

About this task

After assembling the complete probe card, measure the planarity of all the four contact tabs.

Before you begin

You have followed all the steps in Step 1: Check stiffener specification

Assemble the complete Probe Card with stiffener, PCB, Probe head, Backer.

Procedure

  1. On the granite table, adjust the gauge blocks.

    If available use a gauge block fixture, see note above.

    Note Advantest recommends using a fixture plate to hold the gauge blocks in place, see drawings for N2360-21605 in Direct-Probe probe card layout information and Direct-Probe stiffener.
  2. Place the probe card/stiffener assembly onto the gauge blocks so that the contact tabs on the stiffener frame rests with its reference surface on the gauge blocks.
  3. Measure the gap, if any between the contact tab and the gauge block at all positions using the thickness gauge.
  4. If adjustment is required, add round shims between the stiffener frame and the contact tab.

    Thickness of the round shim will depend on the gap measurement.

Results

You have measured the planarity of the contact tabs of the complete probe card.

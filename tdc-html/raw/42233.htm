Topic 42233

Parameter: tel_reversed_binning

Some models of TEL or KLA probers (basically certain 10xx or 20S models installed in Europe or USA) may use a different order of binning data, while working in multi-die scenarios.

This is due to different firmware releases of the systems software of the prober. The result of a reversed binning is visible by comparing the bin result maps of SmarTest and of the prober. If the prober uses a reversed binning (for example, in a scenario with a 4 die probe card), the bin data of site 1 is sent to the probe position 4, the bin data of site 2 is sent to the probe position 3, 3 is sent to 2, and 4 is sent to 1. The TEL driver plug-in can deal with this problem by setting this parameter to "yes". This causes to plug-in to correct the binning so that the bin data of site 1 is sent to the probe position 1, and so on.

Driver default: "no"

Changeable at run-time: yes

Applicable for: probers

Used by plug-in: TEL

Example: tel_reversed_binning: "yes"

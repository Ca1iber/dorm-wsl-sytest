Throughput and performance enhancement requires considerable effort to analyze testflows and test programs, identify potential problems and find ways to solve issues. The following reference will guide you through the process of effectively reducing the test time and increase multi-site efficiency of your test program without compromising the test coverage.

There are multiple methodologies which enable the increase of device throughput on the V93000.

  • The high pin count, per-pin capabilities of the V93000 enable highly parallel multi-site testing. The flexibility of configuration to match the device specific cores under test provide the additional benefit of ConCurrent Test (CCT).
  • The V93000 SmarTest software enables parallel threading of calculation (SmartCalc) processes to take advantage of the multi-core, multi-processor tester workstation to minimize calculation overhead during test.

In order to select the right direction to follow, you need a good understanding of the whole situation you are working on including the device, the test system.

Each of the above mentioned possibility requires different approaches and even the usage of different SmarTest tools set to understand the problem and solve it. Whether it is hidden upload for an analog dominant applications or hardware trigger in an RF environment, you will use this weapons to make the best usage of your tester resources and SmarTest features and thus reduce significantly your COT.

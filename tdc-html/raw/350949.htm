XPS256 pogo cable assembly EK751HDA: 16 HD pogo blocks, 256 channels

The EK751HDA pogo cable assembly connects 256 channels of an XPS256 card via 16 high-density (HD) pogo blocks to the DUT board.

Basic data and pogo block

Overall length: 750 mm
Pogo block style: 4-row high-density (HD) pogo block
Pogo blocks: 16
Channels per pogo block: 16
Total channels: 256
Part number: EK751-61602

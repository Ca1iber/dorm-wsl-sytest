E8023PC pogo cable assembly: 4 HD pogo blocks (DPS128-HC only)

The E8023PC pogo cable assembly connects the DPS128 via four HD pogo blocks to the DUT board, ganging two DPS128 channels to one channel on the pogo block side. This pogo cable assembly is a solution for the reuse of existing DPS32 setups, as it has the same pogo pin assignment.

Pogo cable assembly data

Overall length: 750 mm
Pogo block style: HD, 4-row pogo block
Pogo blocks: 4
Channels per pogo block: 16
DPS128 channels per 1 channel on pogo block: 2
Total number of channels on pogo block level: 64
Max. current per channel on pogo block level: 2 A

Two DPS128 channels are ganged in the pogo block and routed as one channel.

Orientation mark ●
The table on the left uses the following naming scheme:
  • P#: Power #
  • PS#: Power sense #
  • GND: Common ground for all channels of the card
  • GS#: Ground sense (separate line for each channel)
  • NC: Pin not connected. These pins are internally used. Do not connect these pins on the DUT board but provide landing pads.
  • (...): Values in parentheses indicate the channels on the DPS128-HC side.

"#" is the channel number 1  … 16.

For the layout of the channel modules and logical boards see DPS128/DPS64 hardware overview and Concept of logical boards for SmarTest 7 and see DPS128/DPS64 hardware overview for SmarTest 8.

Note There is no guarding. Force and sense lines should be connected as close as possible to the load.

DPS128 power budget

The DPS128/DPS64 card features two power domains with a dedicated power budget. Each domain is able to deliver half of the overall power budget. The power domain to channel mapping must be observed for DPS128 cards when assigning the available channels to the DUT pins. For more information refer to DPS128/DPS64 power budget.

PMUX01 supply pins

The supply pins of the PMUX01 Power MUX provide the power for the multiplexers and controllers, and define the logic levels and return path for the control pins.

VCC

The three VCC pins are the supply pins of the card. The pins should be connected to XPP5V and XPP5Vs on the DUT board. All three VCC pins must be connected in parallel on the DUT board to ensure that the specifications for the card and the maximum ratings for the pogo pins are met.

The voltage at the VCC pins and GND pins defines the thresholds of the I2C interface.

GND

The three GND pins are the return pins of the VCC pins. The pins should be connected to UGND on the DUT board. All three GND pins must be connected in parallel on the DUT board to ensure that the specifications for the card and the maximum ratings for the pogo pins are met.

The voltage at the VCC pins and GND pins defines the thresholds of the I2C interface. The GND pins are the ground reference for the I2C interface.

Topic 56955

Implicit setting of termination mode for standard/single-ended

The termination mode for Standard/Single-Ended is not specified directly. It is determined from the presence (or absence) of the ioh, iol and vt group of resources.

Specification for the termination mode

Termination mode

 

Specification

off

(off)

ioh, iol and vt are not specified

driver terminated

(t)

Only vt is specified

driver high terminated

(h)

vt = vih

driver low terminated

(l)

vt = vil

active load terminated

(a)

ioh, iol and vt are specified

Functional changes

  1. Introduced before SmarTest 6.3.2

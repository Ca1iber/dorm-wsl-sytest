Utility lines (V93000 systems)

Utility lines allow you to control external devices such as relays, solenoids or indicators, depending on the application.

The utility line configuration and characteristics of V93000 Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems is defined in this section. For the utility line configuration and characteristics of V93000 EXA Scale systems, refer to Utility lines (V93000 EXA Scale systems).

Utility lines 129-256 are optional and supported from SmarTest release 7.2.0 on.

The maximum voltage and the impedance depend on the configuration of the system:

  • An internal pull up resistor is available per utility line.
  • The pull-up resistor is connected to the internal supply voltage of the utility line.
  • The resulting output voltage of the open output depends on the configuration of the system and will be in the range from 3.3 V to 5.0 V.

We recommend using external pull up resistors for applications with higher requirements.

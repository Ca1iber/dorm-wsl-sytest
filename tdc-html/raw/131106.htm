Topic 131106

Period resolution for multiport setups

The period resolution options differ for pure Smart Scale ports and mixed or pure Pin Scale ports:

  • In Pin Scale systems, every port in a multiport setup must follow the same period resolution. As a result, several ports have their port periods auto-corrected.
  • In Smart Scale systems, each port in a multiport setup can have its own period resolution and therefore can generate its own port period, independent of other ports.

    Every Smart Scale channel has its own phase locked loop (PLL), and the period of this local PLL defines its clock resolution. Every Smart Scale channel can therefore have a different clock resolution, and consequently also a different period resolution.

    For SOC systems, you typically use this feature on the port level for ports that contain only Smart Scale pins.

  • Ports that contain a mix of both Smart Scale and Pin Scale channels behave like Pin Scale ports without Smart Scale channels.

Closing a ticket

To close a ticket, click Close Ticket in the ticket detail page.

About this task

You can close the ticket when the issue proposed in the ticket has been resolved.

Before you begin

You must be logged in to the help center.

Procedure

  1. In the help center, click the My Area tab.
  2. Click the ticket that you want to close.

    The details of the ticket are displayed.

  3. Click the Close ticket button.

    A dialog opens.



  4. Click Yes.

Results

This ticket is closed.

Functional changes

  1. Introduced in SmarTest 7.6.2

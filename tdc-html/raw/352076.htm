PS5000 differential pin pair assignment

Pin Scale 5000 channels can be configured as single ended channels or as differential channel pairs. The graphics and tables show the assignment of the differential pin pairs on each pogo block.

Differential pin pairs of the standard-density (SD) pogo block:

A1 - A3     A2 - A4
A5 - A7 A6 - A8
A9 - A11 A10 - A12
A13 - A15 A14 - A16

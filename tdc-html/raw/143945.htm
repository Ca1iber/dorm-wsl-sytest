PS1600 AWG

The PPMU can be used as an arbitrary waveform generator (AWG), supporting both voltage and current waveforms.

All specifications for static PPMU force mode apply.

Max sample rate1)

150 ksps

 

Max waveform size

only limited by vector memory

Linearity in range of 0...5V (typical values)2)

DNL 250uV (50ppm of range)

INL 300uV (60ppm of range)

Linearity in range of 0...3.3V (typical values)2)

DNL 275uV (83ppm of range)

INL 300uV (90ppm of range)
1) For best AWG performance the neighbor channel of a PS1600 differential pair should not perform any AWG, PMU re-programming or Change Level Operation. PPMU reprogramming may cause noise on adjacent channel of differential pin pair's PPMU. Otherwise max sampling rate may drop to 75ksps and additional timing jitter may be introduced.
2) These values are typical numbers, based on generating a 12bit voltage ramp waveform (4096 steps), using the +/- 40mA PMU current range (Range 1). DNL/INL calculation is done using the best fit method.

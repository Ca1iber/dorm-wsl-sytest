Electrical design rules - DPS-slot power supplies

Subtopics

  1. General Purpose DPS (GP-DPS)
  2. High Current DPS (HC-DPS)
  3. High Voltage DPS (HV-DPS)
  4. Multisite DPS (MS-DPS)

The DPS-slot device power supplies contain independently programmable device power supply (DPS) channels. The DPS-slot device power supplies can reside in the DPS-slot (that is the ninth slot beside the DC/DC cards) of any card cage of the V93000 Smart Scale and Pin Scale systems.

Supported V93000 systems : Smart Scale (SmarTest 7), Pin Scale, HSM

Supported V93000 system generations and variants

This section holds product-specific electrical design rules for the V93000 system generations and variants mentioned above. For the supported set of test head cards on your system, refer to Electrical design: Product-specific rules.

Functional changes

  1. Introduced in SmarTest 7.5.2 / 8.2.5

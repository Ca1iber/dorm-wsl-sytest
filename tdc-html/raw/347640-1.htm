Utility power supply - UPP15V / UPM15V / F24V pins (V93000 EXA Scale systems)

The "Utility power supply - UPP15V / UPM15V / F24V pins" of V93000 EXA Scale systems is routed to the UPP15V, UPM15V, F24VA and F24VB pins of selected utility pogo blocks and provides one +15 V, one -15 V and two floating 24 V outputs.

Note: The 4-row utility pogo blocks of the second Utility Card replace all existing 3-row DPS-slot pogo blocks.

The DPS block sequence of the second Utility Card differs from the UTIL block sequence of the first card. For the correct pogo block assignment of the second card, refer to section Utility pogo block mapping in Utility pogo block - Utility Card (V93000 EXA Scale systems).

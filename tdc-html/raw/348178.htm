Topic 348178

Twinning family board design for Direct-Probe

Due to the Direct-Probe™ mechanical requirements, note that the following pins in groups 8B and 3A cannot be used by the Twinning family board. Refer also to Twinning DUT board design for Direct-Probe.

  • Group 8B: Rows AG...Y and columns 0...7
  • Group 3A: Rows AG...Y and columns 10...18

Topic 148941

Logging in

About this task

You must be logged in to the Advantest Emergency License feature to request emergency licenses. You access the Advantest Emergency License feature through the Advantest homepage. Both pages request a login.

Note All entries in the login screens are case sensitive.

Before you begin

You must have an Advantest customer login, which is based on your email address.

Note You can request a reset for a forgotten password by using the link on the general Advantest login page.
Note All entries in the login screens are case sensitive.

Procedures

Advantest homepage login:

To log in to the Advantest homepage, complete the following steps:

  1. Start your Web browser and connect to the Advantest site at www.advantest.com.
  2. The Advantest License Renewal Portal is located at Advantest License Management System.
  3. Type your customer credentials and click Login. For registered customers the login name is the email address provided during registration.
  4. Enter http://www.advantest.com/licensing.

    The Licensing page opens.

  5. On the Licensing page click the V93000 SoC / Smart Scale link.

    The V93000 Licensing page opens.

  6. Click Emergency Temporary License.

Advantest Emergency License Portal login:

To log in to the Advantest License Renewal feature from the Advantest homepage, complete the following steps:

  1. Select the Emergency License tab.
  2. Select the login type: Username or Entitlement.
  3. Depending on the type of login you are using, complete one of the following two steps:
    1. Username: For logging in using your username, enter the user name for your Advantest customer login and enter the associated Password.
      Note If you are setup for the Renewal Portal you can access the Emergency License Feature with the same login credentials.
    2. Entitlement: For logging in using one of your entitlement IDs, enter the entitlement ID and your email address.
      Note Emergency license are sent to the email address you enter here.
  4. Click Login.
    Note If you cannot login, contact your Advantest representative providing your Advantest customer login name (do not provide your password) and the error message you received.

Results

This opens the Advantest Emergency License License Facility page.

What to do next

Complete the emergency license request form and submit to Advantest. Emergency licenses appropriate for your registered systems will be delivered to you.

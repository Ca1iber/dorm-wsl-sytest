Topic 130453

Slice depth

The slice depth defines how many bits of the state character sequence will be applied to a pin role before proceeding to the next pin role of the group.

For parallel protocols the state characters are specified for a pin role group. They are distributed to all pin roles of a pin role group based on the slice depth setting.

This is a SmarTest terminology used with parallel protocols and corresponding Pin Role Groups.

Parameter: ignored_SRQs

In case the equipment generates SRQs on the GPIB interface, that are not expected by the driver plug-in, the plug-in usually stops operating and the driver fails.

This happens to ensure that the driver plug-in is always synchronized with the equipment state. In case of an incomplete plug-in implementation, it may happen that some SRQs are not handled correctly. To avoid time losses due to bug reports and new driver releases at production sites, this configuration parameter allows the driver plug-in to ignore certain SRQs in general.

The SRQ values range from 0-255.

Note Each given SRQ value in the list will be completely ignored. If SRQs are entered, that are needed by the driver plug-in in some situations but should be ignored in other situations, this parameter cannot be used. In case this parameter is used, a report should be sent to your Advantest representative to help improving future driver plug-in releases.

Driver default: empty list

Changeable at run-time: no

Applicable for: probers and handlers, plug-in dependent

Example: ignored_SRQs: [100 96]

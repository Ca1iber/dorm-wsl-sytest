9.5 inch and 12 inch WPI generic DUT boards

There are four types of generic wafer prober DUT boards: a small and a large board for the V93000 9.5 inch WPI pogo tower, and a small and a large board for the V93000 12 inch WPI pogo tower.

The 9.5 inch WPI supports up to 512 pins with a small WPI DUT board, and up to 1024 pins with a large WPI DUT board. The 12 inch WPI supports up to 1024 pins with either the small or the large WPI DUT board.

For the 9.5 inch WPI and the 12 inch WPI the small and large DUT boards are compatible up to 512 pins.

The 9.5 inch WPI and the 12 inch WPI are not compatible in routing.

The pogo pad block numbering is identical to V93000 DUT boards.

For details see the following drawings of general-purpose DUT boards (in Layout information: DUT board prober interface):

  • D-E7018-96504: 512 pin DUT Board for 9.5 inch WPI
  • D-E7018-96501: 1024 pin DUT Board for 9.5 inch WPI
  • D-E7018-96561: small DUT Board for 12 inch WPI
  • D-E7018-96551: large DUT Board for 12 inch WPI

RF or high-speed digital testing often require custom WPI DUT boards.

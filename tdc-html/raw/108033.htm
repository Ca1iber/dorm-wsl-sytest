Internal Utility Power Supply (SE-UDPS) specifications

The optional "Internal Utility Power Supply (SE-UDPS)" is a product from Salland Engineering (Europe) BV. The SE-UDPS is routed to a 3-row DPS-slot pogo block.

Configuration
  • Any DPS slot can be used
  • The Internal Utility Power Supply can be mixed with any other device power supply in a test head, no limitations apply.
  • Diagnostics requires the diagnostics board E8026DIAG.

Pogo pins

The SE-UDPS is routed to a 3-row DPS-slot pogo block.

Refer to Internal Utility Power Supply (SE-UDPS) pogo pin assignments for more information.
+5.0 V: P1, PS1, G1 (two pins)
+12.0 V: P2, PS2, G2 (two pins)
+15.0 V: P3, PS3, G3 (two pins)
-15.0 V: P4, PS4, G4 (two pins)
General capabilities
  • Overcurrent protection
  • Individual ON / OFF per power supply
  • ON / OFF detection when DUT board is removed
  • Kelvin connection per DPS channel (power line)

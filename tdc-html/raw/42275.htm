Function: ph_pause_start

Notifies the driver that the pause level is entered. This ensures that the driver correctly interprets specific CI flags, for example, the flags check_dev, skip and retest.

A call of ph_pause_start is mandatory when the pause level is entered.

Applicable for: handler and prober driver

Example: PROB_HND_CALL (ph_pause_start)

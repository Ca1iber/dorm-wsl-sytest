Submitting a ticket

To submit a ticket, fill in the information for the reported issue and click Submit.

About this task

For some simple and urgent issues, you can contact the TE-Cloud support team by online chat. When you come across an issue which needs to be clarified by links, images, etc., or the urgency of the issue is not so high, you can submit a ticket.

Before you begin

You must be logged in to the help center.

Procedure

  1. In the help center, click the Submit a ticket button.

    The ticket submission page opens.

  2. Enter the subject and description of the issue.
  3. Select a product name.
  4. Click the Submit button.

Results

The ticket is created with the status Open.

When the support team responds to the ticket, you will get an e-mail with the reply.

If you do not take any action to the ticket for a long time, the status turns to On Hold.

What to do next

To add a reply to the ticket, reply to the ticket.

If the issue has been resolved, close the ticket.

Functional changes

  1. Introduced in SmarTest 7.6.2

Pin Scale 5000 programmable clamp

The programmable clamp is not available for fast mode.

Programmable clamp
Clamp voltage range at |VGS| ≤ 100 mV -2.0 V to +7.0 V
Maximum clamp voltage window 9 V
Clamp voltage resolution 1 mV
Clamp voltage accuracy, verified with a measurement current of 900 µA ± 100 mV

The sustainable total load may not exceed 5,000 mA per card.

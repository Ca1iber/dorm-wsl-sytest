Topic 30745

dpsCurrent_check

Subtopics

  1. Associated Syntax Entities

This expression is used in conjunction with MONITOR_CALL, and lets you check on the device power supply current. The current limit must also be defined beforehand, by the expression dpsCurrent_limit.

What happens if the current exceeds the limit is determined by the out of control rules - see ooc_warning_rule and ooc_stop_rule.

The lines below show that a limit of 0.9A have been set for the DPS current, while later in the die level this limit is checked.

{lot:

MONITOR_CALL(dpsCurrent_limit,0.9);

:

:

:

{die:

MONITOR_CALL(dpsCurrent_check);

:

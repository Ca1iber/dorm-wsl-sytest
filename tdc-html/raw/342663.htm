Overview V93000 EXA Scale systems

These utility resources are available on V93000 EXA Scale systems:
  • Utility lines:

    • Internal utility resources - V93000 EXA Scale systems:

      32 statically controlled utility lines are provided as internal resources of the basic configuration. 32 more statically controlled utility lines can be installed in the second card cage of the SX and LX test heads.

    • Utility Card:

      The optional Utility Card replaces the 32 lines of the basic configuration with 256 dynamically controlled utility lines per card. For more information, refer also to Utility lines in the DUT board design reference.

  • Utility power supplies:
    • Internal utility resources - V93000 EXA Scale systems:

      One +5 V supply and the optional Internal Utility Power Option are provided as internal resources of the basic configuration. An additional optional +5 V supply can be installed in the second card cage of the SX and LX test heads.

    • Utility Card:

      The optional Utility Card replaces the supply of the basic configuration by several other utility power supplies. For more information, refer to also to table Utility power supplies below and to Utility power supplies in the DUT board design reference.

Overview V93000 Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems

These utility resources are available on V93000 Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems:
  • Utility lines:

    Up to 256 utility lines of V93000 Pin Scale and Smart Scale are provided by active or passive wiring boards and routed to up to eight 2-row or 4-row utility pogo blocks. For more information, refer to Utility lines in the DUT board design reference.

  • Internal utility power supply:

    The +5 V supply is routed to the UP5V pins of the utility pogo blocks. For more information, refer to also to table Utility power supplies below and to Utility power supplies in the DUT board design reference.

  • External utility power supply:

    The optional external utility power supply is routed to the XP... pins of the utility pogo blocks. It provides +5 V, -5 V and +3.3 V. See also section Utility power supplies below.

  • Internal Utility Power Supply (SE-UDPS):

    The optional internal supply is routed to a 3-row DPS-slot pogo block. The supply provides +5 V, +12 V, +15 V and -15 V.

Common characteristics of all utility power supplies

Utility power supplies can work in only one quadrant: They can force a positive voltage and drive a current, or they can force a negative voltage and sink a current. The output can be switched on or off.

As a result, the utility power supplies cannot force 0 V at the output and cannot discharge circuitry on the DUT board. The following notes must therefore be observed:
  • The time to bring the voltage down to 0 V depends on the load and the capacitors on the DUT board. High load currents will decrease the time. Large capacitors will increase the time.
  • Switching off a utility power supply for only a short time can lead to a critical voltage drop. See also the description for UP5V on Pin Scale and SmartScale systems in Utility power supplies.
  • The critical drop in the supply voltage, also known as brownout, can bring digital circuits into an undefined state.
  • The current through the internal pull‑up resistor of a deactivated utility lines and the connected circuitry on the DUT board can also push up the output voltage of a switched‑off utility power supply to an undefined value. For more information, refer to Utility line characteristics and Utility line example circuits.

Utility power supply - UP5V pins

The output of the utility supply can be switched on or off simultaneously on all utility pogo blocks of the Utility card or (for the internal utility resources) of the card cage using an API of SmarTest.

With compatibility settings for Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems enabled, the UP5V output of the first card cage or of the first Utility Card is switched on by default1). With standard settings for V93000 EXA Scale systems selected, the outputs of the utility power supply are disabled by default.

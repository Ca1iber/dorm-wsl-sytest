Topic 17816

Minimum period / maximum frequency of the sequencer

When setting up the timing for a pattern, a minimum period time respectively a maximum frequency must be considered.

Test head card Minimum period Maximum sequencer frequency Note
Pin Scale digital cards
Pin Scale 1600 2.5 ns 400 MHz
DC Scale power supply cards
DPS128 / DPS64 20 ns 50 MHz
UHC4T 2.5 ns 400 MHz
XPS256 20 ns 50 MHz

Note that the minimum period for DPS128 is 20 ns. Pattern on DPS pins within a min period of less than 20 ns must be adjusted when migrating to a DPS128 setup.

See also Minimum sequencer period (base rate) for digital cards and Typical issues when converting to DPS128.

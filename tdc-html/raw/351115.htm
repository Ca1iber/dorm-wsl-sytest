XPS256 force and clamp voltages

Each channel of an XPS256 card features two combined force, clamp, and measure voltage ranges.

Voltage ranges

Each PMU features two combined force, clamp, and measure voltage ranges:

Variant Range Minimum and maximum voltage
XPS256 1.5 V1) -0.5 V ... 1.5 V
7.0 V -2.5 V ... 7.0 V

The voltage ranges are common for forcing, clamping, and measuring voltages. The force or clamp range is always the same as the measurement range. For specification data, refer to XPS256 voltage specifications. For the maximum current related to the voltage, refer to XPS256 voltage and current capability specifications.

Minimum clamp voltage distance

The PMU of an XPS256 channel needs a minimum distance between the positive and negative clamp voltage to avoid oscillation.

Variant Voltage range Programmable range Minimum clamp distance
Negative clamp Positive clamp
XPS256 1.5 V -0.5 V ... 0.0 V 0.0 V ... 1.5 V 0.1 V
7.0 V -2.5 V ... 0.0 V 0.0 V ... 7.0 V 0.5 V

The minimum clamp distance means that both the negative and the positive clamp voltage can be set within the full range from the minimum respectively the maximum value to 0.0 V if the minimum distance of the two values is maintained.

Asymmetrical clamp voltage definitions with one of the values set to 0.0 V can be programmed, but internally the channel controller always uses a maximum of -50 mV for the negative clamp and a minimum of +50 mV for the positive clamp.

For clamp voltage specifications, refer to table Voltage clamp in XPS256 voltage specifications.

Dynamic behavior in force voltage or clamp voltage mode

The following force voltage filters are available for an XPS256 channel:

  • Voltage bandwidth

    This value defines the bandwidth that has an effect on the dynamic behavior of an XPS256 channel that is forcing or clamping the voltage.

    Range Min Max Default
    1.5 V 2 Hz 50 kHz 10 kHz
    7 V 2 Hz 50 kHz 10 kHz
  • Voltage slew rate

    The parameter defines the slew rate for changes of the programmed voltage force value.

    Range Min Max Default
    1.5 V 10 μV/μs 2 V/μs 50.0 mV/μs
    7 V 45 μV/μs 2 V/μs 50.0 mV/μs
  • Load capacitance

    Represents the load capacitance on the DUT board. The parameter is used to adapt the dynamic behavior of an XPS256 channel to different load capacitors. Although the filter values are defined for the current ranges, this filter has an effect on the dynamic behavior when a channel is forcing or clamping the voltage.

    Range Min Max2) Default
    1 A 0.0 mF 1.0 mF 0.0 mF
    100 mA 0.0 μF 100.0 μF 0.0 μF
    10 mA 0.0 μF 10.0 μF 0.0 μF
    1 mA 0.0 μF 1.0 μF 0.0 μF
    100 μA 0.0 nF 100.0 nF 0.0 nF
    10 μA 0.0 nF 10.0 nF 0.0 nF

    The values defined in the table above apply for a single channel. For ganging in the 1 A range, the maximum load capacitance value can be multiplied by the number of ganged channels.

Note

Due to the capacitive load of the DUT board and the DUT, increasing the voltage slew rate will always result in a higher current while the forced voltage changes. If the higher current exceeds the programmed clamp current, the effective voltage slew rate is limited and the Constant current flag might be triggered.

For high voltage slew rates, we therefore recommend increasing the clamp current for the time while the forced voltage changes.

Note The following units are to be used for programming the force voltage filters:
Voltage bandwidth: Hz (Hertz)
Voltage slew rate: V / µs (Volts per microsecond)
Load capacitance: F (Farad)

Load step response

The load step response achieved for the XPS256 depends on several settings of the instrument, the capacitors on the DUT board, and finally the height and slew rate of the load step.

For information on how to setup the XPS256 channels and getting the best load step response, refer to XPS256 load step response.

Voltage sense lines

The forced or clamped voltage is measured differentially between the high sense pogo pin (PS#) and the ground sense pogo pin (GS#). The measured value is the voltage feedback value for the digital closed loop controller and is used to adjust the forced or clamped voltage. Refer to Voltage sense lines in XPS256 measure voltages for a detailed description.

The compliance voltage defines the minimum and maximum voltage directly at the P# pogo pin referenced to the common ground (GND). The programmable voltage is limited to the regular limits of the selected voltage range.

See also Disconnect state in XPS256 other performance characteristics.

Force to sense clamp

Each XPS256 channel features a switchable clamp circuitry, which consists of two anti-parallel diodes, between the high force (P#) pogo pin and the high sense (PS#) pogo pin and between the ground sense (GS#) pogo pin and the common ground (GND). The diodes limit the voltage difference between the two pins for abnormal operating conditions. An abnormal operating condition is for example an open sense connection on the DUT board. The diodes limit the voltage increase on the force line to about 1 V in this case3).

The following data must be respected for the diodes:

Maximum operating voltage range or voltage difference between P# and PS# or GS# and GND: ±500 mV
Typical maximum force to sense current within the operating voltage range: ±1 uA

The diodes are automatically connected or disconnected depending on the connection state and the measurement mode:

Connection state Mode State of the clamp circuit
P# to PS# GS# to GND
disconnected Low impedance (loz) inactive inactive
High impedance (hiz) inactive inactive
connected Force voltage active active
Force current
High impedance voltage measurement inactive inactive

DC Scale voltage and current limits

The XPS256 card supports the DC Scale voltage and current limits. SmarTest ensures that the defined voltage and current limits cannot be exceeded by programming higher or lower force or clamp values.

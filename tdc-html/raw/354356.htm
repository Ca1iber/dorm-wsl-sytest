Topic 354356

PS1600 DC offset

The differential pair signals must be set up properly in order to avoid an impact of the pull-down resistor of PS1600 on measurement results.

The measurement result of a PS1600 pin will experience a DC offset if all of the following conditions apply:

  • The pull-down resistor of the mate pin, that is, the pin which would form the differential pair, will be engaged on instrument disconnect.

  • The mate pull-down resistor is found engaged during the measurement.

  • There is a current flowing through the mate pull-down resistor during the measurement, resulting in a voltage difference across this mate pull-down resistor. That is, a voltage ≠ DUTGND is present on the mate pin, caused by the DUT.

Note If the conditions listed above are all met the DC offset will come to effect regardless of whether the mate pin has been configured, or not.

Consider the following situations meeting the conditions listed above:

  • Measurements are to be executed on an enabled site. The mate pins of this enabled site terminate on a disabled site, have their pull-down resistors engaged, and are exposed to a voltage ≠ DUTGND.

  • A mate pin is not configured. Its pull-down resistor has been engaged, and it is exposed to a voltage ≠ DUTGND.

  • A mate pin has been configured. It is not used during the period 0 to T. Its pull-down resistor has been engaged, and its pin is exposed to a voltage ≠ DUTGND. If a measurement is executed within the period 0 to T on the considered pin , the measurement of the considered signal will experience a DC offset.

  • You want to perform a high impedance measurement with direct BADC (VM_HIZ). A BADC has got an input port with high impedance. Hence, the input port will tend to float if no defined input signal is provided. The pull-down resistor can be used to provide such a defined input during the measurement. If there is a current flowing through this pull-down resistor during the measurement, a measurement of the mate pin will experience a DC offset.

How to avoid PS1600 DC offset

You have the following options to prevent a DC offset:

  • Disable the engagement of PS1600 pull-down resistors in general.

    See the section Global default settings above. If the mate pin is unused, the pull-down of the unused pin should also be disabled in the .technology file.

    The drawback is to lose the protective function of this float preventing functionality, and to risk equipment damage. You are recommended to provide an external protection mechanism in this case, for instance a switchable pull-down resistor on a test fixture.

  • Have all mate pins configured and added to the measurement.

    The pull-down function will always be disengaged during the measurement execution.

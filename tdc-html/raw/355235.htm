Topic 355235

Accessing online testers

Subtopics

  1. Getting contact information for an engineering test center
  2. Adding a public SSH key
  3. Booking a tester for a specific time slot
  4. Connecting to a tester over the internet
  5. Transmitting files to the tester

To access an online tester, you need to get in touch with the an engineering test center, book the tester on the TE-Cloud dashboard, and connect to the tester.

The TE-Cloud platform is integrated with ETCs (engineering test centers) which provide the connection to online testers through SSH.

To access an online tester, perform the following steps:
  1. Getting contact information for an engineering test center
  2. Adding a public SSH key
  3. Booking a tester for a specific time slot
  4. Connecting to a tester over the internet

After the connection, you can use the tester for online test and transmit files to the tester.

Functional changes

  1. Introduced in SmarTest 7.6.2

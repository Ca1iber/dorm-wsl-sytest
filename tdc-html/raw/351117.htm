XPS256 force and clamp currents

Each channel of an XPS256 card features six combined force, clamp, and measure current ranges.

Current ranges

Each PMU features six combined force, clamp, and measure current ranges:

Variant Range Minimum and maximum current
XPS256 1 A -1.0 A ... 1.0 A
100 mA -100.0 mA ... 100.0 mA
10 mA -10.0 mA ... 10.0 mA
1 mA -1.0 mA ... 1.0 mA
100 μA -100.0 μA ... 100.0 μA
10 μA -10.0 μA ... 10.0 μA

The current ranges are common for forcing, clamping, and measuring currents. The force or clamp range is always the same as the measurement range. For specification data, refer to XPS256 voltage specifications.

Minimum clamp current distance

The PMU of an XPS256 channel needs a minimum distance between the positive and negative clamp current to avoid oscillation.
Variant Current range Minimum clamp value Minimum clamp distance
XPS256 1 A 10 mA 100 mA
100 mA 1 mA 10 mA
10 mA 100 μA 1 mA
1 mA 10 μA 100 μA
100 μA 1 μA 10 μA
10 μA 100 nA 1 μA

The minimum clamp distance is the distance from the minimum negative clamp value to the minimum positive clamp value. Asymmetrical clamp current definitions within the ranges defined above are supported as long as the minimum clamp distance between the two values is maintained. For clamp current specifications, refer to table Current clamp in XPS256 current specifications.

Dynamic behavior in force current or clamp current mode

The following force current filters are available for an XPS256 channel:

  • Current bandwidth

    This value defines a current bandwidth that has an effect on the dynamic behavior of an XPS256 channel that is forcing or clamping the current.

    Range Min Max Default
    All current ranges 10 Hz 500 kHz 10 kHz
  • Current slew rate

    The parameter defines the slew rate for changes of the programmed current force value.

    Range Min Max Default
    1 A 10.0 μA/μs 200.0 mA/μs 10.0 mA/μs
    100 mA 1.0 μA/μs 20.0 mA/μs 10.0 mA/μs
    10 mA 100.0 nA/μs 2.0 mA/μs 2.0 mA/μs
    1 mA 10.0 nA/μs 200.0 μA/μs 200.0 μA/μs
    100 μA 1.0 nA/μs 20.0 μA/μs 20.0 μA/μs
    10 μA 100.0 pA/μs 2.0 μA/μs 2.0 μA/μs

    The values defined in the table above apply for a single channel. For ganging groups, the current slew rate of the 1 A range can be multiplied by the number of ganged channels.

Note The following units are to be used for programming the force current filters:
Current bandwidth: Hz (Hertz)
Current slew rate: A / μs (Ampere per microsecond)

Load step response

The load step response achieved for the XPS256 depends on several settings of the instrument, the capacitors on the DUT board, and finally the height and slew rate of the load step.

For information on how to setup the XPS256 channels and getting the best load step response, refer to XPS256 load step response.

Fast clamp current

Very fast load changes or shorts of the DUT may cause high current flow exceeding current limit settings for a short time. The PMU output amplifier features a fast clamp current circuitry that limits the current for this short time.

For more information, refer to Hardware current limiter in XPS256 current specifications.

Note

The fast clamp current circuitry decreases the peak output current of output stage that is caused by the dynamic behavior and the limited response time of the digital closed loop control.

The fast clamp current circuitry cannot limit transient currents where the energy is supplied by capacitors on the DUT board or which are caused by parasitic effects such as the stray capacitance of the connection to the DUT.

Overcurrent protection

Overcurrent means that an XPS256 signal cannot maintain a maximum current threshold. Such a condition can occur, for example, if another source or the DUT overdrive the channel output stage of an active XPS256 signal.

The threshold for an overcurrent alarm is defined by the programmed current range plus an overhead of 10 % of the selected current range. This means that you can modify the distance between the programmed clamp or force current by programming a customized current range. The channel controller will then select the next higher hardware defined current range but derive the threshold from the programmed range.
For example, for the combination
Programmed clamp current: 0.5 A
Programmed current range: 0.6 A
the following settings are applied:
Clamp current: 0.5 A
Current range: 1.0 A
Threshold for the overcurrent event: 0.6 A + 10 % of 1.0 A = 0.7 A

In case an overcurrent event has been detected, the Overpower / Overcurrent / Power budget exceeded flag is set after a delay time of 50 ms and the related XPS256 pin is disconnected. Since the utilization of the power budget is monitored by SmarTest before downloading the settings and thus cannot trigger a POWER_BUDGET alarm, only the overcurrent event is mapped to this alarm.

DC Scale voltage and current limits

The XPS256 card supports the DC Scale voltage and current limits. SmarTest ensures that the defined voltage and current limits cannot be exceeded by programming higher or lower force or clamp values.

Scan view preferences brief overview

Specify the sorting of scan chains in the scan view and control the display of the legend.

Scan Pin: Sort by the pin through which the scan chains are accessed.

Displayed Fail Count: Sort by the number of fails in the displayed parts of the scan chains.

Chain Length: Sort by the lengths of the displayed scan chains.

Total Fail Count: Sort by the number of fails in the scan chains (including the parts not shown in the scan view).

Ascending: Show small values first.

Descending: Show large values first.

Show legend: Display a legend beside the chart.

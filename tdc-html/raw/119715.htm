Creating a new pin group

  1. In the Test Program Explorer, right-click the Group entry of the pin configuration section and choose New.
  2. In the New Group dialog, specify the type of the group - fixed or expression-based.
  3. Enter the name of the new pin group.
  4. Click OK.

Depending on the type of the new group:

  • Editing a fixed pin group
  • Editing an expression-based pin group

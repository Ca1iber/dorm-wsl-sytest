Utility power supplies (V93000 systems)

The utility power supplies support DUT boards with additional active components. Typical examples for these active components are relays, switches, operational amplifiers, and buffers.

Overview

Three utility power supplies featuring different voltage and current capabilities are available on V93000 systems.

Internal Utility Power Supply (SE-UDPS) (E7002US)

The optional "Internal Utility Power Supply (SE-UDPS)" is a product from Salland Engineering (Europe) BV. The SE-UDPS is routed to a 3-row DPS-slot pogo block.

Topic 119682

Editing a utility purpose

Procedure

  1. Choose the site for which you wish to edit the utility purpose, by entering its number in the Site field.
  2. To add utility lines to the utility purpose, select them in the Utility Lines field and click Add, or click Add All.

    To remove utility lines from the utility purpose, select them in the Utility Members field and click Remove, or click Remove All.

  3. To set the state of utility lines, select them in the Utility Members field and click the 0, 1, or X button.
  4. To specify a settling time, enter it in the Settling Time field.

    This setting applies to all sites.

Topic 142961

Managing test time reports

To view or delete the test time reports generated or imported with Test Time Utilities, use the Manage Test Time tab on Test Time Utilities.

Before you begin

You have already:

  • Generated test time reports with Test Time Utilities.
  • Imported test time files with Test Time Utilities.

Procedure

  1. Open TP360 and launch Test Time Utilities from the App Center tab.
  2. Click the Manage Test Time tab.

    All the generated and imported test time reports are listed in the order of date and time, as displayed in the following screenshot:

  3. To view the report of a measurement or a measurement set, expand the generated date and time entries and double-click the measurement name.

    The corresponding report is displayed in the Report view.

  4. To delete the reports of any date, any time or any measurement, select the corresponding item and click the delete icon .

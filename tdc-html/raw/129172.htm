SHARED_CONTINUITY_TEST.execute()

The execute() member function of SHARED_CONTINUITY_TEST lets you execute a shared continuity test based on your previous setup.

Syntax

void execute() const

Example

SHARED_CONTINUITY_TEST mTest;
mTest.execute();

Related information

SHARED_CONTINUITY_TEST

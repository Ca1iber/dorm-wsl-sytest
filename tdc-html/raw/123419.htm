Overview of the ready-made test methods

Advantest provides a range of ready-made test methods for driver-sharing tests.

This is a brief overview:

  • BinningExecutor: Evaluates the binning information provided by the BinningHandler test method and bins the devices accordingly.
  • BinningHandler: Provides softt-binning information for failed devices that allows them to continue being tested and binned at the end of a testflow.
  • OnlineCompensation: Compensates for delay effects caused by empty test sites.
  • ResetOverCurrentProtection: Resets the overcurrent protection circuits that may have been triggered in earlier tests.
  • SharedContinuity: Verifies continuity of test signals and checks for short circuits.
  • SharedIDD: Measures IDD and IDDQ on sharing load boards
  • SharedLeakage: Measures the leakage on shared pins.
  • SharedLeakageParallel: Measures the leakage on shared, non-shared, and cross-terminated pins that are terminated by a common DPS.
  • SharedVBump: Changes the supply voltage levels on VDD and/or VDDQ.

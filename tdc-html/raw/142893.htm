Topic 142893

Scan view properties brief overview

Specify the labels and filtering conditions for the scan view.

Titles tab

Chart title: The title of the chart, displayed at the top.

Chart subtitle: The subtitle of the chart, displayed below the title.

Filters tab

Fail Count: The minimum number of fails required for a scan chain to be displayed.

Chain Pin: Only scan chains for this pin are displayed.

Topic 30749

DRIFT_CALIBRATION

Subtopics

  1. Associated Syntax Entities

Performs a drift calibration measurement. You can use it, for example, at the beginning of a test program, repeat the measurement after a number of units have been tested, or associate it with a button in a SCREEN_DIALOG or LINE_DIALOG.

The line,

* drift_calibration_result = DRIFT_CALIBRATION;

performs a drift calibration measurement.

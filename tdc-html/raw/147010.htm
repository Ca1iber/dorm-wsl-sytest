Topic 147010

License Renewal Process

Subtopics

  1. Advantest License Renewal Portal
  2. License Renewal Comparison Tool
  3. Emergency License Requests

Advantest licenses are purchased on a perpetual basis. However, you must physically renew your license keys on a yearly base in March using the web-based Advantest License Renewal Portal. All perpetual licenses expire on 31st March.

To assign an entitlement and initiate the license renewal process, you need the Entitlement ID, which is stated on your entitlement certificate accompanying each purchase. If you lose or misplace an entitlement certificate, contact your Advantest sales representative for a replacement copy.

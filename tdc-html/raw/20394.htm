Topic 20394

Mechanical design for mixed-signal testing

Consider the following mechanical design and marking guidelines:

  • Because analog performance can be degraded by a damaged DUT socket, avoid soldering your DUT socket to the DUT board. Use a receptacle for the DUT socket so you can easily replace a damaged socket.
  • Put a mark at pin 1 of the socket to ensure that the device is always inserted correctly.
  • Ensure that relays and active components can be easily replaced. Use sockets and mark the correct insertion position.
  • Ensure that the DUT board can be easily changed and debugged.
  • Ensure that any required auxiliary components do not interfere with the IC handler or wafer prober.

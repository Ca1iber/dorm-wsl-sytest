GP-DPS load capacitors

The size of the load capacitors depends on the performance range selection of the DPS. See GP-DPS performance ranges.

When determining the size of the load capacitors, consider the following:

  • Maximum current swing:

    A high current swing can occur during operation if the DUT switches from the standard operational state to the quiescent state or from the quiescent state to the standard operational state. This is typically the current swing to be considered when selecting the performance range.

  • Stability of supply voltage range:

    To ensure an optimum supply for the DUT, the supply voltage must be stable within a certain voltage range. Minimum and maximum supply voltages are provided in the DUT's specification sheet. To ensure supply voltage stability, the load capacitance needs to be increased to decrease voltage ripple.

  • Measurement accuracy for low current measurements:

    Any load capacitance introduces current noise to a current measurement. The impact on the measurement accuracy can be significant for very low current measurements. To take full advantage of the high current measurement accuracy of the General Purpose DPS, the load capacitance should not exceed 1 µF.

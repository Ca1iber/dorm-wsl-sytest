Practical tips for selecting PDN capacitors

Slightly different criteria apply, when selecting either bulk or ceramic capacitors for your PDN (power distribution network). For practical design purposes consider the following hints and tips.

Selecting the bulk capacitors

Find a large-value Tantalum capacitor with the lowest ESR (equivalent series resistance) available.

This should be your primary focus for the bulk capacitors. Other parameters, such as a low ESL (equivalent series inductance), play a minimal role in the impedance characteristics of the overall capacitor network.

Tantalum capacitors are really large in size. But since their primary role is to ensure stability for the UHC4 power supply card, they can be placed as close as necessary to the corresponding UHC4 pogo pin block (see also Bulk capacitor placement). Any other large distance location from the DUT is also acceptable.

Selecting the ceramic capacitors

Find specific capacitor values with the absolutely lowest ESL (equivalent serial inductance) available.

This should be your primary focus for the ceramic capacitors. Other parameters, such as a low ESR (equivalent series resistance), play a minimal role in the high-frequency charge supply of these capacitors. In fact, higher ESR values could even be preferred, since they are widening the bandwidth of a capacitor (see also Ceramic capacitor placement ).

Smallest Coordinate Corner (QUAD)

The QUAD parameter determines in which corner of the grid the X_MIN and Y_MIN is;

  • 1 - bottom left
  • 2 - bottom right
  • 3 - top right
  • 4 - top left

    key-in values for X_MIN, X_MAX, Y_MIN and Y_MAX. The corresponding grid appears.

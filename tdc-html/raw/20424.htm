Topic 20424

Stiffener inlay plates

Subtopics

  1. General purpose stiffener inlay plate
  2. Stiffener inlay plate E6980DC
  3. Stiffener inlay plate E6980DD
  4. HSSI/RF stiffener inlay plates

DUT board stiffeners must be equipped with inlay plates when used for docking to handlers. You can adapt DUT board stiffeners to fit different handlers by using different inlay plates. Inlay plates provide mounting holes required for attaching the DUT board and stiffener assembly to the handler. A general purpose version is also offered with a filled device area which can be cut out to accommodate specific nonstandard configurations.

All inlay plates fit both the small and the large DUT board stiffeners and can be used with both the large (1024 pin) and the small (512 pin) DUT interface. For related drawings see Stiffener inlay (Handler solutions).

Note All DUT boards must provide clearance holes at the positions corresponding to the inlay plate mounting holes. See DUT board stiffener clearance holes for the exact locations and dimensions of clearance holes for the most common docking applications. Refer also to the drawings of the specific inlay plate used.

If you need a nonstandard docking solution for your specific docking application, please contact your Advantest representative for support.

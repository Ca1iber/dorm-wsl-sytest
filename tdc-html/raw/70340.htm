Topic 70340

Overon flag

Setting the Overon flag to ON allows you to continue the test flow after a stop bin was reached. The Overon flag can determine how summary statistics are calculated in some of the STDF record fields. The Overon flag is a select button, accessed from either the Testflow Flags dialog or the Bin Dialog window, that switches the Overon flag on or off.

Refer to global_overon for more information.

If the Overon flag is activated, and the global_overon flag has also been set, then even if a device falls into a stop bin the flow will continue immediately after the bin, that is, to the right of the bin, (as indicated by a dotted line in the testflow). If there are no more tests after the stop bin, the device is put in the otherwise bin.

Whether overon is enabled or disabled, only the first bin reached is counted.

Utility Card specifications

The Utility Card features 256 utility lines and several utility power supplies. The specification data for the utility lines and utility power supplies are available in this section.

Utility lines

For the utility line configuration and characteristics of V93000 EXA Scale systems, refer to Utility lines (V93000 EXA Scale systems).

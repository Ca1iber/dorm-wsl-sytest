FVI16 pogo cable assembly (E8026PVP)

The E8026PVP pogo cable assembly connects the sixteen floating channels of the DC Scale FVI16 card to the DUT board.

Pogo cable assembly data

  • Overall length: 600 mm ±10mm
  • Pogo blocks: 4
  • Pogo block style: 4-row pogo block
  • Channels per pogo block: 4

J10 connector orientation

Pogo block layout

The photo to the left shows the top view of an FVI16 pogo block. The empty rows 4, 8, 12, and 16 are named "Separation" in the FVI16 pogo pin assignment.

Assigning the Specification Set to Another Wavetable

  1. Click Change Wavetable in the Change menu.

    A screen similar to that of the figure below appears.

  2. Highlight the specification you want to assign to a different wavetable.
  3. Highlight a wavetable from the Wavetables list.
  4. Click assign.
  5. Repeat these last steps as often as required (for other specification sets), and click done when finished.

Functional changes

  1. Introduced before SmarTest 6.3.2

Topic 56926

Termination types

Subtopics

  1. Active load termination
  2. Driver termination

Transmission line conducts the output signals of a device's o (output) or io (bidirectional) pins to the comparator in the test system. Reflections on the transmission line can distort the signal at the receiver side of the test system. To avoid reflections, the transmission line must be terminated.

In the level setup, you can apply several termination types: driver, clamp, active load, and differential termination. The specific applicable termination type and termination mode depend on the channel card.

For further details, see the command section TERM, TERM? - (TERMination mode) and Data Organization of Equation Based Levels.

Topic 53116

DPS on generic WPI DUT boards

The generic DUT boards support GP-DPS, HV-DPS, and MS-DPS. The DPSs vary in the number of channels:

Supported V93000 systems : Smart Scale (SmarTest 7), Pin Scale

DPS

Channels

MS-DPS

8 (2 channels are mapped to 1 GP-DPS channel)

GP-DPS, HV-DPS

4

The particular probe card and DUT determine which DPSs you can use. You cannot use the High Current DPS.

Note Guarding for DPS lines is not supported on the generic DUT boards.

On the generic DUT boards, a 12 inch WPI probe card can support:

  • 2 channels with 4A each
  • 4 channels with 4A each
  • 2 channels with 8A each
  • 4 channels with 8A each

The HV-DPS is similar to GP-DPS, but output voltages are higher and currents are lower.

The tables below show the wiring for GP-DPS and MS-DPS supporting 4 channels with 8A each.

You can connect 2 force lines and a sense line in a way that uses both force lines from one GP-DPS channel or 2 MS-DPS channels (shown in yellow, 4 groups). The return lines can be grouped accordingly (shown in green).

The following limitations exist:

  • Maximum of 4 channels. The MS-DPS channels are ganged in groups of 2 channels (further ganging is possible).
  • Maximum current is 8A (same as for GP-DPS).
    Note There are no guards around the DPS lines on the generic DUT boards.

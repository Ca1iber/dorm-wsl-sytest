Internal utility power supply (V93000 systems)

The Internal utility power supply of V93000 systems is routed to the UP5V pin of the utility pogo blocks. Depending on the test head type and the type of the DUT interface, one or more Internal utility power supplies are available, each routed to one, two, four or eight utility pogo blocks.

Application notes

Parallel connection of shared supplies
Utility power supply pogo pins of utility blocks that share a common power supply may be connected together. The parallel connection may prevent the usage of the DUT board on another test head type.
No parallel connection of different supplies
CAUTION Utility power supply pogo pins of utility blocks that are connected to different power supplies must not be connected together.
Fuse of internal utility power supplies
The internal utility power supplies are electronically fused. If more than 3.2 A are drawn, the supply heats up triggering the electronic fuse. The supply turns off. After cooling down, the supply comes back up.

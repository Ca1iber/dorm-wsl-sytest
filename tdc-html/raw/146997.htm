The web-based Advantest License Renewal Portal is your single access point for renewing your licenses. Renewing licenses usually involves only a few steps; and if your environment did not change, you are done with just four clicks.

  1. Login to the Advantest site in starting your Web browser and connecting to the Advantest site at www.advantest.com.
  2. The Advantest License Renewal Portal is located at: www.advantest.com/licensing.
  3. Click on the V93000 SoC / Smart Scale link.
  4. Select Renew/Reactivate license (Advantest Renewal Portal) and after logging in, you have access to all tasks belonging to the renewal of licenses.

The Advantest Renewal Portal enables you to:

  • Renew and reactivate licenses
  • Assign entitlements and check their assignment status
  • Download the renewed licenses
  • Download records for the License Renewal Comparison Tool
    Note You can only renew licenses using the License Renewal Portal. For information about activating licenses, see Activating your licenses.

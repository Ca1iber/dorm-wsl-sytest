Topic 12319

Utility Line Commands

Subtopics

  1. Accessing Utility Lines
  2. List of utility line commands

Utility Lines allow you to control external devices such as relays, solenoids or indicators, depending on the application. The lines are implemented as open collector outputs with a maximum current of 60 mA and a maximum voltage of 4.85 V.

In principle, Utility Lines cannot only be used to control the state of an external device, but also to read its current state. On Pin Scale systems all Utility Lines have both (read and write) capabilities.

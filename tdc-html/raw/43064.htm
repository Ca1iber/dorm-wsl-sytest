Coordinates

The dies coordinates are represented by the X_MIN/MAX and Y_MIN/MAX on the left of the screen. The MIN- and MAX- coordinates can be between -79 and +79. By having a mixture of both negative and positive coordinate values, you can assign to the middle die, a value of 0,0. When you enter X_MIN/MAX and Y_MIN/MAX values, the corresponding grid appears on the right-hand side of the screen.

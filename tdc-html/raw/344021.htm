Topic 344021

Verification Phase 2 - Stage 3

About this task

This procedure must be performed by trained personnel experienced with the maintenance and operation of the Accretech UF3000EX series probers

In this task you verify the planarity of the prober head plate with the help of the VPG (Visual Planarity Gauge). You will mount the VPG on the prober during this procedure.

Procedure

  1. On the prober User Interface, press CARD CHANGE, then press CARD CHANGE START.
  2. On the prober User Interface, press REMOVE, to begin the probe-card unloading process. Follow the instructions shown on the prober User Interface to access the APC tray.
  3. The prober SACC/APCC tray will retrieve the "low" stiffener from the bridge beam.
    1. During unloading, when the "low" stiffener physically disconnects from the bottom of the bridge beam, toggle the emulator SD switch to OFF.
    2. When the prober User Interface displays 'Tray-unit moved at the Upper-Position', toggle the emulator DD switch to OFF.
  4. After the APC tray has completely retracted into the loading area with the "low" stiffener, remove the "low" stiffener from the tray.
  5. On the prober User Interface, press CARD CHANGE, then press CARD CHANGE START.
  6. On the prober User Interface, press ATTACH to begin the probe-card loading process. Follow the instructions shown on the prober User Interface to access the APC tray.
  7. Load the VPG onto the APC tray and follow the instructions shown on the prober User Interface to load the "low" stiffener onto the bridge beam.
    1. During loading, when the VPG touches the bottom of the bridge beam, toggle the emulator SD switch to ON.
    2. When the prober User Interface is showing 'Card-holder is setting', toggle the emulator DD switch to ON.
  8. After the APC tray has completely retracted to the loading area, press CONTINUE twice and EXIT on the prober User Interface.
  9. Load the V93000 VPG into the prober and start to measure the height of each of the four outer targets.
  10. Based on the results, verify that the planarity measurements of the VPG are within acceptable specifications based on the application requirements using the VPG.
  11. On the prober User Interface, press CARD CHANGE, then press CARD CHANGE START.
  12. On the prober UI, press REMOVE, to begin the VPG unloading process. Follow the instructions shown on the prober User Interface to access the APC tray.
  13. The prober APC tray will retrieve the VPG from the bridge beam.
    1. During unloading, when the VPG physically disconnects from the bottom of the bridge beam, toggle the emulator SD switch to OFF.
    2. When the prober User Interface displays 'Tray-unit moved at the Upper-Position', toggle the emulator DD switch to OFF.
  14. After the APC tray has moved into the loading area with the VPG, remove the VPG from the tray.
  15. After the APC tray has completely retracted from the loading area, press CONTINUE twice and EXIT on the prober User Interface.

Results

You have completed Stage 3 of Phase 2 of the verification process involving the VPG. This completes the verification process as a whole.

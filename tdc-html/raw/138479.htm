Stiffener wide opening (x16 sites)

The full-size stiffener with wide opening is a 3rd party fabrication package. Use it for final test applications which require additional space. For example,

  • applications with more than 8 sites, where the additional DUTs would interfere with the ribs of the large stiffener.
  • RF applications with bulky or a large number of components.

Below you find the related drawings. They provide the specs and dimensions required for your design tasks.

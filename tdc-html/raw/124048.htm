Test cell setup and verification

Subtopics

  1. Prober head plate adjustment
  2. Test head adjustment
  3. Prober planarity verification

This topic gives an overview of the adjustments and verifications which the users have to perform the Direct-Probe installation.

The following sequence of adjustments and planarity verifications are necessary:

  1. Adjustments
    1. Head plate (prober)
    2. Test head (tester)
  2. Planarity verification
    1. Prober without tester
    2. Test cell (prober and tester)

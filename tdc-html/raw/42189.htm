Configuration Parameters for Site Management

Subtopics

  1. Parameter: handler_site_ids prober_site_ids
  2. Parameter: handler_site_mask and prober_site_mask
  3. Parameter: smartest_site_to_handler_site_map and smartest_site_to_prober_site_map

These parameters provide the driver with the required information about equipment sites (number of sites, site IDs, site conditions), and map SmarTest sites to equipment sites.

PS1600 board ADC

The board ADC provides very accurate high impedance voltage measurements through each digital channel. A board ADC is shared among 16 channels.

The PS1600 board ADC allows very high accuracy measurements with 200 uV relative accuracy by applying the integrated focus calibration1). Here, relative accuracy means that two consecutive measurements are done on the same channel. This is also valid if two consecutive measurements are done on channels out of the group of 16 channels that are sharing one board ADC.

APIs for redundancy analysis

New member functions are added to MTP_MEMORY_TEST to support redundancy analysis.

These member functions are:

  • MTP_MEMORY_TEST.setRAAlgorithm()
  • MTP_MEMORY_TEST.setRABeginAction()
  • MTP_MEMORY_TEST.setRAConfigFile()
  • MTP_MEMORY_TEST.setRACounting()
  • MTP_MEMORY_TEST.setRAEnablingMode()
  • MTP_MEMORY_TEST.setRAEndAction()
  • MTP_MEMORY_TEST.setRAExecutionName()
  • MTP_MEMORY_TEST.setRAGenerateOverlaidBfd()
  • MTP_MEMORY_TEST.setRAUseCountingResults()

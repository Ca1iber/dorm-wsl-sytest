Utility power supplies are used to power active components like relays, operational amplifiers, and buffer on the DUT board or probe card. In contrast to device power supplies, the utility power supplies provide fixed voltages and do not provide a programmable clamp current or measurement functionalities.

Common characteristics of all utility power supplies

Utility power supplies can work in only one quadrant: They can force a positive voltage and drive a current, or they can force a negative voltage and sink a current. The output can be switched on or off.

As a result, the utility power supplies cannot force 0 V at the output and cannot discharge circuitry on the DUT board. The following notes must therefore be observed:
  • The time to bring the voltage down to 0 V depends on the load and the capacitors on the DUT board. High load currents will decrease the time. Large capacitors will increase the time.
  • Switching off a utility power supply for only a short time can lead to a critical voltage drop. See also the description for UP5V on Pin Scale and SmartScale systems in Utility power supplies.
  • The critical drop in the supply voltage, also known as brownout, can bring digital circuits into an undefined state.
  • The current through the internal pull‑up resistor of a deactivated utility lines and the connected circuitry on the DUT board can also push up the output voltage of a switched‑off utility power supply to an undefined value. For more information, refer to Utility line characteristics and Utility line example circuits.

UP5V pin on all utility pogo blocks

Utility power supply - UP5V pins

The output of the utility supply can be switched on or off simultaneously on all utility pogo blocks of the Utility card or (for the internal utility resources) of the card cage using an API of SmarTest.

With compatibility settings for Pin Scale and Smart Scale systems enabled, the UP5V output of the first card cage or of the first Utility Card is switched on by default1). With standard settings for V93000 EXA Scale systems selected, the outputs of the utility power supply are disabled by default.

Internal utility power supply

The UPV5 supply is automatically switched on as soon as the test head is powered up and SmarTest is loaded.
The supply is automatically switched off if an overload condition is detected and is automatically switched on as soon as the faulty condition has been corrected.

The three outputs are automatically switched on as soon as a connection from the force pins P# to the sense inputs PS# is established with docked DUT board.
With an overload condition detected, the supply is automatically switched off. It must be enabled again using the API. The supply is automatically switched off if an overload condition is detected and is automatically switched on as soon as the faulty condition has been corrected.

The switchable utility power supplies can be controlled by test method API UTILITY_POWER_STATUS of SmarTest 7 or by Device Test API IUtilityPower of SmarTest 8.

Taking a picture of a shmoo test in the Shmoo Tool

The snapshot function in the Shmoo Tool takes a picture of both a shmoo plot and its setups.

Procedure

  1. In the menu bar of SmarTest, click Shmoo > SnapShot.

    Or,

    In the toolbar of SmarTest, click the icon.

  2. In the pop-up window, specify a name and directory to save the picture.
  3. Click OK.

Results

The shmoo plot with its setups is saved as a .png file in your specified directory.

Related information

Commands in the menu bar and the shortcuts in the toolbar

Functional changes

  1. Introduced in SmarTest 7.2.0
  2. SmarTest 7.2.1: The snapshot function is enhanced to picture both the shmoo plot and its setups.

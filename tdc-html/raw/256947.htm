Topic 256947

Moving the test head (STH and LTH only)

Use a key combination of the remote control to dock the test head. This is applicable to small test head (STH) and large test head (LTH) systems only.

Procedure

Press UP or DOWN to move the test head in the corresponding direction.

The red OVERRIDE button enables UP and DOWN when the DUT board is locked or the test head is docked.

CAUTION Do not use OVERRIDE on the remote control if the DUT-board stiffener is mounted on the handler or wafer prober!

Results

If moving is allowed by the docking situation, you will have moved the test head upwards or downwards.

Method 2: Using a Sort Method to Define Your Stepping Pattern

Although method 1 can always be used, if you have a lot of die on your wafer, method 2 may be better. It involves,

  • Selecting all dies in one go.
  • Deselecting all dies that you do not want or that are not valid.
  • Applying one of the sorting routines to the selected dies.
  1. Choose DieSelect > Select all die.
  2. Deselect all unwanted or invalid dies (see the note in method 1).
  3. Choose Sort > Menu and choose one of the sort patterns in the Menu dialog box.

    You can decide to sort your dies beginning in any corner - top left (TL), bottom left (BL), top right (TR) or bottom right (BR).

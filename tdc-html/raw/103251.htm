Topic 103251

Mount the probe card cing on the probe card stiffener

About this task

The prepared probe card ring including the RF cables needs to be mounted onto the probe card stiffener.

Before you begin

Ensure you have prepared the probe card ring and have T10 countersink screws available.

Procedure

  1. Arrange the stiffener so the arrows on its edge point away from you.
  2. Place the probe card ring on the stiffener, aligning the arrows on the ring with those on the stiffener.
  3. Secure the ring to the stiffener with six T10 countersink screws.
  4. Connect the SMP rectangle female connectors (N2398A-003) to the probe card as illustrated in the next picture.

What to do next

Mount the probe card into the prober manufacturer kit.

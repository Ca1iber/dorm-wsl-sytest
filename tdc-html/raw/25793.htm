Topic 25793

PMU Settling Times

Before a PMU measurement the PMU must be in a settled state. To ensure a settled PMU state an internal settling time is introduced by the test system. You cannot modify this settling time. With future hardware improvements this internal settling time will always automatically be adapted to the lowest possible value.

You can specify an additional settling time for dc tests using the PMU for their measurement. This settling time may be needed due to a specific DUT board design.

That is, total settling time is the sum of internal settling time plus additional settling time. It is not possible to enter a negative additional settling time to reduce the overall PMU settling time to a value below the internal settling time.

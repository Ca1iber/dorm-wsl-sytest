PMUX01 force and sense pins

The force and sense pins of the PMUX01 Power MUX are the multiplexer inputs and outputs.

F01IN to F12IN

These pins are the inputs of the twelve force multiplexers that are normally connected to the force pins of the multiplexed resources. The force inputs and outputs are designed to carry a high constant current.

F01OUT1 to F12OUT4

These are the outputs of the twelve force multiplexers that are normally connected to the DUT. Each of the force inputs F##IN is assigned to four force outputs F##OUT1 to F##OUT4. The force inputs and outputs are designed to carry a high constant current.

S01IN to S12IN

These pins are the inputs of the twelve sense multiplexers that are normally connected to the sense pins of the multiplexed resources. The sense inputs and outputs are designed to carry only a low current that is typically expected on sense lines.

S01OUT1 to S12OUT4

These are the outputs of the twelve sense multiplexers that are normally connected to the DUT. Each of the sense inputs S##IN is assigned to four sense outputs S##OUT1 to S##OUT4. The sense inputs and outputs are designed to carry only a low current that is typically expected on sense lines.

Absolute maximum ratings

Refer to PMUX01 specifications for the absolute maximum ratings of the force and sense pins.

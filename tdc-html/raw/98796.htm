"Run" and "Run and Branch" test suites

The "Run" and "Run and Branch" test suites are represented by testflow nodes. When a test suite is executed as part of a testflow, a pass/fail result is generated for that particular test suite. Depending on whether you have chosen a "Run test" or "Run and Branch" node, the pass/fail result determines the further flow of the test.

Functional changes

  1. Introduced before SmarTest 6.3.2

Topic 348686

Activating licenses

Subtopics

  1. Activating licenses
  2. Activating licenses advanced
  3. Adding entitlement IDs and organization to a user account

Licenses can be activated from entitlements or available licenses activated to a selected license server. If the license server has access to the internet, it synchronises automatically. For license servers that are not connected to the internet, the license files must be downloaded and installed manually.

Depending on whether you are activating licenses from entitlements or activating available licenses to selected license servers, with or without internet access, the following guides are available:

  • Activating licenses
  • Activating licenses advanced
  • Downloading licenses

Functional changes

  1. Introduced in SmarTest 8.2.5

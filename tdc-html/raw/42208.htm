Topic 42208

Parameter: temp_equipment_unit

The temperature unit entered here is the one, in which the equipment expects to receive the temperature parameters.

Your configuration parameters, entered in the unit specified with temp_conf_unit will by automatically converted to this equipment expected unit. Subsequently they will be transferred to the equipment.

This is the unit used for the transfer of parameters to the equipment.

For example, the equipment may need to receive the values in Celsius but the temperatures you insert may be in Fahrenheit.

The valid entries are "celsius", "fahrenheit" and "kelvin".

If not specified, "celsius" is assumed.

Driver default: "celsius"

Changeable at run-time: yes

Applicable for: handlers and probers, plug-in dependent

Example: temp_equipment_unit: "celsius"

Zooming shmoo plots

There are three manners for you to zoom in and zoom out either a whole shmoo plot or a selected area of the plot:

  • In the toolbar of SmarTest: Click the to zoom in. Click the to zoom out.
  • In the menu bar of SmarTest: Click Shmoo > ZoomIn or ZoomOut.
  • Right-click a shmoo plot and select:
    • Zoom In
    • Zoom Out
    • Full Size: Displays the whole shmoo plot from (x-start, y-start) to (x-end, y-end).
Note When you zoom in and zoom out, the scroll bars around the shmoo plot can help you to view.

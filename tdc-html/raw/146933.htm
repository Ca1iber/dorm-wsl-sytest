Utility pogo blocks on V93000 systems provide the access to utility lines, utility power supplies and some other resources of the V93000 infrastructure.

The availability and configuration of the utility pogo blocks depends on the system type, test head type and the type of the DUT interface.

V93000 EXA Scale systems

V93000 EXA Scale systems utilize the DUT Scale interface for all test head types. Two configurations are available:
  • Utility pogo block - Basic configuration (V93000 EXA Scale systems):

    32 statically controlled utility lines and a +5 V utility power supply per card cage are provided in the basic configuration. These utility resources are routed to one utility pogo block per card cage.

  • Utility pogo block - Utility Card (V93000 EXA Scale systems):

    The optional Utility Card replaces the utility resources of the basic configuration with 256 dynamically controlled utility lines and several utility power supplies per card. The utility resources of a Utility Card are routed to eight utility pogo blocks.

V93000 Smart Scale, Pin Scale and HSM systems

The V93000 systems support utility pogo pin blocks with either 2 pogo pin rows or 4 pogo pin rows (starting with SmarTest 7.2). The support of utility blocks and their organization in groups depends on the test head and the DUT interface:

DUT interface

A-, Compact, and Small test head

 Large test head
DUT Scale IF Utility block 1 ... 8 Utility block 1 ... 8
Small DUT IF Utility block 1 ... 4 n/a
Sequencer control of utility lines is supported on all test heads that are equipped with the DUT Scale interface and on the A-Test head with the small DUT interface.

HPPMU and internal utility power supply mapping
The pogo pin assignment topics for Smart Scale, Pin Scale and HSM systems provide a table that shows how the HPPMU of the Clock board and the Internal utility power supply are mapped to the utility pogo blocks:
Large and DUT scale interface:
  • LTH mapping
  • STH mapping
  • CTH mapping
  • ATH mapping
 Small DUT interface:
  • STH mapping
  • CTH mapping
  • ATH mapping

Note: The HPPMU functionality is not supported in SmarTest 8.

Since internal utility supplies from different Clock boards must not be connected together, the information of the linked tables must be considered when designing the DUT board and connecting the circuitry of the DUT board to the UP5V pins.

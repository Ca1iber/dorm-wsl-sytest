Topic 151255

Hiding the wafer prober chuck

About this task

Before you can proceed with the installation of the Direct Probe™ Bridge Beam hardware on the Accretech UF3000 EX Wafer Prober, you must move the wafer prober chuck out of the installation area. This is to ensure that the chuck does not get damaged during the installation of the Bridge Beam hardware.

CAUTION The procedure described below is only valid for the Accretech UF3000 EX wafer prober in combination with SW version S6.42.03. Refer to the Accretech operating manual for the procedure applicable to your particular wafer prober.

The wafer prober is equipped with a touch screen. You execute all commands on this screen.

Before you begin

  1. Unload any wafers and the probe-card from the prober and store them away from the prober.
  2. Undock the V93000 test head and move it away from the prober.

Procedure

  1. Set the wafer prober to the MAINTENANCE AND ADJUSTMENT mode.

    On the main page touch the MAINT. AND ADJUSTMENT button twice.

  2. The wafer prober enters the MAINTENANCE AND ADJUSTMENT mode and displays the Maintenance and adjustable parameter menu.
    1. Touch the NEXT PAGE button at the bottom of the screen to get to the second page (highlighted in green).
    2. On the second page touch the X,Y,Z,THETA,F AXIS ADJUSTMENT button.
  3. The wafer prober displays the X,Y,Z,THETA,F AXIS ADJUSTMENT page.

    On this page select Manual Operation:

    Touch the MANUAL OPERATION button.

  4. The wafer prober displays the MANUAL OPERATION page.

    On this page touch the AUTO CARD EXCHANGE MOVING button twice.

  5. The chuck moves to the back of the wafer prober, disappearing under the cover, see the figure below.

    When the action is completed, touch the EXIT button in the bottom right corner of the screen, see the screen shot above.

    The wafer prober displays the main page.

  6. Power off the prober.

Results

You have moved the wafer prober chuck out of the installation area.

What to do next

Install the Bridge Beam hardware on the wafer prober. Move on to Bridge beam installation on Accretech UF3000 EX prober - Overview.

Topic 25791

Ambiguity of PPMU Results

As mentioned before, there are two measurement modes available with the PPMU. The two modes are the pass/fail measurement and the value measurements. The results are derived from two independent measurement units (PPMU and Board ADC), which both have a unique and independent error term. However both measurement units are accurate to specified limits. No conclusion can be drawn on the relative matching of the two measurement units with respect to each other, other than that the relative error will not exceed the specified absolute error. Consequently there will be a certain range of ambiguity when the reading takes place very close to the pass/fail limit. This ambiguity range is defined by the relative accuracy of the two measurement units and will not exceed the maximum specified error of the PPMU. Thus this will have no negative effect as long as measurements are guard-banded properly.

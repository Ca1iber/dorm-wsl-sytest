Variable Per-Pin Clock Rate

An extension to the pin mapping syntax lets you run individual pins at clock rates that are divisions of the general clock rate. For example, the statement VDD(CLK.001)=VDD@(cycleratio=1:4) runs the VDD(CLK.001) pin at a quarter of the general clock rate.

Benefit

The ability to run different pins at different rates lets you use DC Scale boards in high-speed testing.

More Info

For further details, see Pin mapping file.

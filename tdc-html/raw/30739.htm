Topic 30739

DISABLE_TESTER_SW_BUTTON

Subtopics

  1. Associated Syntax Entities

Each of the SCREEN_DIALOG and LINE_DIALOG screens have a set of defined buttons associated with them. Which buttons appear also depends at what level (device, wafer, lot, etc.) the screen appears.

In certain circumstances you might not want the developm button to appear; you should, therefore, include the command, DISABLE_TESTER_SW_BUTTON, in that particular LINE_DIALOG or SCREEN_DIALOG.

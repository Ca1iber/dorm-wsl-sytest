Topic 136591

Zooming the display of charts

You can zoom in and out of result charts of the Integrated Result Tool in various ways using the mouse, toolbar, shortcut menu commands, or axis settings.

About this task

Zoom in on a chart to better view details. Zoom out to get an overview of larger areas or the entire chart.

Before you begin

Create a result chart in the Integrated Result Tool.

Procedures

Zooming in

To view an area of the chart at a larger size, do any of the following:

  • Click the Zoom In button on the toolbar.
  • Right-click anywhere in the chart and choose Zoom In from the context menu.
  • Click with the mouse in the chart and, holding down the mouse button, draw a rectangle over the area that you want to zoom in on.

Note that not all methods are supported in all chart types.

Zooming out

To view a larger area of the chart at a smaller size, do any of the following:

  • Click the Zoom Out button on the toolbar.
  • Right-click anywhere in the chart and choose Zoom Out from the shortcut menu.

Note that not all methods are supported in all chart types.

Numerically specifying the display area

To view an area bounded by specific data and value coordinates:

  1. Click the Properties button on the chart's toolbar or choose Properties from the context menu.

    The chart's properties dialog open.

  2. Click the Scales tab.
  3. Set the scales of the axes to the limits of the area you want to view.

    For details, see the description of the properties dialog for the chart type:

    • Trend chart properties
    • Histogram properties
    • Probability chart properties
    • Box chart properties
    • Scatter chart properties
    • FFC Pareto chart properties

      In box charts, you can specify only one axis. In shmoo plots, parametric wafer maps, and bin wafer maps, you cannot configure the axes.

      For example, to view in a trend chart the test from 10 to 20 and value range from 1.3 to 2.2 V, enter these values in the Minimum and Maximum fields in the Data Axis Scale and Value Axis Scale section, respectively, of the Scales tab of the chart's properties dialog.

  4. Click OK.

Showing the entire chart

The default view of a chart is to show the entire chart. To return to this default after you have changed the zoom level, do any of the following:

  • Right-click anywhere in the chart and choose Restore Defaults from the shortcut menu.
  • Double-click anywhere in the chart.

Note that both methods also reset the axes to their default ranges and may reset other properties, depending on the chart type. Not all methods are supported in all chart types.

Results

The zoom level of the chart is set according to your action.

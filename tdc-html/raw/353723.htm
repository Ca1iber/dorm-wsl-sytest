Changing settings in multiple Measurement windows

You can open the Measurement window for associated signals and modify setting for all signals for which a Measurement window is open.

Before you begin

Open two or more Measurement windows.

Procedure

  1. Change values as required in the open Measurement windows.

    Changed values and modes are highlighted with a turquoise background.

  2. In the Measurement window, click MEAS.

    The measurement is executed for the associated signal and the values are displayed in black on white.

Results

You have opened the Measurement window for multiple signals and modified setting for any signals for which a Measurement window is open.

Functional changes

  1. Introduced in SmarTest 7.4.3

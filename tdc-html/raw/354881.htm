Step 3: Backer height measurement

This topic describes how to measure and to adjust the planarity of the Direct Probe backer assembly per specification drawing.

About this task

Note This topic uses an example probe backer layout that may differ from your probe backer. Therefore, the description may also differ in detail to your assembly.

The Backer height upper limit depends on various factors like probe array size, probe needle type, probe planarity, probe over drive and wafer pad/bump.

The limit should be determined by the probe card company based on the needle design and application.

In most cases, the method implements the use of readily available mechanical measurement tools.

All subsequent planarity adjustments to the probe backer assembly, such as adjusting the probe head planarity, should be made in reference to datum A (4x contact tabs) of the probe card assembly and of the probe backer.

Measurement and adjustment of the probe backer assembly is recommended when any potential mechanical variation is possible, such as replacing the PCB, shipping, etc.

In this topic the following tools are used.

EXA Scale table:

  • Minimum working area: 457.2 mm x 304.8 mm
  • Inspection grade +/- 1 mil (0.00254 mm) unilateral tolerance

Rectangular gauge block:

  • Minimum quantity: 5
  • Grade ASME 0
  • NIST traceable

Thickness gauge:

  • Minimum thickness: 1 mil (0.0254 mm)

Before you begin

Make sure that you already adjusted the planarity of the probe card assembly according to the description in Step 2: Contact Tab planarity measurement of complete Probe Card.

Procedure

  1. On the EXA Scale table, adjust the gauge blocks.

    If available use a gauge block fixture, see note above.

    Note Advantest recommends using a fixture plate to hold the gauge blocks in place, see drawings for N2360-21605 in Direct-Probe probe card layout information and Direct-Probe stiffener.
  2. Place the probe card/stiffener assembly onto the gauge blocks so that the contact tabs on the stiffener frame rests on the gauge blocks.
  3. Measure the gap, if any between the top of the probe backer and four contact tab using the thickness gauge. the height required is to be ≥ 0.00 mm.
  4. Note the gap measurement result using the thickness gauge.
  5. If the height is < 0.00 mm, add the shims between the probe backer standoffs / insulated boss and the probe backer frame or adjustable pins to reach the required height.

    Thickness of the round shim will depend on the gap measurement.

Results

You have measured and adjusted the Backer height.

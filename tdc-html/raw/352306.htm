PS1600 pogo cable assembly E8030PSDL: 8 SD pogo blocks

The E8030PSDL long pogo cable assembly connects the 128 channels of a PS1600 card via 8 standard-density (SD) pogo blocks to the DUT board.

The long pogo assembly for the Pin Scale 1600 can be clearly distinguished by its red cable sleeves. It is 10 cm longer than the standard pogo assembly and has an overall length of about 50 cm. The additional length gives you more flexibility in guiding the cable inside or outside the test head.
Note The long PS1600 pogo cable is used only in the compact test head (CTH) for the special cable routing from CC1/CC2 slot 8 to group 5 pogo block 401-408.

Basic data and pogo block

Overall length: 500 mm
Pogo block style: 4-row standard-density (SD) pogo block
Pogo blocks: 8
Channels per pogo block: 16
Total channels: 128

Like the standard pogo cable, the long pogo cable has one 128-pin connector at one end and eight 16-pin pogo blocks at the other end. It retains compatibility with the pogo pin layout of other digital cards.

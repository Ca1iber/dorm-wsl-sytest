Topic 127690

Executing demodulation

About this task

This task shows how to execute demodulation in the Signal Analyzer.

Before you begin

Make sure that the Signal Analyzer is open and the demodulation tool is open.

Procedure

In the demodulation window, click Execute.

After each successful execution the results in the results panel will be updated and all result related graphs and table editors will be automatically refreshed. The execution time in milliseconds is displayed left of the Execute button.

What to do next

Verify the results.

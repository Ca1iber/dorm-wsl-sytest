Topic 351380

Deprecated TMU_PINS methods

Subtopics

  1. TMU_PINS.uploadPeriodResults()
  2. TMU_PINS.uploadRawResults()
  3. TMU_PINS.uploadRawResultsFlat()

The TMU_PINS upload methods are deprecated, but for compatibility reasons they are still usable.

The recommended way for uploading results is to use the TMU_TASK upload methods, which also provide features for hidden upload:

  • TMU_TASK.uploadPeriodResults()
  • TMU_TASK.uploadRawResults()
  • TMU_TASK.uploadRawResultsFlat()

Functional changes

  1. Introduced in SmarTest 7.4.3

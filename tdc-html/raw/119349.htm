Golden device: Replacing failed state characters

Replaces failed state characters (SC) according to the current mapping. The replacement works per pattern and on state characters only.

The Scope tab displays the selected cycle range, and the failed pins. You can add and remove pins, or change their sequence.

On the Mapping tab you can change the proposed mapping items, that is the failing state character, its replacement character, and the selector. Click any of the values (except in the Selector column) and type the new value.

To choose a different mapping, either select another mapping (Mapping Name) or define a mapping (New).

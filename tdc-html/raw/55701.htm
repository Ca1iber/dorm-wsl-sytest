Parameter: debug_level

For debugging purposes, the driver may generate more or less detailed messages. The level of details is defined by this parameter. If not specified differently in the attributes definition, the parameter settings -1 (no messages) ... 4 (highest detail) are valid.

Driver default: 1

Changeable at run-time: yes

Applicable for: handlers and probers

Example: debug_level: 2

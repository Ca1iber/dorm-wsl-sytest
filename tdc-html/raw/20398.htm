Topic 20398

Maintaining signal quality in mixed-signal testing

Improper design of the DUT board can cause reflections in the signal paths as well as signal deterioration due to attenuation and phase changes.

You can maintain signal quality using the following techniques:

  • Avoid runs of wire on the DUT board. Use strip lines and coaxial cables instead.
  • Avoid using vias on the DUT board. If the use of a via is unavoidable, make sure to specify a 50 Ω equivalent via to the custom DUT board manufacturer.
  • Avoid stubs on the DUT board. If you must have a stub, for example, to connect a socket to a signal track, place the junction of the stub between the pogo pad and the DUT pin and insert a surface-mounted series resistor between the junction and the socket.
  • Avoid unnecessary sharp corners in strip lines. Use a radius, or a chamfered corner instead.
  • Avoid size changes and notches in strip lines.
  • When soldering coaxial cables to strip lines, minimize the length of unshielded core and provide a good ground connection for the coaxial cable shield.

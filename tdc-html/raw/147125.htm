PMUX01 Power MUX pogo pin assignment

The Power MUX pogo block connects the Power MUX card to the DUT board.

Supported V93000 systems : EXA Scale (SmarTest 8), Smart Scale (SmarTest 7+8)

The 34-row pogo block is divided into two logical 17-row blocks A and B.

Pogo pin description
Pin Description DUT board connection
F##IN MUX 01 ... 12 force input
F##OUT1 MUX 01 ... 12 force output 1
F##OUT2 MUX 01 ... 12 force output 2
F##OUT3 MUX 01 ... 12 force output 3
F##OUT4 MUX 01 ... 12 force output 4
S##IN MUX 01 ... 12 sense input
S##OUT1 MUX 01 ... 12 sense output 1
S##OUT2 MUX 01 ... 12 sense output 2
S##OUT3 MUX 01 ... 12 sense output 3
S##OUT4 MUX 01 ... 12 sense output 4
GND Supply GND of the PMUX card and

reference GND for I2C pins

 Connect to UGND (Utility ground)
VCC Supply VCC of the card Connect to XPP5V and XPP5Vs
SDA Serial data (I2C) Connect to SDA line of the I2C bus
SCL Serial clock (I2C) Connect to SCL line of the I2C bus
OE Output enable Connect to UGND to enable the card
ID# Card ID Connect to UGND or leave open according to the PMUX01 addressing scheme.
JTAG Reserved Do not connect
P2V5 Reserved Do not connect

Notes on the pogo pin descriptions:

  • Pin: ## = MUX 01 ... 12, # = ID 0 ... 3
  • Digital pins or utility pins are to be used to generate the required I2C signals for the I2C bus.

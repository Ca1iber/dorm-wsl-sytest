SHARED_IDD_TEST.execute()

The execute() member function of SHARED_IDD_TEST lets you execute a shared IDD test based on your previous setup.

Syntax

void execute() const

Example

SHARED_IDD_TEST mTest;
mTest.execute();

Related information

SHARED_IDD_TEST

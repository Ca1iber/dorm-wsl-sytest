Topic 105066

Card types

Subtopics

  1. DC-DC granularity

A V93000 test head can be equipped with different types of test head cards.

A test head primarily contains the following cards:

  • Digital cards
  • Device Power Supplies and VI Cards
    • DC Scale cards (residing in channel card slots)
  • DC/DC converter
    • For the power supply to the card cage slots.
    • In Smart Scale systems, each DC/DC converter card supplies power to two adjacent slots in the card cage.
    • For Smart Scale systems, see also DC/DC granularity.

Configuration guidelines:

Configuration of the test system involves assigning digital channel cards and power supplies to specific channels of the test head.

The Card Cage to Pogo Block Standard Assignments and the Card compatibility topic provide an overview of the rules for configuring products and hardware to ensure compatibility and scalability of the test system.

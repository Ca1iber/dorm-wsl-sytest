Cooling requirements

For use with one CX (9 slot) test head

Cooling unit E2760FAE Small liquid-liquid cooling unit
Cooling capability 18 kW @ 30 °C regulated secondary water temperature
Cooling medium De-ionized water
Primary chilled water temperature range 5 °C to 20 °C (41 °F to 68 °F)

For use with one SX (18 slot) test head

Cooling unit E2760FAT Liquid-liquid cooling unit
Cooling capability 36 kW @ 30°C regulated secondary water temperature
Cooling medium De-ionized water
Primary chilled water temperature range 5 °C to 20 °C (41 °F to 68 °F)

For use with one LX (27 slot) test head

Cooling unit E2760FAX and E2760FAXV Liquid-liquid cooling unit
Cooling capability 54 kW @ 30°C regulated secondary water temperature
Cooling medium De-ionized water
Primary chilled water temperature range 5 °C to 20 °C (41 °F to 68 °F)

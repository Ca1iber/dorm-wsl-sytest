What is Measured?

With the pin margin tool you can determine just how much headroom you have for each of the following hardware resources:

  • test system period
  • edge delays
  • driver levels
  • receive thresholds
  • clamp voltages
  • load currents
  • termination/commutation voltages
  • DPS voltages (as a varying parameter of the other measurements)
  • DPS voltages (when measured independently of other parameters)
  • DPS currents (when measured independently of other parameters)

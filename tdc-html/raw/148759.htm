AVI64 pogo cable assembly (E8025AVP)

The E8025AVP pogo cable assembly connects the 64 channels of the DC Scale AVI64 card to the DUT board.

Pogo cable assembly data

  • Overall length: 500 mm
  • Pogo blocks: 4
  • Pogo block style: 4-row pogo block
  • Channels per pogo block: 16

J10 connector orientation

Topic 30698

BrdTemp_check

Subtopics

  1. Associated Syntax Entities

This expression is used in conjunction with MONITOR_CALL, and provides for a check of the tester's I/O boards' temperature. The limits of the board temperature must also be defined beforehand, by the expression BrdTemp_limit.

What happens if the temperature of the boards exceeds the limits, is determined by the out of control rules - see ooc_warning_rule and ooc_stop_rule.

The lines below show that limits of ± 2 ℃ (3.6 ℉) have been set on the boards at the lot level, while later in the wafer level these limits are checked and logged.

{lot:

MONITOR_CALL(BrdTemp_limit,-2.0,2.0);

:

:

:

{WAFERwafer:

*temp_state=MONITOR_CALL(BrdTemp_check);

:

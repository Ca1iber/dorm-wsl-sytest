My Area

The My Area page lists the tickets you have submitted to the TE-Cloud team.



Search box
Entering the search term or ticket number and pressing Enter displays tickets containing the entered term or number.
Ticket status
Clicking Open, Closed, or On Hold displays tickets that have the respective status.
Ticket list
The tickets you have submitted that match the selected status and the specified search terms.
Add ticket
Clicking this button opens the Submit a ticket page.

Functional changes

  1. Introduced in SmarTest 7.6.2

PS5000 pogo cable assembly EK601-61603: 8 HD pogo blocks

The EK601-61603 pogo cable assembly connects the 256 channels of a PS5000 card via 8 high-density (HD) pogo blocks to the DUT board.

Basic data and pogo block

Overall length: 585 mm
Pogo block style: 4-row high-density (HD) pogo block
Pogo blocks: 8
Channels per pogo block: 32
Total channels: 256

Orientation mark of pogo blocks

Note If a pogo block from a PS5000 card has been installed back to front, the pins will not fit through the safety cover matrix on the skew board EK853AD. This will prevent PMTT from finding the reversed pogo block, resulting in a "not found" message. In such a case check the pogo block for correct orientation before taking further troubleshooting steps.

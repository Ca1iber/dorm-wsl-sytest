Topic 14923

Showing/Hiding Information

Using the two right-most Buttons you can display or hide the following two sections of the Pin Margin window to view the result data in the Results section:

  • the Primary Sets section and
  • the Selections section.

Alternatively, you can use the Display pulldown menu, to display or hide the two sections.

Functional changes

  1. Introduced before SmarTest 6.3.2

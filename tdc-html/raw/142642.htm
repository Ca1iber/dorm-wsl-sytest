Driver-sharing APIs

Four new APIs let you include driver-sharing test in your custom test methods. They complement the existing ready-made test methods.

  • SHARED_CONTINUITY_TEST lets you check the continuity and find short circuits for the DUTs with driver sharing.
  • SHARED_IDD_TEST lets you check the IDD and IDDQ for the DUTs with driver sharing.
  • SHARED_LEAKAGE_PARALLEL_TEST lets you do the parallel leakage test for the DUTs with driver sharing.
  • SHARED_VBUMP_TEST lets you change the power supply voltages during a functional test for the DUTs with driver sharing.

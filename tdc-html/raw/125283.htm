shift()

Shifts a state character sequence similar to shift operators but with more flexibility.

The sequence source parameter is subject to being shifted while a sequence (sequence fill) is being inserted from left or right.

The integer_constant determines the amount of bits to shift and its sign tells the direction.

  • negative integer_constant: shifts left
  • positive integer_constant: shifts right

In case of shifting left, the left most n bits are discarded and the left most n bits of the repeated fill sequence are inserted on the right.

In case of shifting right, the right most n bits are discarded and the right most n bits of the repeated fill sequence are inserted on the left.

If a state characters is used which cannot be converted to integer, an error is issued.

Syntax

sequence := shift(sequence source, integer_constant, sequence fill)
Parameter

I/O

Parameter

Description

I

sequence source

Source state character sequence on which the shift will be performed.

I

integer_constant

Amount of bits to be shifted.

A negative number is used for shifting left.

A positive number is used for shifting right.

I

sequence fill

State character sequence to be filled into the sequence source.

If this sequence is shorter than integer_constant it will be repeated till the length of integer_constant is reached.

Example

shift("001", 2, "001") results in "010".

shift("LLXHR", -3, "X") results in "HRXXX" (shifting left).

shift("LLXHR", -3, "X0") results in "HRX0X" (shifting left).

shift("LLXHR", 3, "X0") results in "0X0LL".

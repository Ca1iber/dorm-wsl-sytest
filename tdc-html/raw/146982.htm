Topic 146982

Logging in

Subtopics

  1. Applying for login data to the Renewal Portal

About this task

You must be logged in to the Advantest License Renewal Portal to complete the license renewal procedure. You access the Advantest License Renewal Portal through the Advantest site. Both request a login.

Before you begin

You must know your Advantest customer login, which is based on your email address.

Note Password reset is possible on the general Advantest login page.
Note All entries in the login screens are case sensitive.

The login procedure is a 2-step process: Login to the Advantest site, then login to the Advantest License Renewal Portal.

Procedures

1. Advantest site login:

  1. Start your Web browser and connect to the Advantest site at www.advantest.com.
  2. Type your customer credentials and click Login. For registered customers the login name is the email address provided during registration.
  3. Enter www.advantest.com/licensing.

    The Licensing page opens.

  4. On the Licensing page click the V93000 SoC / Smart Scale link.

    The V93000 Licensing page opens.

2. Advantest License Renewal Portal login:

  1. On the V93000 Licensing page click Renew/Re-activate license (Advantest Renewal Portal).
  2. Type the user name and password for your Advantest customer login.
  3. Click Login.
    Note If you cannot login, contact your Advantest representative providing your Advantest customer login user name (do not provide your password) and the error message you received about the Renewal Portal.

Results

This opens the Advantest License Renewal Portal page, giving access to:

  • Dashboard
  • Assign entitlements
  • Renew licenses
  • Download licenses
  • Reports
  • Manage aliases
  • License comparison
  • User profile (click your user name in the upper-right corner)

What to do next

To log out after you have completed your tasks, click Log Out in the upper-right corner.

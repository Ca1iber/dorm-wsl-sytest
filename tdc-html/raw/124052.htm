Topic 124052

Test head adjustment

Subtopics

  1. DUT interface adjustment during docking

This topic describes how to adjust the test head for the planarity verification.

Before you begin

Adjust the prober head as described in Prober head plate adjustment.

Note A probe card is not required to be inside the prober.

Procedure

  1. Verify that the DUT interface floating capability and the theta capability are enabled.
  2. Hard-dock the test head.
  3. Verify that the test-head is visually planar to the prober head-plate in the side axis:
    1. Check the angle of the gap between the test head cover and the prober head plate.
    2. If required, adjust the parallel adjustment handle.
  4. Verify that the test head is visually planar to the prober head-plate in the front axis:
    1. Check the angle of the gap between the test head cover and the prober head plate.
    2. If required, adjust the test head angle (for details see Test head movement in the LTH/STH Manipulator Installation Guide).
  5. Lock the manipulator arms by securing:
    • two horizontal movement locks
    • the swing-out lock

      as shown in the following figure.

What to do next

Verify the prober planarity as described in Prober planarity verification.

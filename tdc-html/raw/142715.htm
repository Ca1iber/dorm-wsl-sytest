PDebug

PDebug is a new tool that lets you test DUT boards for various electrical problems.

Benefit

Verifying the electrical integrity of the DUT board increases confidence in test results and helps to prevent false fails caused by the board and not the DUT.

Note PDebug becomes obsolete in MTP 7.5.0, MTP 7.4.5, and future versions.

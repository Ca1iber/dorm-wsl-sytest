Topic 91505

PSHX card pogo assembly and channels

The Pin Scale HX pogo block assembly consists of two 16-channel pogo blocks.

Supported V93000 systems : Pin Scale, HSM

The first pogo block, the "high-speed pogo", provides most of the high-speed signals, some low speed signals, and must reside in an S2 type position on the test head. The second pogo block, the "low-speed pogo", is for low-speed signals only.

The figures show the high-speed (cyan/blue/green cables) and low speed (blue cables) pogo blocks of the Pin Scale HX Card pogo assembly and one of its pogo pin blocks.

The table below specifies the pin assignment.

Note Some of the "low-speed" signals on the low-speed pogo block are running at 3.2 Gbit/s. The "low-speed" Mux/DeMux signals for X4 operation mode are located on the "high-speed" pogo block.
Note Unlike other pogo blocks of the V93000 system, the Pin Scale HX Card pogo blocks utilize all 17 pogo pins.

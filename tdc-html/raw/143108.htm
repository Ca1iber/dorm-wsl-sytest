Topic 143108

PVI8 pogo pin assignment

Subtopics

  1. PVI8 pogo cable assembly (E8013PVP)

The PVI8 card is a high-current and high-voltage power supply card that is optimized for a wide range of analog power tests. It belongs to the DC Scale family and can reside in any channel card slot. The E8013PVP pogo cable assembly connects the eight floating channels of the DC Scale PVI8 card to the DUT board.

Supported V93000 systems : Smart Scale (SmarTest 7)

Pogo pin description

Pin Description DUT board connection Alias / also known as
HF# High force line of the channel HighForce
HS# High sense line of the channel HighSense
LF# Low force line of the channel LowForce
LS# Low sense line of the channel LowSense
GUARD# Guard line of the HF# and HS# line
TMU_H# TMU high input
TMU_L# TMU low input
CMON# Current monitor output
GANG_IN_H Gang control high input line from master channel on other card Refer to PVI8 ganged wiring and ganging control pogo pins for information how to connect these pins.
GANG_IN_L Gang control low input line from master channel on other card
GANG_OUT_H Gang control high output line to slave channel on other card
GANG_OUT_L Gang control low output line to slave channel on other card
SAFETY_5V Control line of the safety circuit Connect to an adequate +5.0 V supply via a safety interlock on the DUT board or the DUT interface.
SAFETY_GND Return line of the safety circuit Connect to the return pin of the +5.0 V supply at SAFETY_P and to the DUT board ground.
DNC Do not connect pin These pins are internally used. Do not connect these pins on the DUT board.
Separation Separation row between the channels To ensure that adjacent high voltage pins or traces have no effect the Separation rows, adequate measures like sufficient insulation space have to be respected.

Notes on the pogo pin descriptions:

  • Pin: # = Channel 1 … 8
  • The Hardware View of PVI8 shows an "UTI" signal at the CMON# output. This internal feature is currently reserved and not accessible by the user.
  • The DNC pins are internally connected and reserved. To ensure that adjacent high voltage pins or traces have no effect on the DNC pins, adequate measures like sufficient insulation space have to be respected.
CAUTION The force lines "HF#" and "LF#" are each wired to three pogo pins. These three pogo pins must be connected in parallel on the DUT board to ensure that the specifications for the card and the maximum ratings for the pogo pins are met.

Model file

Refer to model file topics Pogocabling entries for different card types and DPS Section for information on how to define the PVI8 pogo blocks in the model file.

Note Although the eight channels of the PVI8 card are physically routed to two pogo blocks, SmarTest assigns all eight channels to the first block "A".

Specific design rules

Specific design rules must be followed when designing a new DUT board and assigning PVI8 channels:

  • Pin assignment for Fast Sync / Fast Match: For fast local synchronization, the synchronized DUT pins must be assigned to the same group of channels respectively to the same test processor.
  • HV (High-Voltage) protection: Since the PVI8 card may be connected to low-voltage cards via defective DUTs or unintentionally closed connections, we recommend that you implement a HV (High-Voltage) protection circuitry on your DUT boards to protect the low-voltage cards.

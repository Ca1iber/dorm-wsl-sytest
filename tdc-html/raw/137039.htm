Topic 137039

Removing synchronization markers

Remove a sync marker that is attached to a vector of a certain pin to delete it or move it to another position.

About this task

This task describes how to remove a sync marker from a non-protocol-port pattern (control pattern).

Before you begin

Find the location of the sync marker you want to remove within your 'control pattern'. Do this by checking the locations marked with little arrows above the pin name and left to the vector number.

Verify the name of a sync marker by move the mouse over the cell with the triangle.

Procedure

  1. Right-click the vector value of the pin that is marked with a small triangle having the verified sync marker attached.
  2. Click Remove Sync Marker from the menu that pops up.

Results

You removed (deleted) your sync marker from the 'control pattern'.

What to do next

Update your test methods and remove (or rename) the corresponding sync markers there, too.

You also may re-insert the sync marker you just deleted at a different position by following the task: Adding synchronization markers.

Replying to a ticket

To reply to a ticket, enter the reply in the ticket detail page and click Send.

About this task

If you reply to a ticket whose status is Closed, it will be reopened.

Before you begin

You must be logged in to the help center.

Procedure

  1. In the help center, click the My Area tab.
  2. Click the ticket to which you want to reply.

    The details of the ticket are displayed.



  3. Click Reply.

    The ticket edit field opens.

  4. Enter your reply.
  5. Click the Send button.

Results

Your reply has been sent to the TE-Cloud support team.

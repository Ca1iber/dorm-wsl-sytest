Concept of the Pin Scale HX card

Subtopics

  1. Pin Scale HX card requirements for pattern mode
  2. Pin Scale HX card requirements for loopback mode
  3. Equalization
  4. Bidirectional signalling

The Pin Scale HX card is a high-speed digital card. It extends application data rates up to 12.8 Gbit/s. To achieve this, it multiplexes 2 or 4 Pin Scale 3600 data channels into one Pin Scale HX card lane. Two channels are required for data rates up to 6.4 Gbit/s, four channels for rates up to 12.8 Gbit/s.

Each Pin Scale HX card has two differential drive and two differential receive channels (lanes).

The card can be used in several operational modes (relay modes). The main operational modes are:

  • PARAMETRIC_LOOP

    Here, the Pin Scale HX card loops the DUT's output back to its input through the Pin Scale HX card.

  • PATTERN

    Here, the functionality is similar to the Pin Scale cards but at speeds up to 12.8 Gbit/s. In the PATTERN mode, the card works together with a Pin Scale 3600 card to re-time 2 or 4 Pin Scale channels to the desired bit rate of 6.4 or 12.8 Gbit/s respectively.

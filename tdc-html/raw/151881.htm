Topic 151881

Protecting the chuck on the wafer prober

About this task

Before you can proceed with the installation of the Direct Probe™ Bridge Beam hardware on the TEL Precio wafer prober, you must cover the prober chuck with soft protective material, for example a thick sheet of ESD-safe foam. This is to ensure that the chuck does not get damaged during the installation of the Bridge Beam hardware.

Procedure

  1. Turn off the prober at the main power switch.
  2. Remove the cover to the right of the SACC (Semi-Automated Card Changer) door and underneath the mains ON/OFF switch, see the figure above.
  3. Disconnect the compressed air supply by pulling off the supply hose, see the figure above for the location.
  4. Cover the chuck with a sheet of ESD-safe foam.

    The figure below shows two pieces of foam sheeting placed over the chuck, one after the other.

What to do next

Proceed to the installation of the Direct ProbeTM Bridge Beam.

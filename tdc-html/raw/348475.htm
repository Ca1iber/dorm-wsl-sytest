DC-Scale cards contain independently programmable channels. The DC Scale cards reside in I/O channel card slots.

Supported V93000 system generations and variants

This section holds product-specific electrical design rules for all V93000 system generations and variants. For the supported set of test head cards on your system, refer to Electrical design: Product-specific rules.

XPS256 pogo pins

The output pins of an XPS256 card are the channel specific P#, PS#, and GS# pins and several common GND pins.

Pogo pin P#

P# is the low impedance high force output pin of an XPS256 channel. The pin is optimized for low DC resistance and carry currents up to 1 A.

Pogo pin PS#

PS# is the high impedance voltage sense input of an XPS256 channel. The PS# pin must be connected to the high force pin P# directly at the DUT to compensate the voltage drop on the high force line.

Pogo pin GS#

GS# is the high impedance ground reference input of an XPS256 channel. The GS# pin must be connected to a GND pin of the DUT to compensate the voltage drop on the common ground and return path.

Pogo pins GND

The GND pins are the common ground and return path for all channels of an XPS256 card. The GND pins must be connected to a common ground plane of the DUT board. All GND pins must be connected in parallel to ensure that the specifications for the card and the maximum ratings for the pogo pins are met.

Specific design rules

Specific design rules must be followed when designing a new DUT board for the XPS256. For more information refer to
  • XPS256 recommendations for use and
  • XPS256 capacitor requirements.

Voltage sense lines

The voltage at the DUT is measured differentially between the high sense pin (PS#) and the ground sense pin (GS#). Both pins are available per XPS256 channel. The high sense pin (PS#) must be connected to the related high force pogo pin(P#) directly at the DUT pins if the Kelvin connection scheme is used. The ground sense pin (GS#) must be connected to the common ground pin (GND) or an adequate DUT pin directly at the DUT both for all connection schemes.

The voltage difference between the force pins and sense pins is typically caused by the voltage drop, produced by current flow over parasitic resistances on the DUT board. A maximum voltage difference must be maintained between the high force pin (P#) and the high sense pin (PS#) and between the ground sense pin (GS#) and the common ground. Violating this rule will result in a wrong force voltage and wrong voltage measurements.

For the maximum voltage difference between P# and PS# and between the common ground and GS# refer to section Force to sense clamp below. You must also consider the total voltage drop on the force and return line as shown for the Continuous voltage and current capabilities per channel of the XPS256 voltage and current capability specifications.

For parameters and maximum values of the sense pins, refer to table Disconnect state of the XPS256 other performance characteristics.

Force to sense clamp

Each XPS256 channel features a switchable clamp circuitry, which consists of two anti-parallel diodes, between the high force (P#) pogo pin and the high sense (PS#) pogo pin and between the ground sense (GS#) pogo pin and the common ground (GND). The diodes limit the voltage difference between the two pins for abnormal operating conditions. An abnormal operating condition is for example an open sense connection on the DUT board. The diodes limit the voltage increase on the force line to about 1 V in this case1).

The following data must be respected for the diodes:

Maximum operating voltage range or voltage difference between P# and PS# or GS# and GND: ±500 mV
Typical maximum force to sense current within the operating voltage range: ±1 uA

Pull down circuit at P#, PS# and GS#

Each of the pins P#, PS# and GS# features a circuit that pulls the pin down to ground. The circuit keeps the voltage at disconnected pins at a defined level of about 0 V referenced to the GND pin.
  • Pull‑down resistance: Typical 200 kΩ

Overload protection

The output pogo pins of the XPS256 channels are designed to withstand following overload conditions:

  • Voltages within the operating range are tolerated at any of the pins P#, PS#, and GS# independent of the programmed function.
    Operating voltage range
    XPS256 (EK751HC):

    -0.5 V … +1.5 V in 1.5 V range

    -2.5 V … +7.0 V in 7.0 V range
  • In disconnected state, the following voltages can be tolerated at DUT terminals P#, PS#, and GS# while maintaining leakage specification.
    Compliance voltage range (disconnected)
    XPS256 (EK751HC): -4.5 V … +7.5 V
    For more information, refer to table Compliance range (at disconnect state) in XPS256 other specifications.
  • For voltages outside the compliance range, the following limits are to be observed regardless of the programmed function and state:
    Overvoltage protection
    Maximum voltage range -80 V … +80 V
    Maximum energy of voltage spike 20 mJ
    Maximum current 1.0 A
CAUTION Additional protection circuitry is required on the DUT board if XPS256 channels are operated together with other cards that can violate the rules defined above. Other cards that may exceed the defined limits for the XPS256 channels are for example the DC Scale AVI64 cards.

For more information regarding protection circuitry on the DUT board, refer to HV (High-Voltage) protection.

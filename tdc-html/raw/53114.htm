Topic 53114

Ground connections for generic WPI DUT boards

The ground connections of the generic WPI DUT boards provide a low resistance. Analog and digital grounds are separated.

Some pins on analog boards belong to digital ground. These pins are routed through the pogo segments like all other analog pins, but the corresponding ground pin is connected to digital ground. There is no connection to the ground block of the analog pogo segment.

Ground for pin 1-4 of standard analog channel boards and all ground pins of block 209/S2, 225/S2 and 229/S1 are connected to digital ground (all ground pins of 229/S2 are connected to analog ground).

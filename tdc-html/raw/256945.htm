Docking the test head

Use a key combination of the remote control to dock the test head.

About this task

Procedure

  1. On the remote control, press HARD DOCK.

    The test head is pulled into position, all three hard docking pins enter the docking cylinders. In this position the UP and DOWN buttons are disabled by a sensor beneath one of the docking cylinders.

  2. Press DUT DOCK to connect the test head pogo pins to the DUT board.

    In this position a sensor blocks the HARD UNDOCK and the UP and DOWN buttons on the remote control.

  3. When docking is completed, press HARD NEUTRAL to prevent unnecessary strain on the interface.

Results

You have docked the test head.

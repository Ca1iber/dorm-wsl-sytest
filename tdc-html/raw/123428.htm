SharedVBump test method

The SharedVBump test method lets you change the supply voltage levels for sharing load boards.

Because traditional methods no longer work with some load boards, VBump is performed by routing DPS32 channels to VDD and VDDQ.

Levels are set up only for the VDD pin.

SharedVBump relies on the proper setup of the vbump keyword in the utility control section of the Sharing configuration file to correctly route the DPS32 channels to VDD and VDDQ (see SharedVBump syntax for details). It then handles all channel setup and the functional test autonomously.

Note If you require greater control over test details than this test method offers, you can also develop your own shared VBump test method with the SHARED_VBUMP_TEST API.

Test methodologies

There are four execution cases for Shared VBump, depending on whether VDD and/or VDDQ are to be measured and on whether the maximum DPS32 current exceeds 1.5 A.

Except for the VDD bump, execution always requires properly connecting VDD and/or VDDQ to the power supplies, executing, and resetting them afterwards. High-current vbump and VDDQ bump (without VDD) are two-step processes that switch lines between the steps.

VDD bump

This is a single-step process that is executed with default utility line settings.

VDDQ bump

This is a two-step process that involves routing VDD to VDDQ:

  1. The utility lines are switched to connect DPS32 to the VDDQs of the first four sites (DPS32_1 to DUT1, DPS32_2 to DUT2, etc.).

    MS-DPS is connected to the VDDs of all eight sites and the VDDQs of DUT5-DUT8.

  2. The functional test is executed.
  3. The utility lines are switched to connect DPS32 to the VDDQs of the second four sites (DPS32_1 to DUT5, DPS32_2 to DUT6, etc.).

    MS-DPS is connected to the VDDs of all eight sites and the VDDQs of DUT1-DUT4.

  4. The functional test is executed.
  5. The utility lines are restored to their defaults.

VDD and VDDQ bump

This is single-step process that uses a common voltage for VDD and VDDQ. One DPS32 drives two VDDs and two VDDQs; no MS-DPS is used:

  1. The utility lines are switched to connect DPS32 to the VDDs and VDDQs of all 8 sites (DPS32_1 to DUT1 and DUT5, DPS32_2 to DUT2 and DUT6, DPS32_3 to DUT3 and DUT7, DPS32_4 to DUT4 and DUT8).
  2. The functional test is executed.
  3. The utility lines are restored to their defaults.

VDD and VDDQ bump, high current

When voltages of 1.5 A or more are involved, bumping VDD and VDDQ is a two-step process. It uses common voltages for VDD and VDDQ, with one DPS32 driving one VDD and one VDDQ:

  1. The utility lines are switched to connect DPS32 to the VDDs and VDDQs of the first four sites (DPS32_1 to DUT1, DPS32_2 to DUT2, etc.).

    MS-DPS is connected to the VDDs and VDDQs of DUT5-DUT8.

  2. The functional test is executed.
  3. The utility lines are switched to connect DPS32 to the VDDs and VDDQs of the second 4 sites (DPS32_1 to DUT5, DPS32_2 to DUT6, etc.).

    MS-DPS is connected to the VDDs and VDDQs of DUT1-DUT4.

  4. The functional test is executed.
  5. The utility lines are restored to their defaults.
